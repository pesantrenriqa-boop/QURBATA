# F-003 — QURBATA DNA

**Status:** Official Working Draft  
**Version:** v0.1  

## 1. Purpose

QURBATA DNA defines the fundamental identity, educational philosophy, and design principles that distinguish QURBATA as a System Integrasi Pendidikan Al-Qur’an.

This document becomes the reference for curriculum, books, assessments, teacher development, and derivative learning products.

## 2. Core Identity

QURBATA is not merely a reading method or textbook series. QURBATA is a structured Qur’anic education system that integrates:

- Qur’anic literacy
- Reading proficiency
- Memorization development
- Arabic language competency
- Understanding of meaning
- Character formation

## 3. Educational Principle

QURBATA follows the principle:

> From recognizing the letters of the Qur’an to living by the guidance of the Qur’an.

Learning progresses through measurable stages:

Huruf → Kata → Frasa Qur’ani → Ayat → Makna → Hafalan → Pemahaman → Pengamalan

## 4. Learning DNA

### 4.1 Progressive

Materials are arranged from simple to complex with controlled difficulty escalation.

### 4.2 Cumulative

New competencies continuously strengthen previously mastered competencies.

### 4.3 Integrated

Reading, Arabic vocabulary, meaning, memorization, and character education are developed as one system.

### 4.4 Authentic

Examples are derived from authentic Qur’anic language, hadith, and meaningful Arabic usage.

### 4.5 Measurable

Each competency has clear indicators, assessment standards, and quality control mechanisms.

## 5. Long-Term Vision

QURBATA aims to establish a globally applicable standard for Qur’anic education that preserves traditional transmission while utilizing modern educational system design.

## 6. Governance Note

All curriculum, books, assessments, and derivative products must maintain consistency with this DNA document and the QURBATA Constitution.
