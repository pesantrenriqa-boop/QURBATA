# F-001 — QURBATA Constitution

**Document class:** Foundation / Normative  
**Status:** Draft v0.1  
**Authority:** Highest internal normative document of QURBATA

**Language policy:** English normative text with Indonesian explanatory notes  

---

## 0. Preamble

QURBATA is established as a long-term educational, scholarly, publishing, and technological initiative for the structured teaching and learning of the Qur'an. It is designed to serve learners, teachers, institutions, researchers, authors, and system developers through a coherent framework that is faithful to the Qur'an, pedagogically sound, measurable, adaptable, and accountable.

This Constitution defines the permanent principles, hierarchy, scope, governance, and amendment rules of QURBATA. All derivative documents, curricula, books, assessments, training systems, applications, datasets, publications, and institutional implementations shall conform to this Constitution.

**Catatan Indonesia:**  
Dokumen ini merupakan konstitusi internal tertinggi QURBATA. Seluruh produk dan keputusan turunannya wajib konsisten dengan dokumen ini.

---

## 1. Identity

### 1.1 Name

The official name of the system is **QURBATA**.

### 1.2 Nature

QURBATA is:

1. a Qur'anic learning system;
2. a curriculum architecture;
3. a graded book and learning-resource framework;
4. an assessment and certification framework;
5. a teacher-development system;
6. a research and continuous-improvement program;
7. a foundation for digital implementation and integration.

### 1.3 Institutional origin

QURBATA is developed within the educational ecosystem of Rumah Ilmu Al-Qur'an (RIQA) and Dar ad-Dirosat al-Quraniyah (DDQ), while being designed for broader scholarly and educational use.

### 1.4 Primary object

The primary object of QURBATA is the learner's progressively verified ability to recognize, pronounce, read, understand at the designated level, practise, preserve, and transmit Qur'anic learning with correctness, adab, confidence, and responsibility.

---

## 2. Purpose

QURBATA exists to establish a coherent and measurable pathway for Qur'anic learning from the earliest stage to advanced competency.

Its purposes are to:

1. reduce fragmentation in Qur'anic learning;
2. provide a clear sequence of competencies;
3. protect accuracy of pronunciation and reading;
4. unite instruction, practice, assessment, and remediation;
5. support learners with different ages, starting points, and learning speeds;
6. equip teachers with consistent standards;
7. generate reliable evidence for curriculum improvement;
8. enable responsible translation into books, media, software, and institutional programs.

---

## 3. Vision

To become a trusted, research-informed, and internationally adaptable Qur'anic learning system that nurtures learners who read correctly, learn progressively, practise with adab, and grow into responsible bearers of the Qur'an.

**Catatan Indonesia:**  
Visi ini tidak membatasi QURBATA hanya sebagai buku baca Al-Qur'an. QURBATA diarahkan menjadi sistem pembelajaran yang utuh, dapat diteliti, diukur, dikembangkan, dan diterapkan lintas lembaga.

---

## 4. Mission

QURBATA shall:

1. formulate a competency-based Qur'anic learning progression;
2. develop graded learning materials based on controlled introduction, cumulative review, direct practice, and mastery;
3. establish valid, transparent, and practical assessment standards;
4. prepare teachers through defined competency and certification pathways;
5. document all normative terms and decisions;
6. conduct research on learner performance, teaching effectiveness, and material quality;
7. maintain an auditable system of versioning, review, and revision;
8. support print, classroom, digital, and assisted-learning implementation without weakening pedagogical integrity.

---

## 5. Foundational Commitments

### 5.1 Qur'anic fidelity

QURBATA shall preserve respect for the Qur'an and shall not introduce content, presentation, or practices that diminish its sanctity, distort its reading, or trivialize its transmission.

### 5.2 Correctness before speed

Accuracy of sound, articulation, distinction, and reading shall take precedence over premature speed.

### 5.3 Gradual progression

Learning shall proceed from simpler to more complex competencies through clearly controlled stages.

### 5.4 Mastery before advancement

A learner shall advance based on demonstrated competency, not merely attendance, age, duration, or completion of pages.

### 5.5 Cumulative review

Previously learned material shall remain active through planned review and shall not disappear when new material is introduced.

### 5.6 Direct guided learning

Talqin, talaqqi, listening, imitation, correction, repetition, and supervised practice are essential components of Qur'anic learning.

### 5.7 Learner dignity

Correction shall protect the learner's dignity. Humiliation, fear-based instruction, ridicule, and harmful comparison are incompatible with QURBATA.

### 5.8 Adab integration

Adab is not an optional supplement. It shall be embedded in learning conduct, teacher conduct, material design, assessment, and institutional implementation.

### 5.9 Evidence-informed improvement

Revision shall be based on documented observation, assessment evidence, research, expert review, and implementation experience.

### 5.10 Accessibility with integrity

QURBATA shall seek broad accessibility while maintaining standards of correctness and instructional quality.

---

## 6. Educational Principles

Every QURBATA curriculum and learning resource shall satisfy the following principles:

1. **Defined outcome:** every unit shall state what the learner must be able to do.
2. **Controlled load:** new material shall be limited and proportionate to the learner level.
3. **Visible progression:** competency growth shall be traceable across units and levels.
4. **Active response:** learners shall read, respond, distinguish, recall, or perform; passive exposure alone is insufficient.
5. **Frequent feedback:** errors shall be identified and corrected as close as possible to the learning event.
6. **Distributed review:** review shall be repeated over time, not concentrated only at the end.
7. **Contrastive practice:** confusable sounds, forms, lengths, and rules shall be deliberately contrasted.
8. **Transfer:** learners shall be prepared to apply learned competencies to unfamiliar Qur'anic examples.
9. **Remediation:** every essential assessment shall be connected to a clear corrective pathway.
10. **Age-sensitive presentation:** visual density, language, task length, and teacher guidance shall be adapted without lowering the competency standard.

---

## 7. Learning Architecture

### 7.1 Core learning cycle

A standard QURBATA learning cycle may include:

1. preparation and adab;
2. review of prior competency;
3. teacher modelling;
4. guided imitation;
5. controlled practice;
6. individual reading or performance;
7. immediate correction;
8. independent or repeated practice;
9. formative evaluation;
10. recording and follow-up.

### 7.2 Material introduction

New letters, sounds, forms, harakat, combinations, lengths, tajwid rules, vocabulary, memorisation, and applied reading shall be introduced only according to the approved sequence.

### 7.3 Page and session discipline

Books and session plans shall not overload a single encounter. One page should normally serve one meeting and may serve no more than two meetings unless the relevant curriculum standard explicitly provides otherwise.

### 7.4 Review ratio

Each lesson shall contain an intentional balance between new material and review. The exact ratio may vary by level, but review is mandatory in every instructional stage.

### 7.5 Advancement

Advancement shall depend on a defined mastery threshold and documented teacher judgment supported by assessment evidence.

---

## 8. Assessment Principles

### 8.1 Purpose

Assessment exists to protect learning quality, identify needs, guide remediation, confirm mastery, and support responsible certification.

### 8.2 Assessment classes

QURBATA may use:

1. diagnostic assessment;
2. formative assessment;
3. mastery checks;
4. summative assessment;
5. oral performance assessment;
6. written or recognition assessment where appropriate;
7. teacher competency assessment;
8. implementation-quality assessment.

### 8.3 Validity

An assessment shall measure the competency it claims to measure.

### 8.4 Transparency

Criteria, error categories, mastery thresholds, and remediation procedures shall be documented and understandable to authorized users.

### 8.5 No automatic certification

Certificates shall not be issued solely because a learner attended, paid, completed pages, or reached a time limit.

### 8.6 Recorded evidence

Essential progression and certification decisions shall be supported by a retained record appropriate to the context.

---

## 9. Teacher Principles

A QURBATA teacher shall:

1. demonstrate verified competency appropriate to the level taught;
2. preserve correct modelling;
3. correct errors accurately and respectfully;
4. follow the approved sequence and assessment standards;
5. record progress honestly;
6. distinguish between learner difficulty and learner negligence;
7. pursue continuing development;
8. avoid unauthorized alteration of essential standards;
9. protect learner privacy and dignity;
10. embody adab toward the Qur'an, learners, colleagues, and institution.

Teacher authority is instructional and ethical, not arbitrary. No teacher may declare mastery contrary to documented standards.

---

## 10. Research Principles

QURBATA research shall:

1. address relevant educational questions;
2. use transparent methods;
3. protect participants;
4. obtain appropriate consent and institutional authorization;
5. distinguish evidence from opinion;
6. retain sufficient documentation for audit or replication where possible;
7. report limitations honestly;
8. avoid manipulating results to protect reputation or commercial interest;
9. translate findings into reviewed improvement proposals;
10. preserve the religious and educational dignity of participants and content.

Detailed research rules shall be governed by **F-005 Research Protocol**.

## 12. Document Hierarchy

The normative hierarchy is:

1. **F-001 Constitution** — supreme internal normative authority;
2. **Foundation documents F-002 to F-005**;
3. **Standards and governance documents**;
4. **Curriculum architecture and competency frameworks**;
5. **Assessment frameworks and teacher standards**;
6. **Book specifications and authoring rules**;
7. **Operational guides, templates, and implementation manuals**;
8. **Local adaptations and derivative resources**.

A lower-level document shall not contradict a higher-level document. Where conflict occurs, the higher-level document prevails until an authorized harmonization is completed.

## 13. Foundation Document System

The Foundation set consists of:

- **F-001 — Constitution**
- **F-002 — Lexicon**
- **F-003 — DNA**
- **F-004 — Graduate Profile**
- **F-005 — Research Protocol**

Each Foundation document has a distinct role:

- F-001 defines authority and permanent principles.
- F-002 controls terminology.
- F-003 defines the essential identity and design logic of QURBATA.
- F-004 defines the intended learner outcomes.
- F-005 governs research and evidence generation.

F-001 shall be harmonized after substantive approval of F-002 to F-005.

## 14. Governance

### 14.1 Stewardship

QURBATA shall be governed by an authorized stewardship structure designated by its founding institution.

### 14.2 Roles

Governance may include:

- Founder or institutional authority;
- Constitution and standards custodian;
- curriculum board;
- Qur'anic accuracy reviewers;
- research and evaluation board;
- teacher-standard authority;
- publication and technology teams;
- implementation partners.

### 14.3 Separation of proposal and approval

Major normative changes shall distinguish between:

1. proposal;
2. technical review;
3. religious or Qur'anic accuracy review where required;
4. field validation where required;
5. approval;
6. publication.

### 14.4 Conflict of interest

Reviewers and decision-makers shall disclose interests that may compromise impartial judgment.

## 16. Amendment Policy

### 16.1 Ordinary amendment

An ordinary amendment requires:

1. written rationale;
2. identification of affected documents and implementations;
3. technical review;
4. approval by the authorized governance body;
5. a recorded version change;
6. communication to affected users.

### 16.2 Major amendment

A major amendment is one that changes identity, vision, hierarchy, mastery principles, Qur'anic accuracy standards, governance authority, or the Foundation structure. It requires enhanced review and explicit approval by the highest authorized body.

### 16.3 Emergency correction

A clear error that threatens Qur'anic accuracy, learner safety, legal compliance, or data protection may be corrected immediately, provided that the correction and rationale are documented and subsequently reviewed.

### 16.4 Non-retroactivity

Changes shall not silently rewrite historical records. Superseded versions shall be preserved in the archive.

---

## 17. Adaptation and Translation

QURBATA may be translated and adapted for different languages, countries, age groups, institutions, and delivery modes.

Adaptation shall not alter:

1. essential competency standards;
2. Qur'anic correctness requirements;
3. mastery-based progression;
4. document hierarchy;
5. assessment integrity;
6. learner dignity and ethical protections.

Any material adaptation shall identify the source version and the nature of the adaptation.

---

## 18. Technology and Artificial Intelligence

Technology may support QURBATA through content management, learner records, randomised practice, assessment support, audio analysis, teacher tools, publishing, analytics, and accessibility.

Technology shall not:

1. replace qualified human verification where human talaqqi and correction are required;
2. issue unreviewed religious or competency claims;
3. conceal uncertainty or model limitations;
4. compromise learner privacy;
5. alter normative content without authorization;
6. prioritize engagement metrics over educational integrity.

All automated systems shall remain accountable to approved QURBATA standards.

---

## 19. Intellectual Integrity and Attribution

QURBATA shall respect scholarly attribution, copyright, licensing, authorship, contributor recognition, and source integrity.

Reference to prior educational methods may inform QURBATA's pedagogical analysis, but QURBATA shall not unlawfully reproduce protected content, layouts, branding, or proprietary materials.

Original contributions shall be documented through repository history, publication records, or other authorized mechanisms.

---

## 20. Protection of Learners and Data

QURBATA implementation shall protect:

- learner dignity;
- personal data;
- assessment confidentiality where necessary;
- children's safety;
- fair access;
- accurate records;
- the right to correction of administrative error.

Collection of data shall be proportionate to legitimate educational, operational, or research needs.

---

## 21. Quality Assurance

Quality assurance shall include:

1. content review;
2. Qur'anic accuracy review;
3. curriculum coherence review;
4. age and usability review;
5. assessment review;
6. pilot implementation;
7. learner-performance analysis;
8. teacher feedback;
9. error and revision tracking;
10. periodic system audit.

No material shall be declared mature solely because it has been designed or published. Maturity requires evidence from implementation and review.

---

## 22. Non-Negotiable Provisions

The following provisions are non-negotiable unless this Constitution is formally amended:

1. reverence and fidelity toward the Qur'an;
2. correctness before speed;
3. gradual and cumulative progression;
4. mastery before advancement;
5. direct guided correction as an essential learning element;
6. integration of adab;
7. honest assessment and certification;
8. protection of learner dignity;
9. documented improvement based on evidence;
10. preservation of normative document hierarchy.

---

## 23. Closing Declaration

QURBATA shall be developed as an amanah: carefully, transparently, cumulatively, and with awareness that errors in Qur'anic education may affect generations of learners and teachers.

Every contributor, teacher, reviewer, researcher, publisher, developer, and institution participating in QURBATA is bound to protect its educational integrity, Qur'anic fidelity, and public trust.

This Constitution takes internal normative effect upon formal approval and remains controlling until superseded by an authorized version.

## Annex A — Initial Compliance Questions

Every proposed QURBATA document, book, feature, assessment, or implementation shall be checked against the following questions:

1. Is it consistent with F-001?
2. Are its terms consistent with F-002?
3. Does it preserve the identity defined in F-003?
4. Does it contribute to the outcomes defined in F-004?
5. Is any research or evidence process consistent with F-005?
6. Is the learner competency stated clearly?
7. Is progression based on mastery?
8. Is review built into the design?
9. Are Qur'anic accuracy and adab protected?
10. Is the change documented and traceable?
