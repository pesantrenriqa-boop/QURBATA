# QURBATA Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

QURBATA is an educational project rooted in respect for the Qur’an, scholarly responsibility, adab, honesty, and service. Everyone participating in its development is expected to communicate and work with dignity.

Expected conduct includes:

- treating contributors, teachers, learners, reviewers, and institutions respectfully;
- correcting errors without humiliation;
- distinguishing evidence, interpretation, proposal, and approved policy;
- acknowledging sources and intellectual contributions;
- protecting learner data and confidential institutional information;
- prioritizing accuracy and educational benefit over speed or personal recognition.

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment, intimidation, insults, or discriminatory treatment;
- knowingly introducing false citations, fabricated evidence, or misleading claims;
- misusing confidential materials or personal data;
- presenting unapproved drafts as official QURBATA policy;
- deleting or concealing material to obscure the history of a substantive decision.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository activity, reviews, discussions, research, testing, training, publication, and other work performed under the QURBATA name.

## Handling Problems

Concerns should be documented clearly and addressed through the project’s institutional leadership. Responses should be proportionate, confidential where appropriate, and focused on protecting people, scholarly integrity, and the project’s mission.

## Educational Adab

Disagreement is permitted and often necessary. It must be expressed with evidence, clarity, patience, and respect. Criticism should target the content or process, never the dignity of the person.
