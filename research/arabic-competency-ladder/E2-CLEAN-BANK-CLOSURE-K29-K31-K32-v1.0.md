## K29 — Imperative recognition
**Decision: PROMOTE TO E2.**

Independent Qur'anic occurrences provide sufficient morphological diversity for clean recognition of fi‘l amr. Canonical examples include:
- QS 6:90 — `اقْتَدِهْ`
- QS 9:65 — `قُلْ`
- QS 11:37 — `وَاصْنَعِ`
- QS 25:57 — `قُلْ`

Boundary rule: K29 recognizes positive imperative morphology. It must remain distinct from K30 prohibition, where `لا` governs a jussive imperfect verb.

## K31 — Interrogative hamzah recognition
**Decision: PROMOTE TO E2.**

Clean positive occurrences:
- QS 27:59 — interrogative alif prefixed to the divine proper noun: `آللَّهُ` / analysis: prefixed interrogative alif + proper noun.
- QS 27:60 — `أَإِلَٰهٌ` / analysis: prefixed interrogative alif + indefinite noun.

The two occurrences demonstrate the target operation across different nominal hosts. K31 is recognition of the interrogative alif, not generic recognition of every written initial hamzah.

Boundary / negative control:
- lexical hamzah belonging to a word is not K31;
- other special hamzah constructions must be occurrence-validated rather than automatically classified as ordinary interrogation;
- punctuation or translation alone is insufficient evidence.

## K32 — `قد` recognition
**Decision: PROMOTE TO E2.**

The Qur'anic corpus provides numerous independent occurrences tagged as the certainty particle `قد`, including:
- QS 2:118 — `قَدْ بَيَّنَّا`
- QS 2:134 — `قَدْ خَلَتْ`
- QS 2:144 — `قَدْ نَرَىٰ`
- QS 2:231 — `فَقَدْ ظَلَمَ`
- QS 2:237 — `وَقَدْ فَرَضْتُمْ`

This bank covers bare `قد` as well as conjunction-prefixed forms (`فقد`, `وقد`) and both perfect/imperfect verbal environments.

Boundary rule: K32 at this stage is particle recognition. Fine-grained semantic interpretation (certainty, completion, contextual force) is not required unless assigned to a later competence.
