# Clean Bank + Prerequisite Stress Test K51 — `حذف النون` v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K50 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

In a validated jussive environment, learner recognizes a familiar mudhari‘ pattern whose normally present inflectional nūn is absent and identifies that absence as the local jazm effect.

## Central prerequisite question

Does K51 secretly require a full prior chapter on `الأفعال الخمسة`?

### Answer

**No, provided the base-form contrast is controlled and already familiar.**

The learner does not need to enumerate or conjugate the complete five-verb paradigm. K51 may use a paired-form recognition strategy:

`familiar non-jussive form with ن` ↔ `validated jussive form without ن`.

The new operation is then local and singular: identify deletion of the inflectional nūn.

## Minimum prerequisite layer

Before a K51 occurrence is admitted, the learner must already be able to recognize the relevant surface ending as belonging to a familiar mudhari‘ form. This prerequisite may be supplied through earlier morphology exposure or controlled lexical pairing; it is **not** expanded into full paradigm production inside K51.

## Clean-bank classes

### PASS-A — direct paired contrast
- familiar form with nūn available/known;
- jussive environment clear;
- corresponding form lacks that nūn;
- no other morphological operation competes.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean occurrence
Additional syntax is ≤ K50 and does not obscure the deletion target.

### REVIEW-BASE
The jussive form is analyzable, but the learner could not reasonably know what nūn is missing without first learning a broader paradigm.

### REVIEW-ORTH
Orthographic/phonological details make the deletion pedagogically non-transparent.

### PREMATURE
- multiple simultaneous morphological changes;
- ambiguous base form;
- advanced person/number derivation required;
- complete `الأفعال الخمسة` production needed to solve the item;
- disputed analysis.

## Anti-fragmentation test

Do not create separate K numbers for each person/number realization. The learner operation remains:

`known nūn-bearing inflectional pattern → jazm → deletion of ن`.

Person/number variants belong to internal staging and later mastery breadth.

## Contrast with K50

K50 reconstructs a **lexical weak-final segment**.

K51 reconstructs an **inflectional nūn** associated with a known mudhari‘ pattern.

The two deletions therefore differ in grammatical representation and are independently assessable.

## Full `الأفعال الخمسة` rule boundary

K51 does not require the learner to state:
- the traditional five-form list;
- full raf‘/nasb/jazm paradigm;
- all attached subject pronoun terminology;
- complete conjugation tables.

Those may be organized later as a rule-generalization or morphology consolidation layer if pedagogically useful.

## Stress-test verdict

**PASS WITH CONTROLLED-BASE REQUIREMENT.**

K51 can remain a single competence without a new prerequisite K if examples are admitted only when the nūn-bearing base is already familiar or explicitly available for comparison.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K51 — Mengenali efek jazm berupa `حذف النون` pada pola fi‘il mudhari‘ yang bentuk nūn-bearing dasarnya sudah familiar.**
