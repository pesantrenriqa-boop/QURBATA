## K05 — detached pronoun recognition

Confirmed `هُوَ` occurs independently in QS 2:255 and QS 112:1. These are separate Qur'anic contexts, but lexical-form diversity remains limited to one detached pronoun form in this batch.

**Decision:** retain **E1**. Multiple occurrences of the same pronoun are not enough for the desired diversified E2 recognition bank. Next expansion should normalize `هي / أنت / أنتم` or other frozen basic forms.

## K08 — simple mubtada' + overt nominal khabar

Clean candidates:
- `اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ` — QS 112:2
- `مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ` — QS 48:29

For K08 the second span is analyzed only at the top predication level: `مُحَمَّدٌ` mubtada' and `رَسُولُ اللَّهِ` nominal khabar. The internal idhafah is earlier/later cumulative structure and should be controlled pedagogically; because it is not as minimal as QS 112:2, this does not yet justify a fully clean diversified E2 bank by itself.

**Decision:** retain **E1**, mark **E2 candidate** pending a second minimal non-idhafah nominal predicate.
