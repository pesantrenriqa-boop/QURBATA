# Clean-Bank Stress Test K47 — Simple Shart v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K46 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Objective

Menguji apakah K47 dapat tetap atomik sebagai **boundary + linkage** pada struktur syarth sederhana tanpa diam-diam memasukkan dua kompetensi tambahan: teori jazm dan marker khusus jawab syarth.

## Core operation

Learner identifies:

`أداة الشرط` → `جملة الشرط` → `جواب الشرط`

and states the relation as **condition → result**.

## Clean-bank policy

### PASS-A — pure boundary/linkage
- explicit validated conditional marker;
- short condition clause;
- short response clause;
- both clauses internally analyzable with K1–K46;
- no explanation of mood change is needed to establish the relation;
- no special jawab marker is required for the learner merely to locate the response.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
Additional material is allowed only when every required operation is already frozen.

### REVIEW-JAZM
The relation is clear, but teaching the occurrence properly would require explaining verbal jussive morphology as a new operation.

### REVIEW-JAWAB-MARKER
The response is identified primarily through a marker whose function has not yet been mastered independently.

### REVIEW-AMBIG
Conditional marker/function is occurrence-sensitive or can be confused with another particle class.

### PREMATURE
- omitted jawab;
- nested conditionals;
- oath/condition competition;
- rhetorical/counterfactual semantics requiring a distinct operation;
- complex ellipsis;
- clause boundary not locally recoverable.

## Stress test: is jazm a hard prerequisite?

**Finding:** no, not for K47 core.

The learner can recognize the condition→result architecture before being able to explain why a verb is majzum. Morphological effect is therefore a later competence, provided K47 examples are selected so the relation itself is independently visible.

## Stress test: are jawab markers a hard prerequisite?

**Finding:** no, not universally.

When the response is a simple immediately following clause and its role is locally clear, no new jawab-marker competence is required. Occurrences whose response depends on `فـ` or another specialized marker for identification should remain REVIEW until that marker is separately handled.

## Stress test: one K or split?

Possible split:
- K47a boundary of condition clause;
- K47b boundary of response clause + linkage.

**Decision:** do not split.

Reason: in a clean simple conditional, identifying the condition clause without identifying its corresponding response does not establish the target relation. The pedagogical operation is naturally relational and is assessed as one unit.

## Anti-leak exclusions

K47 does not include:
- full أدوات الشرط inventory;
- jussive morphology rules;
- deletion of jawab;
- full `فاء الجواب` theory;
- `إذا`/`لو` semantic taxonomy;
- nested or discourse-level conditionals.

## Freeze judgement

K47 is **structurally stable** as a single competence if final gate preserves the clean-bank restrictions above.

The relation remains one new operation: detect a validated condition marker, delimit the two simple clauses, and link them as condition→result.

## Recommendation

Proceed to DRAFT-FREEZE K47 with strict note that REVIEW-JAZM and REVIEW-JAWAB-MARKER examples are not part of core teaching evidence until later competencies unlock those phenomena.
