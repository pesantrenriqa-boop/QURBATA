# Expansion K38 — Inna Construction v0.2

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K37 DRAFT-FROZEN.  
**Target:** `إنّ + اسم إنّ + خبر إنّ` sederhana, cumulative-only.

## 1. Freeze criteria

K38 may freeze only if:
- the construction is structurally distinct from K33 recognition;
- enough PASS-A/PASS-B evidence exists across varied contexts;
- no core example requires K39+;
- `لام التوكيد`, clausal khabar, silah maushul, and other later relations are filtered out or tagged REVIEW/PREMATURE.

## 2. Evidence classes

### PASS-A — pure core
Pattern:
`إنّ + اسم ظاهر/ضمير already known + خبر nominal sederhana`

No new governor/particle/relation beyond K38.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
Same K38 relation plus only earlier K structures such as:
- idhafah already frozen;
- na'at already frozen;
- jar–majrur already frozen;
- attached pronoun functions already frozen;
provided they do not change the target relation.

### REVIEW-LAM
Contains emphatic lām not yet taught as a distinct competence.

### PREMATURE
Requires:
- full `ليس/كان` construction;
- relative-clause linkage;
- conditional linkage;
- clausal khabar outside prior competence;
- other new government relation.

## 3. Clean-yield assessment

The Qur'anic corpus provides many `إنّ` occurrences, but the **usable K38 bank is materially smaller** after strict filtering. This is expected and desirable.

The main causes of rejection/review are:
1. khabar is a verbal clause;
2. khabar contains `لام التوكيد`;
3. اسم إنّ or khabar embeds relative-clause structure;
4. the construction is nested inside another governor;
5. the useful semantic unit requires a larger clause than K38 alone.

Despite this, there remains a sufficiently broad class of simple/cumulative nominal examples to justify K38 as a real competence. The pedagogical bank should not be forced to exactly 30 examples if doing so contaminates the level.

## 4. Pedagogical minimum evidence rule

For K38 specifically, freeze readiness is based on **quality + diversity**, not a hard count.

Recommended teaching bank:
- aim 20–30+ if clean occurrences support it;
- minimum acceptable research freeze may be lower if all examples are high-confidence and cover multiple forms of اسم/خبر already known;
- corpus bank remains unlimited and may grow later without changing the K definition.

## 5. Scope of K38

K38 teaches the learner to:
1. recognize that `إنّ` changes the simple nominal-predication frame;
2. identify the **اسم إنّ** in a clean construction;
3. identify the **خبر إنّ** in a clean construction;
4. observe the case pattern from examples, without requiring a full abstract i'rab system;
5. distinguish K38 construction from mere recognition of `إنّ` at K33.

K38 does **not** teach:
- full أخوات إنّ;
- لام الابتداء/التوكيد;
- clausal khabar system;
- relative-clause khabar;
- advanced fronting/postponement;
- full theoretical nasb/rafa' paradigm.

## 6. Counterexample audit

No hard dependency reversal has been found that requires K39 (`ليس`) or K40 (`كان`) before K38.

Likewise, `لام التوكيد` is not a prerequisite; it is only a frequent co-occurring feature.

## 7. Freeze recommendation

**K38 = READY FOR DRAFT-FREEZE**, with the following permanent constraints:
- only occurrence-validated examples;
- pure/cumulative clean examples form the teaching set;
- REVIEW-LAM and PREMATURE examples remain in research bank but do not define the level;
- no quota inflation.

## 8. Next frontier after freeze

Provisional:
- K39-CAND — simple `ليس + اسم + خبر`;
- K40-CAND — simple `كان + اسم + خبر`;
- K41-CAND — fa'il mustatir dasar;
- K42-CAND — isim maushul + silah minimal.

Before fixing K39, perform a head-to-head between full `ليس` and full `كان`, using the same clean-yield and dependency criteria.
