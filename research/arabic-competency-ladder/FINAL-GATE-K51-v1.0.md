## Competency

**K51 — Mengenali efek jazm berupa `حذف النون` pada pola fi‘il mudhari‘ yang bentuk nūn-bearing dasarnya sudah familiar.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated jussive occurrence and a familiar corresponding mudhari‘ pattern, learner can:
1. identify the target as mudhari‘;
2. recognize the jussive environment;
3. notice that the expected inflectional nūn is absent;
4. relate that deletion to jazm.

## Prerequisite policy

A full prior mastery of `الأفعال الخمسة` is **not** required for core K51. However, every admitted teaching item must provide or presuppose a familiar nūn-bearing base. If the learner must derive the entire paradigm to know what is missing, the occurrence is above K51.

## Included

- controlled familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ patterns;
- one inflectional nūn deletion;
- locally transparent jussive environment;
- person/number variants as internal staging;
- cumulative syntax/morphology through K50.

## Excluded

- full `الأفعال الخمسة` paradigm production;
- full raf‘/nasb/jazm table;
- multiple simultaneous morphological changes;
- ambiguous base reconstruction;
- advanced orthographic explanation;
- complete attached-subject terminology as a requirement.
