# Clean Bank + Stress Test K64 — Explicit Cause / Reason Relation v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K63 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

Learner recognizes that one Qur'anic proposition functions as an explicit reason/ground for another proposition, using a validated overt reason/explanatory signal.

Core reasoning:

`Proposition A ← REASON B`

The learner must identify both the proposition being explained and the proposition that supplies the reason.

## PASS requirements

- both proposition spans are analyzable using ≤ K63;
- reason/explanatory signal is overt;
- the signal's function is occurrence-specifically validated;
- the reason relation is local and directional;
- no purpose/goal interpretation competes strongly;
- no hidden `أن`, new mood rule, or ellipsis is needed;
- no tafsir-only inference is required;
- one relation is sufficient to solve the item.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — overt local reason
Two overt propositions + overt validated explanatory marker + clear reason direction.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean reason
Internal clause complexity is already mastered, but the explanatory relation remains transparent.

### REVIEW-PURPOSE
The construction may mean purpose rather than cause/reason and cannot be solved from current competencies alone.

### REVIEW-MARKER
The marker is overt but highly multifunctional and requires a new disambiguation lesson.

### REVIEW-DISCOURSE
The relation depends on distant context or tafsir-level argumentation.

### PREMATURE
- implicit causality only;
- multiple candidate reasons;
- advanced balaghah as prerequisite;
- simultaneous hidden syntax/morphology;
- disputed explanatory classification.

## Stress test 1 — Is K64 just K63 backwards?

No. K63 asks which proposition is the consequence. K64 asks which proposition provides the grounds/explanation. The discourse role to be identified is distinct even when the underlying real-world relation is connected.

## Stress test 2 — Cause vs purpose

Core K64 excludes constructions where the learner must decide between:
- `because/reason` and
- `in order to/purpose`.

Purpose constructions remain a separate frontier because they often interact with nasb and hidden-governor machinery.

## Stress test 3 — Marker dependence

No particle is itself K64. Surface recognition is insufficient. The learner must identify the semantic relation between the proposition spans. Therefore different validated overt markers may be application breadth if the learner operation remains unchanged.

## Stress test 4 — Tafsir dependence

K64 is linguistic/discourse-semantic, not an inference-from-tafsir competence. If the cause relation becomes clear only after importing broad exegetical context, the occurrence is excluded from the clean bank.

## Assessment design

Core prompt:

`أيُّ المعنيين هو المعلَّل؟ وأيُّهما يبيّن السبب أو العلة؟`

Indonesian support:

`Pernyataan mana yang dijelaskan, dan mana yang menjadi sebab/alasan?`

Expected response identifies the explained proposition and its reason, then points to the overt validated signal.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH STRICT CAUSE-vs-PURPOSE FILTER.**

K64 is independently assessable and remains one operation if occurrence selection prevents purpose ambiguity and tafsir-only inference.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K64 — Mengenali relasi sebab/alasan antara dua proposisi Qurani melalui marker eksplisit yang tervalidasi, lalu menentukan proposisi yang dijelaskan dan proposisi yang menjadi sebab/alasan.**
