# Evidence Gate K49 — Basic Jazm by Sukun v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K48 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K49 target

Learner can recognize, in a validated simple conditional occurrence already analyzable through K47/K48, that a familiar **fi'il mudhari'** shows the basic **jazm effect by final sukun**.

Core operation:

`fi'il mudhari' familiar` → occurs in simple shart environment → **final sukun recognized as jazm effect**

K49 is intentionally restricted to the most transparent surface sign. It is not a full chapter on علامات الجزم.

## Hard prerequisites

- K47 simple condition→result linkage;
- K48 may be present but is not required unless the response uses `فاء جواب الشرط`;
- prior recognition of the relevant verbal form/morphology.

## Scope

### Included
- active simple fi'il mudhari';
- form already recognizable from earlier morphology scope;
- final consonant capable of showing sukun transparently;
- conditional position locally clear;
- no extra deletion analysis needed.

### Excluded
- `حذف حرف العلة`;
- `حذف النون`;
- irregular/ambiguous surface forms;
- full inventory of jussive governors;
- non-conditional jazm unless separately added later;
- advanced phonological/orthographic complications.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
Simple conditional occurrence; mudhari' form is familiar; final sukun is overt/transparent; no new operation beyond K49.

### PASS-B
Additional material is cumulative-clean through K48.

### REVIEW-MORPH
The word is jussive, but recognizing the underlying verbal form requires morphology beyond current scope.

### REVIEW-ORTH
Surface Qur'anic orthography/recitation makes the sukun effect pedagogically non-transparent without extra explanation.

### REVIEW-GOV
Jazm is clear but identifying exactly which governor is responsible would require a new rule set beyond the validated simple shart relation.

### PREMATURE
- deletion of weak letter;
- deletion of nun;
- nested conditional;
- multiple interacting governors;
- disputed syntactic analysis;
- advanced ellipsis.

## Anti-leak rule

At K49 the learner only needs to answer:

> `ما الفعل المضارع هنا؟ وما العلامة الظاهرة التي تدل على جزمه في هذا المثال؟`

Do not require:
- complete علامات الجزم;
- full conjugation table;
- all أدوات الجزم;
- explanation of every Qur'anic orthographic variant.

## Distinctness

K49 is not merely an expansion of K47. K47 teaches clause linkage; K49 teaches a **morphosyntactic effect on the verb form** caused within that already-known environment.

## Freeze criteria

K49 may freeze only if:
1. clean examples show final sukun transparently;
2. the verbal base is already recognizable;
3. the conditional relation is already clear without new clause theory;
4. no weak-letter/nun deletion is required;
5. evidence is sufficient to generalize the single operation without forcing quota.
