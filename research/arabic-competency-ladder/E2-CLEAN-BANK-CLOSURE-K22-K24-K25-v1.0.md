## K24 — demonstrative as mubtada' with simple predicate

Candidate forms:
- `ذَٰلِكَ الْكِتَابُ` — QS 2:2
- `ذَٰلِكَ مَثَلُهُمْ` — QS 48:29

The first is attractive but the precise syntactic analysis of `الكتاب` can be discussed with alternative traditional parsing depending on pedagogical framing; the second contains an attached-pronoun possessive inside the predicate. For strict clean-bank purposes, this is not yet sufficiently diversified and ambiguity-free.

**Decision: K24 remains E1 / E2-CANDIDATE.**

## K25 — mastered PP attached to a simple verb occurrence

Verified candidate environments:
- `تَجْرِي فِي الْبَحْرِ` — QS 2:164
- `أَنزَلَ اللَّهُ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ` — QS 2:164
- existing anchor environment from QS 48:29 (`يَبْتَغُونَ ... مِنَ اللَّهِ`)

These provide more than one verb and more than one preposition, with overt lexical verbs and locally interpretable PP attachment. `تجري في البحر` is especially clean because no attached-pronoun complement is required. `أنزل الله من السماء` also provides an overt subject and a simple source PP.

Boundary controls:
- `بِمَا يَنفَعُ النَّاسَ` in QS 2:164 is not used as a core K25 example because `ما` introduces additional structure.
- `فَأَحْيَا بِهِ الْأَرْضَ` is not used as the cleanest K25 item because the PP complement is an attached pronoun (K20).

**Decision: K25 PROMOTE E1 → E2.**
