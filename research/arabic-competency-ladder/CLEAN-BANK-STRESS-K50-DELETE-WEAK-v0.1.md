# Clean Bank + Stress Test K50 — Jazm by `حذف حرف العلة` v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K49 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

In a validated simple jussive/conditional environment, learner recognizes a familiar weak-final `فعل مضارع` whose final weak letter is absent because of jazm, and mentally restores only that local final segment.

## Internal staging, not separate K

The surface family may involve an underlying final:
- alif-type;
- wāw-type;
- yā’-type.

These are treated as **internal variants of one operation** when the learner action is unchanged:

`known weak-final mudhari‘ → jussive environment → final weak segment deleted`.

Do not create three competencies merely because the lexical weak letter differs.

## PASS requirements

- base mudhari‘ is already familiar or supplied in controlled comparison;
- only one final weak segment needs restoration;
- jussive environment is clear from earlier competencies;
- no other deletion competes;
- no full weak-verb paradigm is needed;
- Qur'anic spelling does not create a second orthographic lesson.

## Stress test — alif / wāw / yā’ split

### Question
Does each weak-final class create a distinct learner operation?

### Result
**No, at core level.** The reconstruction target differs lexically, but the grammatical reasoning remains the same. Therefore the three forms belong to staged examples inside K50 rather than K50/K51/K52 fragmentation.

A form moves to REVIEW if its lexical base is not recoverable without broader morphology.

## Contrast with K49

K49:
- overt sukūn is visible;
- learner labels a surface ending.

K50:
- expected weak-final segment is absent;
- learner performs one local reconstruction.

Therefore K50 is genuinely more demanding and distinct.

## Contrast with `حذف النون`

Deletion of nūn should remain outside K50 because identifying it reliably depends on the person/number morphology of the five-verb pattern. That adds a different morphological dependency, not merely another final letter.

## Anti-overload

K50 does not require:
- names/categories of all weak verbs;
- complete conjugation tables;
- historical morphology;
- deletion outside jussive contexts;
- simultaneous analysis of several omitted segments.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH INTERNAL STAGING.**

Alif/wāw/yā’ weak-final realizations can remain within one K50 if examples are ordered from maximally transparent to less transparent and every occurrence passes the one-step reconstruction gate.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K50 — Mengenali jazm melalui `حذف حرف العلة` pada fi‘il mudhari‘ mu‘tal akhir yang bentuk dasarnya familiar, dengan satu rekonstruksi lokal.**
