## Problem
A REL tag or identification of ṣilat al-mawṣūl proves the relative-pronoun/clause relation, but does not by itself prove that a specific resumptive element ('ā'id) is deleted. K45 therefore requires reconstruction evidence beyond ordinary relative-clause detection.

## Mandatory positive gate
A K45 positive item must satisfy all conditions:
1. explicit relative pronoun / relative construction;
2. a locally identifiable syntactic slot inside the relative clause;
3. no overt resumptive fills that slot;
4. the antecedent supplies a uniquely recoverable referent for the missing slot;
5. reconstruction does not require unrestricted tafsir inference;
6. traditional i'rāb or another authoritative grammatical analysis supports the ellipsis where interpretation is sensitive.

## Mandatory controls
N1 — overt-resumptive relative clause: must be excluded from deleted-resumptive positives.
N2 — relative clause where the antecedent is represented by an overt/embedded subject or object: exclude unless the target is independently shown to be an additional deleted slot.
N3 — clause with no uniquely recoverable missing argument: exclude.
N4 — merely semantic coreference without syntactic ellipsis: exclude.

## Evidence-source hierarchy
Tier 1: Qur'anic text and local morphology/syntax.
Tier 2: traditional i'rāb / grammatical analysis of the verse.
Tier 3: tafsir only as interpretive confirmation, not as the sole proof of deletion.
