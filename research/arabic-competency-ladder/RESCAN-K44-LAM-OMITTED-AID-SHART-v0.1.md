# Rescan K44 — Lām Tawkīd vs Omitted `عائد` vs Simple Shart v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K43 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan operasi baru paling ringan setelah K43, dengan prinsip:
- cumulative-only;
- tidak mengubah satu K menjadi bab nahwu penuh;
- tidak menambah K hanya karena lexical frequency;
- Qur'anic occurrence harus dapat divalidasi secara lokal.

---

## Candidate A — `لام التوكيد` in already-mastered `إنّ` construction

### Foundation
- K33: recognition `إنّ`;
- K38: simple `إنّ + اسم + خبر` construction.

### New operation
Recognize an overt emphatic lām attached to/preceding the relevant predicate constituent and identify its **emphasis function** without changing the underlying K38 parse.

### Dependency burden
Low to moderate:
- overt marker;
- no omitted element;
- underlying syntax already K38;
- scope can be restricted to clear occurrences where lām sits in an already analyzable `إنّ` construction.

### Main risk
Not every initial lām is the same phenomenon. Occurrence-specific classification is mandatory; do not merge oath lām, jawab lām, command lām, or other lām functions.

**Judgement: VERY HIGH for K44 if narrowly scoped.**

---

## Candidate B — omitted/estimated `عائد`

### Foundation
- K42: maushul + silah boundary;
- K43: explicit `عائد`.

### New operation
Infer a resumptive relation that is not overtly present.

### Dependency burden
High:
- requires reconstruction/ellipsis;
- deletion conditions vary;
- may require syntactic-role recovery;
- risks turning into advanced relative-clause theory.

**Judgement: DISTINCT but clearly heavier than overt emphatic lām.**

---

## Candidate C — simple conditional relation

### New operation
Link condition protasis to apodosis and eventually understand marker/mood behavior.

### Dependency burden
High:
- two-clause relation;
- particle ambiguity (`إنْ` vs `إنّ` etc.);
- verb mood/jazm may become relevant;
- jawab boundary and markers can vary.

A recognition-only K for `إنْ` would be low-yield and risk lexical inflation; the meaningful competence is relational and therefore heavier.

**Judgement: DISTINCT but later.**

---

## Head-to-head decision

**K44-CAND — restricted `لام التوكيد` in simple `إنّ` construction.**

Reason:
1. overt rather than reconstructed;
2. builds directly on frozen K38;
3. adds one semantic/scope operation while preserving underlying parse;
4. occurrence filtering can separate other lām types;
5. substantially lighter than ellipsis or two-clause conditional linkage.

## K44 provisional definition

Learner can identify **the emphatic lām in a validated simple `إنّ` construction** and state that it adds emphasis while leaving the already-mastered `إنّ + اسم + خبر` relation intact.

## Hard exclusions

K44 does not teach:
- all types of Arabic lām;
- lām al-amr;
- lām jawab al-qasam;
- lām in conditional response;
- full rhetoric of emphasis;
- advanced scope ambiguity.

## Provisional frontier after K44

Do not assign numbers yet. Rescan after K44 among:

- expansion of nawāsikh predicate types;
- other corpus-discovered structural operations.
