# Final Gate K46 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN — RESEARCH LAYER  
**Baseline:** K1–K45 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K46 — Mengenali jumlah fi'liyah sederhana sebagai khabar bagi mubtada' eksplisit, dengan `رابط` lokal yang seluruh mekanismenya telah dikuasai pada K sebelumnya.**

## Core learner operation

Learner can:
1. identify the explicit mubtada';
2. delimit a short verbal clause;
3. recognize that the entire verbal clause occupies the khabar slot;
4. identify a transparent local link to the mubtada' using already-mastered pronoun/subject relations.

## Allowed core

- short verbal clause;
- explicit or basic hidden subject already covered by prior Ks;
- transparent pronoun/link;
- complements only if cumulative-clean through K45.

## Locked

K46 does not unlock:
- taxonomy of روابط الجملة الخبرية;
- nominal clause as khabar;
- clausal khabar under `إنّ`, `كان`, or `ليس`;
- conditional relations;
- nested clauses;
- advanced discourse reference;
- disputed parsing.

## Dependency decision

Stress testing confirmed that `رابط` does not require a prior standalone K in the restricted core. Its realizations can be interpreted using previously frozen pronoun, subject, and reference competencies. The distinct new operation is **clause-as-predicate embedding**.

## Freeze judgement

**PASS — DRAFT-FROZEN.**

K46 is stable enough for research-layer sequencing. Any expansion to other clause types or host constructions must pass separate evidence gates.
