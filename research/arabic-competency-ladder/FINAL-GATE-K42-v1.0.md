# Final Gate K42 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN — RESEARCH LAYER ONLY

## K42 — REL-MAWSUL-SILAH
**Mengenali hubungan isim maushul dengan silahnya dan menentukan batas silah sederhana.**

## Core operation

`اسم موصول` → `صلة الموصول`

Learner identifies:
1. the explicit isim maushul;
2. where the silah begins;
3. where the minimal silah ends in a simple occurrence.

## Hard prerequisites

- K23 isim maushul recognition;
- nominal/verbal relations already frozen;
- K41 basic fa'il mustatir where needed inside short verbal silah.

## Locked at K42

K42 does **not** unlock:
- full taxonomy of `عائد`;
- omitted resumptive reconstruction;
- nested relative clauses;
- advanced discourse antecedence;
- conditional linkage;
- complex clausal parsing beyond cumulative K1–K41.

## Evidence policy

Core evidence counts only when:
- maushul is explicit;
- silah is short and boundary is pedagogically clear;
- internal grammar is cumulative through K41;
- full `عائد` analysis is not required to teach the target operation.

Cases needing explicit resumptive analysis remain `REVIEW-AID` or later.

## Distinctness decision

Boundary/linkage recognition and full `عائد` analysis are distinct learnable operations. Therefore K42 remains atomic; `عائد` is deferred to a later frontier and does not automatically receive K43.

## Integrity

No dependency reversal found. K41 appropriately precedes K42 because simple verbal silah may contain a basic hidden subject.

## Updated frozen span

Research-layer competency sequence is now DRAFT-FROZEN through **K42**.
