## Competency

**K49 — Mengenali efek jazm dasar berupa sukūn yang transparan pada fi‘il mudhari‘ dalam struktur syarth sederhana.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated simple conditional occurrence, learner can:
1. identify the target as `فعل مضارع`;
2. recognize the local jussive environment;
3. identify transparent final sukūn as the jazm effect in that occurrence.

## Included

- sound/familiar mudhari‘;
- simple conditional environment;
- transparent surface sukūn;
- occurrence-specific recognition;
- cumulative internal syntax through K48.

## Excluded

- `حذف حرف العلة`;
- `حذف النون`;
- full taxonomy of `أدوات الجزم`;
- general jazm outside the validated scope;
- advanced Qur'anic orthographic explanation;
- irregular morphology;
- complete i‘rāb theory.
