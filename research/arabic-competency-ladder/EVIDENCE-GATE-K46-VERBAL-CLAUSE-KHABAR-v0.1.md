# Evidence Gate K46 — Simple Verbal Clause as Khabar v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K45 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K46 target

Learner can recognize that the **khabar of an explicit mubtada' is a short verbal clause (`جملة فعلية`)**, not merely a single lexical item or PP.

Core representation:

`مبتدأ` → `[فعل + فاعل/فاعل مستتر (+ complements already mastered)]` = `خبر جملة`

The new operation is **clause-as-predicate recognition**.

## Why this is distinct

Earlier competencies establish:
- nominal predication;
- verbal clause structure;
- explicit and basic hidden subjects;
- pronoun relations;
- relative-clause boundary/linkage.

K46 combines already-mastered internal verbal syntax with a new external role: the whole verbal clause fills the khabar slot.

## The `رابط` risk

A verbal sentence functioning as khabar commonly needs a relation (`رابط`) connecting it to the mubtada'. This can be an overt pronoun or another locally transparent link.

### K46 policy

K46 does **not** teach a full taxonomy of روابط الجملة الخبرية.

PASS examples require the link to be transparent using K1–K45, preferably:
- explicit pronoun already analyzable;
- basic hidden subject whose referential relation is locally obvious under K41;
- another link that does not require a new theory.

If explaining the رابط itself requires a new operation, classify REVIEW-RABIT or PREMATURE.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
- explicit/simple mubtada';
- short verbal clause as khabar;
- internal clause fully ≤ K45;
- رابط transparent;
- no nested clause.

### PASS-B
Additional material is allowed only when cumulative-clean through K45.

### REVIEW-RABIT
Clause-as-khabar is plausible/clear, but the link to mubtada' needs an operation not yet independently mastered.

### REVIEW-BOUNDARY
Determining where the khabar clause ends requires higher discourse/clause analysis.

### PREMATURE
- nested clause;
- conditional relation;
- complex relative construction;
- disputed analysis;
- omitted multi-element linkage;
- later nawasikh/clausal-predicate behavior being imported.

## Anti-leak rule

At K46 the learner should answer:

> `أين المبتدأ؟ وما الجملة الفعلية التي وقعت خبراً له؟ وما الرابط الظاهر أو البسيط بينهما؟`

Do not require:
- all kinds of رابط;
- full clause taxonomy;
- conditional parsing;
- advanced discourse reference;
- full i'rab of every internal word.

## Freeze criteria

K46 can freeze only if:
1. clean Qur'anic occurrences establish clause-as-predicate robustly;
2. رابط is transparent and does not become a hidden second competence;
3. internal verbal clauses require only K1–K45;
4. examples are structurally diverse enough to generalize;
5. no numeric quota is forced by admitting K47+ dependencies.
