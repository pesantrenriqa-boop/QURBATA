## K47 — REL-SHART-CORE
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali batas dan hubungan syarth sederhana: marker → جملة الشرط → جواب الشرط.
- **Primary domain:** clause / interclausal dependency.
- **Learner operation:** identify explicit conditional marker, delimit protasis and apodosis, and state the condition→result relation.
- **Hard prerequisites:** all internal clause grammar must be ≤ K46.
- **Exclusions:** full شرط taxonomy; jazm rules; full فاء الجواب theory; omitted/nested جواب; oath-condition competition; rhetorical/counterfactual semantics.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K48 — REL-FA-JAWAB-EXPLICIT
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali فاء جواب الشرط eksplisit dan menentukan awal جواب الشرط.
- **Primary domain:** clause / conditional response marker.
- **Learner operation:** within a K47-valid conditional, classify overt فـ specifically as فاء جواب الشرط and mark response onset.
- **Hard prerequisite:** K47.
- **Exclusions:** full taxonomy of فـ; memorization of all جواب types requiring fā’; full jazm theory; omitted/nested/disputed conditionals.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K49 — MORPH-JAZM-SUKUN
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali efek jazm dasar berupa sukūn transparan pada mudhari‘ dalam syarth sederhana.
- **Primary domain:** morphology / mood realization.
- **Learner operation:** identify familiar mudhari‘, validate jussive environment, and connect final sukūn to jazm.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K47 conditional architecture + prior mudhari‘ recognition.
- **Exclusions:** حذف حرف العلة; حذف النون; full jussive-governor inventory; irregular/advanced orthography; complete i‘rāb theory.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K50 — MORPH-JAZM-DELETE-WEAK
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali jazm melalui حذف حرف العلة pada weak-final mudhari‘ yang familiar.
- **Primary domain:** morphology / reconstructive mood realization.
- **Learner operation:** detect one missing final weak segment and reconstruct it locally as the jussive effect.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K49 + familiarity with the lexical/base form.
- **Internal staging:** alif-/wāw-/yā’-final are variants inside one K.
- **Exclusions:** حذف النون; full weak-verb taxonomy; full paradigms; ambiguous base; multiple deletions.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K51 — MORPH-JAZM-DELETE-NUN
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali efek jazm berupa حذف النون pada familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ patterns.
- **Primary domain:** morphology / reconstructive mood realization.
- **Learner operation:** notice absence of expected inflectional nūn and relate that deletion to jazm.
- **Hard prerequisites:** validated jussive environment + controlled familiar nūn-bearing base.
- **Exclusions:** full الأفعال الخمسة production; complete mood tables; ambiguous nūn identity; advanced person/number derivation.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K52 — MORPH-NASB-FATHA
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali efek nasb dasar berupa fatḥah transparan pada familiar mudhari‘ setelah nāṣib eksplisit tervalidasi.
- **Primary domain:** morphology / mood realization.
- **Learner operation:** identify overt governor, target mudhari‘, visible final fatḥah, and connect the ending to nasb.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K7 mudhari‘ recognition + cumulative syntax/morphology through K51.
- **Exclusions:** full نواصب المضارع inventory; hidden أن; nasb by حذف النون; complex weak-final realization; full mood paradigm.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K53 — SYN-ELLIPSIS-HIDDEN-AN
- **Canonical competence:** merekonstruksi satu أن مقدرة yang tervalidasi sebagai nāṣib bagi mudhari‘ dalam konstruksi terkontrol.
- **Primary domain:** syntax / controlled ellipsis reconstruction.
- **Learner operation:** identify trigger context, reconstruct one hidden أن, and connect it to the verb’s nasb.
- **Hard prerequisite:** K52 visible nasb effect plus the trigger family used by the item.
- **Exclusions:** full taxonomy of أن المضمرة; all obligatory/permissible environments; disputed/multiple hidden governors; advanced morphology.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K54 — REL-FA-JAWAB-PREDICT
- **Canonical competence:** memprediksi kebutuhan فاء جواب الشرط ketika جواب syarth berbentuk jumlah ismiyyah, lalu memverifikasi marker tersebut.
- **Primary domain:** clause / generative conditional reasoning.
- **Learner operation:** classify response as nominal, predict fā’, then verify overt marker in Qur'anic occurrence.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K47 + K48 + ability to recognize a nominal response.
- **Exclusions:** full traditional list of جواب types requiring fā’; omitted/nested/disputed jawab; oath-condition competition.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K55 — MORPH-RAF-DAMMA
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali raf‘ dasar pada familiar sound-final mudhari‘ melalui overt ḍammah dalam lingkungan tanpa active nāṣib/jāzim.
- **Primary domain:** morphology / mood realization.
- **Learner operation:** validate absence of an active local governor, identify final ḍammah, and classify the verb as marfū‘.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K7 + ability to distinguish nasb/jazm environments developed through K49–K54.
- **Exclusions:** ثبوت النون; ضمة مقدرة; weak-final non-visible endings; full mood production.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K56 — MORPH-RAF-THUBUT-NUN
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali raf‘ melalui ثبوت النون pada familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ patterns.
- **Primary domain:** morphology / mood realization.
- **Learner operation:** validate raf‘ environment, identify retained inflectional nūn, and classify ثبوت النون as the raf‘ sign.
- **Hard prerequisites:** familiar nūn-bearing base + K55-level raf‘ environment recognition.
- **Exclusions:** full الأفعال الخمسة production; complete mood tables; ambiguous nūn; estimated endings.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K57 — MORPH-RAF-ESTIMATED-DAMMA
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali raf‘ pada weak-final mudhari‘ melalui ضمة مقدرة, dimulai dari alif-final dan diperluas terkontrol.
- **Primary domain:** morphology / abstract i‘rāb representation.
- **Learner operation:** identify familiar weak-final mudhari‘, validate raf‘ environment, note absence of overt ḍammah, and classify the sign as estimated.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K55 raf‘ reasoning + weak-final form familiarity.
- **Internal staging:** alif-final core; wāw/yā’ controlled extension.
- **Exclusions:** full theory of التعذر والثقل; nominal estimated i‘rāb; multiple estimated signs; disputed parsing; full weak-verb taxonomy.
- **Architecture status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.
