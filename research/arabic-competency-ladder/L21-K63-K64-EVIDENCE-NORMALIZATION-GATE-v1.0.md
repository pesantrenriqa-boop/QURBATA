## 1. Authoritative status

Both competencies are already DRAFT-FROZEN in the authoritative ladder.

### K63
Learner identifies two overt propositions, an overt prevalidated linker, and classifies proposition B as the result/consequence of proposition A.

Core exclusions include ambiguous temporal-vs-result readings, ordinary condition→response items, implicit causality, long-distance inference, and unresolved multifunctional `ف`.

### K64
Learner identifies the proposition being explained, the proposition supplying the reason, and an overt validated explanatory signal.

Core exclusions include purpose/goal ambiguity, hidden `أن` as a new operation, tafsir-only causality, advanced balaghah, and unresolved multifunctional markers.

## 3. Required K63 occurrence signature

A candidate can enter L21 placement only if all are verified:
1. proposition A overt;
2. proposition B overt;
3. overt linker;
4. linker independently validated as result/consequence in that occurrence;
5. B is not merely later in time (K59);
6. relation is not simply condition→response (K47/K48);
7. no tafsir-only inference;
8. one-operation scoring is possible.

Candidate status until such evidence is normalized: `HOLD-EVIDENCE-NORMALIZATION`.

## 4. Required K64 occurrence signature

A candidate can enter L21 placement only if all are verified:
1. explained proposition overt;
2. reason proposition overt;
3. overt explanatory/reason signal;
4. marker function occurrence-specifically validated;
5. reason is not purpose/goal (K66);
6. no hidden syntax/mood operation is newly required;
7. no tafsir-only causal inference;
8. local directional relation can be objectively scored.

Candidate status until such evidence is normalized: `HOLD-EVIDENCE-NORMALIZATION`.
