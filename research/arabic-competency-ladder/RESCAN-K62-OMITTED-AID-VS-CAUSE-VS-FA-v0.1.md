# RESCAN K62 — Omitted `العائد` vs Causal Relation vs Filtered `ف` v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE SELECTION  
**Baseline:** K1–K61 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K62 must add a distinct learner operation after explicit relative-clause reference resolution. The next K must not be chosen merely because it is the next traditional rule.

## Candidate A — omitted `العائد` in relative clauses

Potential operation:

`اسم موصول + صلة مكتملة ظاهرياً → detect an unexpressed resumptive dependency required by the relative construction`

### Strengths
- natural conceptual extension from K60–K61;
- moves from overt reference resolution to structurally licensed implicit reference;
- introduces a controlled null-element inference.

### Risks
- can overlap with general ellipsis/reconstruction;
- omitted resumptives vary by syntactic position and licensing conditions;
- proving the omission may require advanced transitivity/governance knowledge;
- could become a textbook rule-list about when deletion is allowed.

### Verdict
**DEFER as immediate K62.** The operation is real but dependency cost is currently high. It should wait until a clean, tightly licensed subtype can be isolated.

---

## Candidate B — explicit causal/result relation between two already-analyzable clauses

Potential operation:

`clause A → overt causal/result marker → clause B` and learner identifies B as consequence/result of A.

### Strengths
- advances from structural linkage to semantic relation;
- useful for Qur'anic comprehension;
- distinct from neutral coordination (K58) and temporal sequencing (K59).

### Risks
- causal/result markers are heterogeneous;
- `ف` is multifunctional;
- semantic inference may outrun the grammar if marker choice is not tightly controlled.

### Verdict
**PROMISING, but marker-specific filtering required before numbering.**

---

## Candidate C — filtered clause sequencing/result with `ف`

Potential operation:

Learner first disambiguates a Qur'anic `ف` from already-known `فاء جواب الشرط`, then recognizes a clean clause-to-clause relation such as close progression/result.

### Strengths
- builds on K48/K54 disambiguation and K58/K59 clause relations;
- overt marker;
- potentially high-frequency Qur'anic utility.

### Risks
- asking both “which fā’?” and “what relation?” may create two operations in one K;
- temporal succession and consequence are not always cleanly separable;
- surface `ف` cannot itself guarantee the intended relation.

### Verdict
**DO NOT FREEZE AS A BROAD `ف` COMPETENCY.** A narrower subtype is needed.

---

## Candidate D — contrastive coordination with overt `لكن`/related marker

Potential operation:

`proposition A + overt contrast marker + proposition B → identify correction/contrast relation`

### Strengths
- overt semantic relation;
- distinct from sequencing;
- can be assessed with two already-analyzable spans;
- does not depend on reconstructing a null element.

### Risks
- forms/functions of `لكن` vary;
- nominal/clausal attachment must be controlled;
- full contrastive-particle inventory would overload the K.

### Verdict
**STRONG CANDIDATE if a clause-level overt subset is clean.**

---

## Candidate E — explicit reason/cause clause via a highly transparent lexical/particle construction

### Verdict
Potentially strong, but requires corpus-specific evidence before selecting a marker.

# Selection logic

At this point, no candidate should be frozen directly. The most responsible next step is a **marker-specific evidence comparison** between:

1. clause-level overt contrast (`لكن` or another validated marker), and
2. overt result/consequence relation using a tightly filtered `ف` subset.

The omitted `العائد` remains in the frontier queue but is not selected because its inferential and prerequisite burden is higher.

## K62 frontier formulation

K62 should likely introduce a **new semantic relation between already analyzable clauses**, rather than another invisible relative-clause element.

## Decision

**Advance to K62 marker-specific evidence scan: CONTRAST vs RESULT.**

Do not assign K62 yet. Require:
- one overt marker;
- two spans analyzable ≤ K61;
- one new relation only;
- no simultaneous marker-disambiguation burden;
- stable Qur'anic evidence;
- no full conjunction inventory.
