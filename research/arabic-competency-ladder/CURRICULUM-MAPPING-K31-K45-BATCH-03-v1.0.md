## Purpose
Map K31–K45 from canonical linguistic competencies into prerequisite-safe curriculum delivery without equating competency ID with teaching level, meeting, page, or test item.

## Stage logic
- S3 = Sentence Relations: learners identify and operate relations among constituents/clauses while later features remain controlled.
- S4 = Complex Clause Integration: learners integrate multiple grammatical relations, ellipsis/reconstruction, and higher clause dependencies.

- CP-S3B: K35–K36

- CP-S4B: K45, but only with teacher-review fallback for uncertain reconstruction.

## Placement-test implications
1. K31–K37 can be sampled through contrastive recognition and bounded analysis.
2. K38–K43 may function as stage discriminators because they require integration of earlier operations.
3. K45 must never be scored from translation intuition alone; the omitted resumptive must be uniquely reconstructable from the local grammatical environment.
4. Failure on a high K does not automatically invalidate all lower K; diagnostic scoring remains competency-granular.

## Acceleration
A learner may skip instructional bundles when prerequisite competencies are directly demonstrated. Acceleration changes delivery path, not mastery thresholds.
