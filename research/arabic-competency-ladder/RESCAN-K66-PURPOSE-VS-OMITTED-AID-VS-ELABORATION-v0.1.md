# RESCAN K66 — Purpose vs Omitted `العائد` vs Elaboration v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE SELECTION  
**Baseline:** K1–K65 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K66 must add one new learner operation. It must not merely continue textbook ordering or duplicate prior discourse relations.

## Candidate A — explicit purpose/goal relation

Potential operation:

`Action/statement A → overt validated purpose marker → Goal B`

Learner identifies B as the intended goal/purpose of A.

### Strengths
- semantically distinct from K64 cause/reason;
- high value for Qur'anic comprehension;
- can be framed as a directed discourse-semantic relation;
- completes a useful contrast:
  - cause/reason = why something is/was so;
  - purpose/goal = for what end something is done.

### Risks
- many purpose constructions involve `لام التعليل`, nasb, or hidden `أن`;
- some examples would require multiple prior operations simultaneously;
- particle/function ambiguity can become the real task.

### Containment
Core K66 should include only occurrences where:
- purpose relation is overt and independently validated;
- internal mood/morphology is already fully analyzable ≤ K65;
- learner does not need to discover a new hidden governor;
- no cause-vs-purpose ambiguity remains.

### Verdict
**STRONGEST CANDIDATE if marker-specific filtering succeeds.**

## Candidate B — omitted `العائد` subtype

Potential operation:

`اسم موصول + صلة → infer one missing resumptive element`

### Strengths
- natural extension of K60–K61;
- introduces null-reference reconstruction.

### Risks
- licensing conditions vary;
- often requires transitivity/governance knowledge;
- easy to turn into a rule list;
- higher inferential burden than overt purpose relations.

### Verdict
**DEFER.** Still too dependency-heavy for K66 core.

---

## Candidate C — elaboration/specification relation

Potential operation:

`general proposition A → overt explanatory/specifying expression B`

### Strengths
- useful discourse relation;
- could extend beyond cause/result/contrast.

### Risks
- surface marking is heterogeneous;
- can overlap with بدل, apposition, تفسير, relative clauses, or lexical restatement;
- clean marker-specific evidence is less obvious.

### Verdict
**DEFER pending cleaner marker family.**

## Candidate D — more exception subtype theory

### Verdict
**MASTERY/TRANSFER**, not K66. Do not split `إلا` subtypes into competency numbers.

# Selection

## K66-CAND

> **Mengenali relasi tujuan/غاية antara suatu tindakan atau proposisi Qurani dan tujuan yang dinyatakan secara eksplisit melalui konstruksi yang sudah tervalidasi.**

Core representation:

`A → PURPOSE/GOAL → B`

Learner operation:
1. identify the action/proposition being oriented toward an end;
2. identify the overt purpose expression;
3. state B as the intended goal rather than the prior cause.

## Distinctness from K64

K64 asks:
`Mengapa A terjadi/ditetapkan?` → reason/grounds.

K66 asks:
`Untuk tujuan apa A dilakukan/diarahkan?` → intended end.

This is a genuine semantic-direction contrast.

## Initial evidence policy

PASS only when:
- the purpose reading is overt and standard;
- both A and B are locally identifiable;
- no new mood rule must be learned;
- hidden `أن` is not itself the target operation;
- no serious causal reading competes;
- no long tafsir inference is required.

REVIEW/PREMATURE:
- ambiguous cause/purpose;
- purpose inferred only from context;
- multiple nested goals;
- new nasb mechanism;
- hidden-governor reconstruction as prerequisite;
- rhetoric-only final-cause interpretation.

## Decision

**Advance K66-CAND to marker-specific evidence/distinctness gate.**

Do not freeze until the corpus yields a clean overt subset that can be solved as one operation: identify intended goal.
