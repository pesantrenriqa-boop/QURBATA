# Evidence Gate K52 — Nasb by Transparent Fatḥah v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K51 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K52 target

Learner can recognize a familiar `فعل مضارع` in a validated simple nasb environment and identify a **transparent final fatḥah** as the local effect of nasb.

Core operation:

`فعل مضارع familiar` + validated nāṣib environment → **fatḥah as nasb effect**

K52 introduces mood-effect recognition for nasb, not the full chapter of `نواصب المضارع`.

## Scope policy

PASS examples must use only an occurrence whose nasb environment is already explicit and locally transparent. The learner should not need to infer an omitted `أن` or reconstruct a complex syntactic relation.

Preferred first-stage environments are explicit particles/structures whose function is stable in that occurrence and whose surrounding grammar is ≤ K51.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — pure transparent nasb
- target is clearly mudhari‘;
- nāṣib is explicit and occurrence-specific function is clear;
- final fatḥah is visible and pedagogically transparent;
- no weak-final complication;
- no five-verb nūn deletion;
- no hidden particle.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
Additional material is allowed only when all non-K52 operations are already frozen through K51.

### REVIEW-PARTICLE
Surface particle is explicit but its function would require a new particle taxonomy or disputed classification.

### REVIEW-MORPH
Nasb is valid, but the surface realization is not simple fatḥah (e.g. deletion-based morphology) or otherwise exceeds K52 core.

### REVIEW-HIDDEN
Nasb depends on an omitted/estimated `أن` or another reconstruction not yet unlocked.

### PREMATURE
- advanced purpose/result constructions;
- multiple possible governors;
- hidden governor analysis;
- complex clause embedding;
- simultaneous new morphological operation;
- disputed Qur'anic grammatical analysis.

## Anti-leak rule

At K52 the learner only needs to answer:

> `ما الفعل المضارع؟ وما الناصب الظاهر؟ وما أثر النصب الظاهر في آخر الفعل؟`

Do not require:
- full list of `نواصب المضارع`;
- all meanings/usages of `أن، لن، كي، حتى...`;
- omitted `أن` theory;
- nasb by deletion of nūn;
- advanced semantic scope.

## Distinctness from K49

K49 taught a visible **jazm** effect via sukūn. K52 teaches a visible **nasb** effect via fatḥah. The learner must now distinguish two different syntactic environments and two different mood outcomes.

## Freeze criteria

K52 may freeze only if:
1. clean Qur'anic examples show explicit, stable nasb environments;
2. final fatḥah is truly transparent;
3. no hidden governor theory is needed;
4. deletion-based nasb morphology remains outside core;
5. the competence remains independently assessable from mere particle recognition.
