# FINAL GATE K48 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K47 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K48 — Mengenali `فاء جواب الشرط` yang eksplisit pada struktur syarth sederhana dan menggunakannya untuk menentukan awal jawab syarth.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated simple conditional occurrence already within K47 scope, learner can:
1. locate the conditional relation;
2. identify the overt `فـ` that functions specifically as `فاء جواب الشرط`;
3. mark the beginning of the response after that marker.

## Dependency

Requires K47 simple conditional boundary/linkage. Generic recognition of `فـ` is insufficient; the learner must classify its local syntactic function.

## Included

- overt `فاء جواب الشرط`;
- simple, stable conditional-response boundary;
- internal material analyzable through K1–K47;
- occurrence-specific functional classification.

## Excluded

- full taxonomy of Arabic `فـ`;
- memorization of all rules requiring fā’ in jawab;
- full jazm theory;
- oath–condition competition;
- omitted jawab;
- nested conditionals;
- disputed or discourse-heavy analyses.

## Assessment prompt

`أين فاء جواب الشرط؟ وأين يبدأ جواب الشرط بعدها؟`

## Gate result

- distinctness: PASS;
- cumulative dependency: PASS;
- anti-inventory: PASS;
- jazm independence: PASS;
- scope control: PASS.

**Decision: K48 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

The broader rule explaining *when* a conditional response requires fā’ is not bundled into K48. K48 is an occurrence-level recognition competence. Rule generalization remains a later candidate if it proves pedagogically distinct.
