# Rescan K55 — Predictive Fā’ Transfer vs Raf‘ Recognition vs Other Structural Operations v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE

## Purpose

Mencari operasi baru paling ringan setelah K54 tanpa:
- mengubah tangga menjadi daftar subkategori `فاء جواب الشرط`;
- membuat K baru untuk sekadar konsolidasi K lama;
- mengikuti urutan kitab nahwu secara mekanis.

---

## Candidate A — Predictive `فاء جواب الشرط` for another response type

Potential examples: response introduced by `لن`, `قد`, `سوف`, or a talabiyyah pattern.

### Dependency burden
Moderate–high.

Why not automatically K55:
- K54 already introduced the **predictive** operation;
- changing the response subtype may be transfer/application, not a new learner operation;
- each subtype adds rule-list risk.

**Judgement:** usually application breadth, not default K55.

---

## Candidate B — Explicit raf‘ effect on familiar mudhari‘ in an ungoverned/default environment

### Foundation
- K49–K53 establish syntax→morphology effects for jazm and nasb.

### New operation
Recognize that a familiar mudhari‘ remains in its default/raf‘ state when no validated nāṣib or jāzim is governing it, and identify a transparent visible raf‘ ending in controlled examples.

### Dependency burden
Low–moderate.

Potential value:
- closes the basic three-way mood contrast without making the contrast itself a K;
- gives the learner an explicit positive model of raf‘ rather than treating it only as “not nasb/not jazm”;
- can be occurrence-specific and form-based.

### Risk
If defined too abstractly as “default case,” it becomes meta-grammar. Core must use overtly familiar endings and a controlled absence-of-governor test.

**Judgement:** HIGH K55 candidate.

## Candidate C — Full raf‘–nasb–jazm contrast/generalization

### Operation
Given several forms, classify mood from governor + ending.

### Problem
This is primarily a **mastery/consolidation layer** over K49–K55-type evidence. It does not necessarily introduce a new atomic operation.

**Judgement:** do not allocate a K by default.

## Head-to-head result

**K55-CAND — mengenali raf‘ dasar pada fi‘il mudhari‘ familiar dalam lingkungan yang tidak memiliki nāṣib/jāzim tervalidasi, dengan tanda permukaan yang transparan.**

This is not “the whole marfū‘ chapter.” It introduces one local operation:

`familiar mudhari‘ + no active nasb/jazm governor → recognize transparent raf‘ form`.

## Scope proposal

Start with forms whose raf‘ marker is maximally transparent and already familiar. Avoid immediately bundling:
- raf‘ by ثبوت النون in full `الأفعال الخمسة` theory;
- تقدير الضمة on weak-final verbs;
- disputed or orthographically opaque examples;
- full mood paradigm production.

## Architectural rationale

K49–K55 can then form a controlled mood bridge:
- K49–K51: jazm effects;
- K52–K53: nasb effects;
- K55: explicit raf‘ recognition.

The three-way system is then available for later consolidation without creating a redundant “summary K.”
