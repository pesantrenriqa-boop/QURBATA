# Clean Bank + Stress Test K46 — Verbal-Clause Khabar and `رابط` v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE RESEARCH  
**Baseline:** K1–K45 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Question

Apakah kemampuan mengenali `جملة فعلية` sebagai khabar dapat diajarkan sebagai satu operasi baru, atau `رابط` antara mubtada' dan jumlah khabariyyah harus menjadi kompetensi tersendiri terlebih dahulu?

## Core finding

Pada occurrence sederhana, `رابط` dapat diperlakukan sebagai **dependency yang sudah didukung** oleh kompetensi lama bila link tersebut berupa:

1. **dhamir eksplisit** yang kembali kepada mubtada' dan perannya sudah analyzable;
2. **fa'il mustatir dasar** yang grammatical person-nya jelas dan referensinya lokal;
3. hubungan pronominal sederhana lain yang tidak memerlukan teori baru.

Dengan batas ini, operasi baru K46 tetap tunggal:

> melihat bahwa keseluruhan jumlah fi'liyah mengisi slot khabar.

## Why `رابط` does not need a prior standalone K

A standalone K would be justified only if learner must perform a new general operation that is not already available. In clean K46 examples, learner already knows:
- pronoun recognition;
- verb–subject relation;
- basic hidden subject;
- local antecedent/reference relations from relative-clause work.

Thus the link can be **recognized using existing operations**, while K46 teaches the new syntactic embedding: clause-as-predicate.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
- explicit mubtada';
- short verbal clause as khabar;
- transparent explicit pronoun `رابط`;
- internal clause ≤ K45.

### PASS-B
- explicit mubtada';
- short verbal clause as khabar;
- simple hidden-subject link already covered by K41;
- no wider discourse inference.

### REVIEW-RABIT
- link exists but requires a new general rule or advanced discourse antecedence.

### REVIEW-BOUNDARY
- clause-as-khabar is plausible, but its end boundary is not locally obvious.

### PREMATURE
- nested clause;
- conditional relation;
- clausal predicate hosted by a nawāsikh construction whose specific integration is not yet taught;
- multiple ellipses;
- disputed parsing.

## Stress test against split proposal

### Split proposal
K46 = `رابط` in khabar clause  
K47 = verbal clause as khabar

### Rejection
This would over-fragment the ladder because `رابط` itself is not a single new structure here; it is realized by previously mastered pronoun/subject relations. The new operation is the **external syntactic function of the whole clause**.

Therefore do not split K46 merely because a link exists.

## Freeze recommendation

K46 can freeze with the following wording:

**K46 — mengenali jumlah fi'liyah sederhana sebagai khabar bagi mubtada' eksplisit, dengan `رابط` lokal yang seluruh mekanismenya telah dikuasai pada K sebelumnya.**

## Locked material

K46 does not unlock:
- full taxonomy of روابط الجملة الخبرية;
- nominal clause as khabar;
- clausal khabar under `إنّ/كان/ليس`;
- conditional clauses;
- advanced discourse reference.

## Decision

**PASS — no dependency reversal.**

`رابط` does not need its own prior K for the restricted core. Proceed to final gate K46, then rescan K47.
