# Clean Bank + Stress Test K52 — Transparent Nasb by Fatḥah v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K51 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

In a validated simple environment containing an explicit nāṣib, learner recognizes a familiar `فعل مضارع` whose transparent final fatḥah is the local effect of nasb.

The new operation is:

`explicit nāṣib → familiar mudhari‘ → visible fatḥah = nasb effect`.

## Core governor policy

K52 does **not** require broad mastery of `نواصب المضارع`. Clean-bank examples should begin with one or two maximally explicit governors whose function is stable in the admitted occurrence.

Governor diversity is a mastery-breadth issue, not a reason to bundle an entire particle inventory into the competency.

## PASS requirements

- nāṣib is overt;
- its governing relation is occurrence-specifically validated;
- target is a familiar sound-final mudhari‘;
- final fatḥah is pedagogically visible;
- no deletion of nūn;
- no hidden `أن`;
- no weak-final complication;
- no second new syntactic operation is required.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
Explicit governor + familiar sound-final mudhari‘ + transparent fatḥah.

### PASS-B
Additional context is cumulative-clean through K51 and does not obscure the governor→verb relation.

### REVIEW-GOV
Particle is overt, but identifying it as nāṣib requires a broader rule not yet available.

### REVIEW-MORPH
Nasb is grammatically present but its surface realization is not simple fatḥah.

### PREMATURE
- `أن` مقدرة;
- `حذف النون` as nasb sign;
- complex weak-final behavior;
- disputed governance;
- multiple new dependencies.

## Stress test 1 — Is this merely "spot a fatḥah"?

No. Fatḥah alone is insufficient. The learner must connect the ending to a validated governing environment and to a mudhari‘ target. This parallels K49's syntax→morphology reasoning while introducing a different mood relation.

## Stress test 2 — Must every nāṣib be learned first?

No. A competency may establish the morphological effect using a restricted set of explicit governors. Expanding the governor inventory can occur inside later mastery/application layers unless a governor introduces a genuinely new operation.

## Stress test 3 — Is nasb too similar to K49 jazm?

The meta-pattern is cumulative, but the grammatical mapping is new:
- K49: jussive environment → sukūn;
- K52: accusative/subjunctive environment → fatḥah.

The learner must classify a different governing relation and a different surface effect. Distinctness therefore passes.

## Stress test 4 — Should `حذف النون` for nasb be bundled here?

No. Although the same deletion surface may later occur under nasb, that requires transferring K51's inflectional pattern into a new governing environment. It should be tested separately as transfer/generalization rather than silently expanding K52.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH RESTRICTED EXPLICIT-GOVERNOR SCOPE.**

K52 is teachable and distinct if the initial bank privileges overt, stable nāṣib relations and transparent fatḥah.
