## E3 gate
E3 is not awarded for raw occurrence count. A competency must demonstrate:
1. independent Qur'anic occurrence diversity;
2. meaningful lexical/morphological/syntactic host diversity appropriate to the competency;
3. stable learner operation across occurrences;
4. explicit boundary or negative controls;
5. locality sufficient for assessment construction;
6. no dependence on a competency above the target operation.
