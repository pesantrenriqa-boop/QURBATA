# RESCAN K63 — Result Relation vs Omitted `العائد` vs Other Overt Discourse Relations v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE SELECTION  
**Baseline:** K1–K62 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K63 must add one new learner operation after K62 contrast/correction. It must not duplicate coordination, sequencing, relative attachment, or explicit-reference resolution.

## Candidate A — explicit result/consequence relation

Potential operation:

`proposition A → overt result marker → proposition B` and learner identifies B as consequence/result of A.

### Strengths
- discourse-semantic relation distinct from K62 contrast;
- highly useful for Qur'anic comprehension;
- can use two spans already analyzable through K62.

### Risks
- `ف` remains multifunctional;
- some result readings are inferential rather than directly encoded;
- danger of bundling marker disambiguation + semantic classification.

### Containment strategy
Only admit occurrences where:
- `ف` has already been independently validated as non-conditional and non-sequential for the target relation;
- the second proposition is an unambiguous consequence/result of the first;
- no additional hidden governor or ellipsis is needed.

### Verdict
**PROMISING BUT REQUIRES VERY TIGHT FILTERING.**

## Candidate B — omitted `العائد` in a maximally transparent subtype

Potential operation:

`اسم موصول + صلة ظاهرة + missing local complement position → infer one omitted resumptive linked to the relative antecedent`.

### Strengths
- direct progression from K61;
- adds controlled null-reference inference.

### Risks
- can require transitivity, valency, or advanced governance;
- omission rules vary by position;
- easily turns into an exception inventory.

### Verdict
**DEFER.** Still higher prerequisite cost than a clean overt discourse relation.

---

## Candidate C — explicit explanatory relation (`أي`/other clarifying marker)

Potential operation:

`proposition/expression A + overt clarification marker + proposition/expression B → identify B as explanation/specification of A`.

### Strengths
- overt marker;
- semantic relation distinct from contrast and sequence;
- potentially single-operation.

### Risks
- marker inventory and functions vary;
- clause-level evidence may be less uniform.

### Verdict
**SECONDARY CANDIDATE.** Needs corpus-yield confirmation.

---

## Candidate D — explicit alternative/choice relation (`أو`) at clause level

Potential operation:

`proposition A + أو + proposition B → recognize alternatives`.

### Strengths
- overt marker;
- low structural burden;
- clear semantic relation in clean cases.

### Risks
- `أو` has several Qur'anic readings (choice, permissibility, uncertainty relative to speaker framing, division, etc.);
- risks collapsing distinct semantics.

### Verdict
**DEFER AS BROAD K.** Could work only with a narrowly validated subtype.

# Comparative judgment

The best K63 architecture remains a **new overt discourse relation between already analyzable propositions**. Among candidates, RESULT is strongest if and only if marker disambiguation is pre-filtered outside the learner task.

## K63-CAND

> **Mengenali relasi hasil/akibat antara dua proposisi Qurani yang sudah dapat dianalisis, melalui marker eksplisit yang telah tervalidasi sebagai penanda consequence/result dalam occurrence tersebut.**

The competency is not “identify fā’.” The learner receives/works with an occurrence whose marker function has been cleanly established and must classify the proposition-level relation as result/consequence.

## One-operation rule

Learner operation:
1. identify proposition A;
2. identify proposition B;
3. identify the overt validated linker;
4. classify B as result/consequence of A.

The marker-function disambiguation itself is **not** a new learner operation inside core K63; ambiguous cases are excluded.

## Distinctness

- K58: coordination only;
- K59: temporal sequence;
- K62: contrast/correction;
- K63-CAND: consequence/result.

## Decision

**Advance K63-CAND to a strict marker-specific evidence/distinctness gate.**

Priority: validate whether a sufficiently clean Qur'anic result subset exists without forcing learners to solve multifunctional `ف` first. If yield is weak, fall back to an alternate overt discourse relation rather than lowering the gate.
