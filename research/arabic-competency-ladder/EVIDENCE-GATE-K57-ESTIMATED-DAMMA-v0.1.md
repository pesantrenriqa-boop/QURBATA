# Evidence Gate K57 — `الضمة المقدرة` on Weak-Final Mudhari‘ v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K56 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K57 target

Learner recognizes that a familiar weak-final `فعل مضارع` is **marfū‘** in a validated environment even though the ḍammah is not overtly visible, and identifies the ending as carrying a **locally estimated ḍammah (`ضمة مقدرة`)**.

Core reasoning:

`فعل مضارع معتل الآخر + بيئة رفع صحيحة + عدم ظهور الضمة → ضمة مقدرة`

## New learner operation

K57 introduces one inferential step beyond K55–K56:

> grammatical state can be valid even when its expected surface vowel is not overtly realized.

The learner must infer the hidden ending from the combination of:
1. known verb type;
2. validated raf‘ environment;
3. absence of an overt competing nasb/jazm effect.

## Core restriction

K57 does **not** teach the full theory of `الإعراب التقديري`.

Initial evidence should use one maximally transparent weak-final class only. Expansion to additional weak-final types is permitted later only if the learner operation remains unchanged.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — maximally transparent estimated raf‘
- familiar weak-final mudhari‘;
- local raf‘ environment is clear;
- no active nāṣib/jāzim;
- no visible ḍammah;
- one stable analysis of estimated ḍammah;
- no second new morphology lesson is required.

### PASS-B — same inference, broader lexical variety
A different familiar weak-final lexeme may enter if the same single inference remains sufficient.

### REVIEW-TYPE
The verb is weak-final, but identifying its class requires broader morphology not yet mastered.

### REVIEW-GOV
The estimated raf‘ analysis may be correct, but governor/environment validation is too complex for K57.

### REVIEW-CAUSE
Explaining *why* the ḍammah is not overt would require a full distinction such as `التعذر` vs `الثقل`; keep outside core K57 unless pedagogically unavoidable.

### PREMATURE
- disputed i‘rāb;
- multiple estimated elements;
- noun-based i‘rāb taqdiri;
- hidden governor plus hidden ending in the same item;
- complex weak morphology;
- full traditional taxonomy of estimated case/mood endings.

## Distinctness from K55

K55:
`raf‘ → overt ḍammah`.

K57:
`raf‘ → no overt ḍammah → infer estimated ḍammah`.

The new competence is not another visible sign; it is **licensed grammatical inference from non-realization**.

## Distinctness from K50/K53

K50 reconstructs a **deleted lexical weak segment** under jazm.

K53 reconstructs a **hidden governor (`أن`)** under nasb.

K57 reconstructs neither a lexical segment nor a governor. It infers an **unrealized inflectional vowel** while the word itself remains overt.

Therefore the representational target is distinct.

## Anti-overload policy

At K57 the learner should be able to answer:

`ما حالة الفعل المضارع؟ وهل الضمة ظاهرة أم مقدرة؟`

The learner does **not** yet need to explain every phonological reason for estimation, enumerate all places of تقدير, or produce a complete weak-verb paradigm.

## Freeze criteria

K57 may freeze only if:
1. clean Qur'anic occurrences provide a stable one-step inference;
2. the verb class is already familiar or supplied in controlled comparison;
3. raf‘ environment is independently clear;
4. no hidden governor is simultaneously required;
5. no full `التعذر/الثقل` taxonomy is necessary for the task;
6. examples can remain cumulative-clean through K56.
