# Distinctness + Evidence Gate K59 — `ثم` vs `ف` for Clause Sequencing v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — DISTINCTNESS/EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K58 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan marker sequencing yang paling bersih untuk K59 setelah K58 memperkenalkan koordinasi antarklausa secara umum.

Candidate operation:

> learner recognizes that two coordinated clauses are not merely linked, but are ordered in event sequence by the coordinator.

---

## Candidate A — `ثم`

### Core interpretation
`ثم` links two clauses and marks ordered sequence with an interval/delay relation.

### Dependency burden
Low–moderate:
- clause boundaries already available from K58;
- overt connector;
- sequencing meaning is structurally salient;
- fewer competing high-frequency syntactic functions than `ف`.

### Risk
Teaching the full semantic theory of تراخي would overburden the K. Core K59 only needs recognition of **ordered-after relation**, with “there is an interval” treated as controlled semantic refinement.

**Judgement: strongest K59 core candidate.**

---

## Candidate B — `ف`

### Core interpretation
`ف` may encode tight sequencing, consequence, linkage, جواب markers, or other functions depending on context.

### Dependency burden
Higher:
- function disambiguation is harder;
- K48 already uses `فاء جواب الشرط`;
- a new clause-sequencing use risks cross-function confusion;
- many occurrences need semantic/contextual analysis beyond a single local operation.

**Judgement: defer as transfer/contrast evidence after `ثم`, not K59 core.**

---

## Distinctness from K58

K58 asks:
`Are these two clauses coordinated by an overt connector?`

K59 must ask:
`Does the connector impose an ordered temporal/event relation between the clauses?`

Thus K59 adds **relation-type classification** on top of already recognized coordination.

## Anti-inventory rule

K59 is not “learn the next conjunction.” It survives only if the learner must interpret a new relation:

`clause 1 → then clause 2`.

Therefore `ثم` is selected because its sequencing contribution is clearer and less polyfunctional than `ف`.

## Core K59 provisional definition

> **K59 — Mengenali bahwa `ثم` yang tervalidasi menghubungkan dua klausa Qurani dalam urutan kejadian, sehingga klausa kedua dipahami terjadi setelah klausa pertama.**

## Scope limits

Do not require:
- full semantic taxonomy of `ثم`;
- quantitative measurement of delay;
- discourse-rhetorical uses where temporal sequencing is not transparent;
- comparison with every function of `ف`;
- nested clause chains;
- ellipsis across coordinated clauses.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
Two clear clauses + overt `ثم` + transparent event sequencing.

### PASS-B
Internal clause complexity is ≤ K58 and sequencing remains locally obvious.

### REVIEW-SEM
`ثم` is overt but the relation is rhetorical/discourse-heavy rather than straightforward event sequence.

### REVIEW-BOUNDARY
Clause spans are not clean enough for first-stage sequencing analysis.

### PREMATURE
- nested chains;
- multiple connectors requiring hierarchy;
- disputed semantic relation;
- ellipsis essential to interpretation.

## Assessment target

`أين الجملة الأولى؟ وأين الجملة الثانية؟ وماذا يفيد ثم بينهما؟`

Expected core answer: second clause occurs after the first in sequence.

## Decision

**Select `ثم` as K59 core. Defer `ف` sequencing to later transfer/contrast evidence.**
