# Clean Bank + Stress Test K60 — Simple `صلة الموصول` Boundaries v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K59 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

Learner identifies an overt familiar `اسم موصول`, determines the beginning and end of the following clause, and classifies that clause as `صلة الموصول`.

Core representation:

`[اسم موصول] + [جملة صلة الموصول]`

The new operation is **relative-clause attachment + boundary recognition**, not recognition of the relative word itself.

## PASS requirements

- `اسم موصول` is overt and familiar;
- the following `صلة` is a complete clause analyzable through ≤ K59;
- start and end of the `صلة` are locally clear;
- no nested relative clause;
- no disputed attachment;
- no advanced `العائد` analysis is required to identify the clause span;
- no omitted relative marker;
- no simultaneous new morphology.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — short verbal ṣilah
Overt relative marker + short verbal clause with transparent boundary.

### PASS-B — short nominal ṣilah
Overt relative marker + short nominal clause with transparent boundary and no new internal operation.

### REVIEW-RETURN
Boundary is clear, but solving the item would require explicit analysis of a complex or omitted `العائد`.

### REVIEW-SPAN
The relative clause is grammatically valid but too long or internally nested for core K60.

### PREMATURE
- nested relative structures;
- ambiguous clause ending;
- disputed relative attachment;
- hidden/complex resumptive dependencies;
- full relative-pronoun paradigm required.

## Stress test 1 — Is K60 merely `spot الذي/الذين`?

No. An item is not solved until the learner marks the `صلة` span and attaches it to the relative marker. Lexical recognition of `اسم موصول` alone is insufficient.

## Stress test 2 — Must `العائد` be mastered first?

No for core K60. The learner can identify the relative clause boundary in carefully selected occurrences without naming or fully analyzing every resumptive relation. Examples that require advanced `العائد` reasoning are withheld.

## Stress test 3 — Distinctness from K58/K59

K58 and K59 relate two clauses to each other. K60 instead relates an **overt nominal relative marker** to a clause that completes its reference. The dependency is asymmetric and local.

## Stress test 4 — Should each `اسم موصول` get its own K?

No. `الذي`, `الذين`, and other overt familiar forms are lexical/inflectional variants. They remain within one competency as long as the learner operation is unchanged.

## Assessment design

Core prompt:

`أين الاسم الموصول؟ وأين تبدأ صلة الموصول وأين تنتهي؟`

Expected response identifies the relative marker and the full `صلة` span.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH BOUNDARY-FIRST SCOPE.**

K60 is distinct, cumulative, and teachable without importing the full theory of `العائد` or the complete `الأسماء الموصولة` inventory.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K60 — Mengenali `صلة الموصول` sebagai klausa yang melekat pada `اسم موصول` eksplisit, dengan menentukan batas awal dan akhirnya dalam konstruksi Qurani sederhana.**
