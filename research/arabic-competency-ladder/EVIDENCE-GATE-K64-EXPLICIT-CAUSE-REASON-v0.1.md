# EVIDENCE GATE K64 — Explicit Cause / Reason Relation v0.1

**Status:** ACTIVE EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K63 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Candidate

> **K64-CAND — Mengenali relasi sebab/alasan antara dua proposisi Qurani melalui marker eksplisit yang tervalidasi, lalu menentukan proposisi mana yang menjadi alasan bagi proposisi lainnya.**

## Core operation

The learner identifies a directed explanatory relation:

`Proposition A ← REASON/CAUSE ← Proposition B`

In pedagogical wording:
- what is being explained/justified?
- which proposition supplies the reason?

## Why this is not K63 reversed mechanically

K63 asks the learner to identify a proposition presented as a **result/consequence**.

K64 asks the learner to identify a proposition presented as a **reason/grounds/explanation** for another proposition.

The two can describe related real-world events, but the discourse task and direction of interpretation differ. K64 therefore passes only where the reason relation is linguistically/discoursally encoded, not merely inferable from world knowledge.

## PASS requirements

1. both spans are analyzable using ≤ K63;
2. an overt marker or construction signals reason/explanation;
3. marker function is validated before inclusion in the clean bank;
4. learner can identify the explained proposition and the reason proposition;
5. no purpose reading competes strongly;
6. no condition→response analysis is required as the new operation;
7. no hidden `أن` or new mood rule is needed;
8. no tafsir-only causal inference is required.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — overt reason relation
Two overt proposition spans and an overt, validated reason/explanatory linker.

### PASS-B — lexicalized explanatory construction
A stable Qur'anic construction whose explanatory function is overt enough that the learner performs relation recognition rather than speculative inference.

### REVIEW-PURPOSE
Reason and purpose readings are difficult to separate without later semantic/syntactic knowledge.

### REVIEW-MARKER
The marker is multifunctional and must itself be newly disambiguated.

### REVIEW-DISCOURSE
The reason relation is plausible but depends on a long discourse span or tafsir-level interpretation.

### PREMATURE
- implicit causal inference without an overt signal;
- hidden purpose/nasb machinery;
- multiple competing reasons;
- rhetorical causality requiring advanced balaghah;
- simultaneous ellipsis reconstruction.

## Distinctness tests

### vs K63 result/consequence
K63: `A → RESULT B`.

K64: `A ← REASON B`.

Assessment must test which proposition functions as the reason, not merely whether the two propositions are causally related.

### vs K47/K48 conditional structure
Condition→response is a grammatical dependency. K64 requires an explanatory/reason relation that can be identified independently of conditional architecture.

### vs purpose
Cause/reason answers approximately **“why is/was this so?”** by supplying grounds already relevant to the proposition. Purpose answers **“for what end?”** and is therefore held for a separate future operation if corpus evidence supports it.

## Marker policy

K64 is relation-based, not particle-based. No individual particle automatically defines the competence. Only occurrence-level validated reason/explanatory functions enter the bank.

## Assessment prompt

`أيُّ المعنيين هو المعلَّل؟ وأيُّهما يبيّن السبب أو العلة؟`

Simplified Indonesian support:

`Pernyataan mana yang dijelaskan, dan mana yang menjadi sebab/alasan?`

Expected response identifies both roles and points to the overt validated signal.
