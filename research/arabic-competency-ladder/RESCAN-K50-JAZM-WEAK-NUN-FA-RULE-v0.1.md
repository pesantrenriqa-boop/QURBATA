# Rescan K50 — Jazm Weak-Letter Deletion vs Nūn Deletion vs Fā’ Rule Generalization v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K49 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan operasi baru paling ringan sesudah K49, tanpa menggabungkan seluruh علامات الجزم dalam satu level dan tanpa memecah tangga hanya berdasarkan daftar kaidah tradisional.

---

## Candidate A — Jazm by `حذف حرف العلة`

### Foundation
- K47: simple conditional relation;
- K49: concept that a jussive syntactic environment can alter the end of a mudhari‘ verb.

### New operation
Recognize that a weak-final mudhari‘ in a validated jussive environment shows jazm through deletion of its final weak letter.

### Dependency burden
Moderate:
- requires prior recognition of the weak-final base form;
- learner must compare the observed form with a familiar non-jussive/base form;
- involves one deletion/reconstruction operation;
- no person-paradigm reasoning is inherently required.

### Main risk
Qur'anic orthography and phonology may obscure whether the deleted weak letter is pedagogically transparent. Evidence must be restricted to stable, clear occurrences.

**Judgement: HIGH candidate.**

---

## Candidate B — Jazm by `حذف النون`

### Foundation
Same syntactic foundation as K49.

### New operation
Recognize jazm in the so-called five-verbs pattern through deletion of nūn.

### Dependency burden
Higher:
- presupposes recognition of the relevant person/number suffix pattern;
- requires distinguishing the stable suffixal material from the deleted nūn;
- risks opening a full `الأفعال الخمسة` paradigm lesson.

**Judgement: DISTINCT but heavier than weak-letter deletion.**

---

## Candidate C — Rule-level generalization for when `جواب الشرط` takes `فـ`

### Foundation
- K47 identifies condition and response;
- K48 identifies overt `فاء جواب الشرط` occurrence-specifically.

### New operation
Generalize across response types to predict/justify when the response is introduced by fā’.

### Dependency burden
High:
- requires categorizing multiple kinds of response clauses;
- mixes nominal, verbal, requestive, frozen, negative/future constructions, etc.;
- shifts from local recognition to rule taxonomy.

**Judgement: pedagogically meaningful, but much heavier and broader.**

---

## Head-to-head result

**K50-CAND — jazm by `حذف حرف العلة` in a tightly restricted weak-final mudhari‘ occurrence.**

Why before `حذف النون`:
1. K49 has already introduced syntax→morphology effect;
2. weak-letter deletion requires one local reconstruction rather than a broader person/number paradigm;
3. it extends the concept of jazm incrementally;
4. it can remain atomic if evidence is restricted to transparent weak-final verbs.

## K50 provisional definition

Learner can recognize that a familiar weak-final `فعل مضارع` in a validated simple jussive environment shows jazm through **deletion of the expected final weak letter**, and can relate the observed form to its familiar non-jussive/base form.

## Hard exclusions

K50 does not teach:
- all weak-verb classes;
- full morphology of ناقص verbs;
- `حذف النون`;
- non-conditional jazm systems;
- orthographic disputes;
- advanced phonological explanation;
- complete i‘rāb theory.

## Evidence classes for next gate

- **PASS-A:** familiar weak-final mudhari‘; one transparent deleted weak letter; simple K47-compatible environment.
- **PASS-B:** cumulative-clean extra material only.
- **REVIEW-ORTH:** deletion analysis is grammatically plausible but Qur'anic orthography is not beginner-transparent.
- **REVIEW-BASE:** base/non-jussive form is not sufficiently established for the learner.
- **PREMATURE:** multiple morphological operations, disputed reconstruction, weak-letter + suffix complexity, or K51+ dependency.

## Decision

Open K50 evidence gate for `حذف حرف العلة`. Keep `حذف النون` and fā’-rule generalization as later candidates pending another rescan after K50.
