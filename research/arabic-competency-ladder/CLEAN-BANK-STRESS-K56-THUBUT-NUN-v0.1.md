# Clean Bank + Stress Test K56 — `ثبوت النون` as Positive Raf‘ Evidence v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K55 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

Learner recognizes a familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ pattern in a validated raf‘ environment and identifies **retention of the inflectional nūn (`ثبوت النون`)** as positive evidence of raf‘.

Core reasoning:

`pola mudhari‘ nūn-bearing yang familiar + lingkungan raf‘ → النون ثابتة → علامة الرفع`

## Positive-evidence requirement

K56 is not solved by merely saying “the nūn was not deleted.” The learner must classify the retained nūn as a **morphological sign of raf‘** in the relevant pattern.

## PASS requirements

- target mudhari‘ pattern is already familiar;
- its nūn is clearly inflectional and overt;
- local environment is validated as raf‘;
- no active nāṣib/jāzim governs the target;
- no additional morphology is needed to identify the nūn-bearing pattern;
- no disputed parsing;
- surrounding syntax is cumulative-clean ≤ K55.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — direct positive retention
Familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ + clear raf‘ environment + overt retained nūn.

### PASS-B — controlled contrast
The occurrence is paired with a familiar K51-style deletion form so the learner can distinguish:
- raf‘ → nūn retained;
- jazm → nūn deleted.

The contrast supports learning but is not required in every assessment item.

### REVIEW-BASE
Nūn is visible, but recognizing it as the relevant inflectional nūn requires an unmastered paradigm.

### REVIEW-GOV
The form is plausibly marfū‘, but the local governing environment is not sufficiently transparent.

### PREMATURE
- full `الأفعال الخمسة` production required;
- complex person/number derivation required;
- ambiguous nūn identity;
- multiple simultaneous morphology operations;
- advanced orthographic complication.

## Stress test 1 — Distinctness from K51

K51 asks the learner to detect **absence** of an expected inflectional nūn under jazm.

K56 asks the learner to interpret **presence/retention** of that nūn as a positive raf‘ sign.

The surface evidence and classification direction are opposite. K56 therefore adds a distinct morphological judgment rather than merely reversing K51 verbally.

## Stress test 2 — Distinctness from K55

K55 uses overt final ḍammah on a sound-final mudhari‘.

K56 uses retained nūn on a different inflectional pattern. Thus the grammatical state is shared (raf‘), but the **surface realization and learner evidence** differ.

## Stress test 3 — Does K56 require full `الأفعال الخمسة`?

No. As with K51, the core competence can be occurrence-specific and pattern-recognition based. Full enumeration, complete conjugation tables, and production across all five traditional forms remain outside K56.

## Anti-fragmentation

Do not assign separate K numbers to each person/number realization. They are internal variants when the learner operation remains:

`known nūn-bearing pattern → retained nūn → raf‘`.

## Assessment design

Core prompt:

`ما الفعل المضارع؟ وما علامة رفعه هنا؟`

Expected response identifies `ثبوت النون` as the positive raf‘ sign. A contrast prompt may additionally ask why the nūn remains here but disappears in a validated jazm occurrence.

## Verdict

**PASS.**

K56 is distinct from both K51 and K55, preserves the one-operation rule, and does not require importing the full five-verb paradigm.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K56 — Mengenali raf‘ melalui `ثبوت النون` pada pola fi‘il mudhari‘ nūn-bearing yang sudah familiar dan tervalidasi.**
