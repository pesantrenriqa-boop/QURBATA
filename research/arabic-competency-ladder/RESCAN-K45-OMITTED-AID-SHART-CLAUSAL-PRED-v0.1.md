# Rescan K45 — Omitted `عائد` vs Simple Shart vs Clausal Predicate v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K44 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Mencari K45 sebagai operasi belajar paling ringan yang benar-benar distinct setelah K44. Kandidat diuji berdasarkan dependency, bukan urutan bab nahwu.

---

## Candidate A — omitted/estimated `عائد` dasar

### Foundation
- K42: identify isim maushul + silah boundary;
- K43: identify explicit `عائد` inside simple silah.

### New operation
Infer that the relative construction contains a resumptive relation even when the returning element is not overt on the surface.

### Dependency burden
Moderate–high:
- requires reconstruction;
- learner must know the expected syntactic slot;
- deletion patterns must not become a full taxonomy;
- occurrence must be locally recoverable without disputed analysis.

### Minimal possible scope
Only cases where:
- maushul and silah boundary are clear;
- the missing relation corresponds to an already-mastered simple role;
- no nested clause;
- no conditional relation;
- no advanced ellipsis beyond the single resumptive slot;
- analysis is stable enough for pedagogical use.

**Judgement: HIGH candidate because it directly extends K42–K43, but evidence gate must be strict.**

---

## Candidate B — simple conditional relation

### New operation
Identify a condition marker, delimit the condition clause, and link it to a simple response/result clause.

### Dependency burden
High:
- two-clause linkage;
- particle classification;
- protasis/apodosis boundary;
- possible jawab markers;
- verbal mood/jazm may become structurally relevant.

A token-recognition-only competence for `إنْ` is rejected as insufficiently meaningful.

**Judgement: DISTINCT, but heavier than one-slot resumptive reconstruction.**

---

## Candidate C — clausal khabar / predicate expansion

### New operation
Recognize that a khabar position in an already-known nominal/nawāsikh construction can be filled by a clause rather than a single noun/adjective/PP.

### Dependency burden
Moderate–high:
- clause boundary;
- internal clause analysis;
- often a linking pronoun is involved;
- risks mixing several predicate types into one K.

This may later need separate competencies by host construction (`مبتدأ`, `إنّ`, `كان`, `ليس`) or a general clause-as-predicate abstraction after sufficient examples.

**Judgement: IMPORTANT but not yet atomic enough for K45.**

---

## Head-to-head result

**K45-CAND — omitted/estimated `عائد` in a tightly restricted simple relative construction.**

Reason:
1. direct continuation from K42 boundary and K43 explicit resumptive;
2. adds exactly one new cognitive operation: recover a locally licensed missing resumptive relation;
3. does not yet require linking two independent clauses as conditional structure does;
4. can be kept atomic if deletion theory is excluded.

## K45 provisional definition

Learner can detect, in a validated simple `اسم موصول + صلة` occurrence, that an expected `عائد` relation is **not overtly expressed** and recover only its basic local syntactic slot.

## Hard exclusions

K45 does not teach:
- full conditions of حذف العائد;
- disputed grammatical reconstructions;
- multiple omitted elements;
- nested relative clauses;
- advanced ellipsis;
- full i‘rab reconstruction;
- conditional linkage.

## Evidence classes for next gate

- **PASS-A:** one clear omitted resumptive slot; all visible grammar ≤ K44.
- **PASS-B:** cumulative-clean extra material only.
- **REVIEW-ROLE:** omission is clear but recovered syntactic role exceeds prior scope.
- **REVIEW-ANALYSIS:** more than one plausible grammatical reconstruction.
- **PREMATURE:** nested clause, multiple ellipses, conditional relation, or other new structural operation.

## Decision

Open K45 evidence gate for **basic omitted `عائد`**, but do not freeze unless occurrence-level evidence proves that the operation can be taught without importing advanced ellipsis theory.

If clean yield fails, K45 must be reconsidered rather than forcing this sequence.
