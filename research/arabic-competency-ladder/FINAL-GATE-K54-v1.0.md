# FINAL GATE K54 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K53 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K54 — Memprediksi kebutuhan `فاء جواب الشرط` ketika jawab syarth yang tervalidasi berbentuk jumlah ismiyah, lalu memverifikasi marker tersebut pada occurrence Qurani.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated simple conditional occurrence, learner can:
1. identify the response as a nominal clause;
2. predict that this response configuration requires `فاء جواب الشرط`;
3. locate and verify the overt fā’ in the occurrence.

## Included

- simple conditional structure;
- clearly nominal response;
- overt validated `فاء جواب الشرط`;
- prediction before verification;
- cumulative analysis through K53.

## Excluded

- memorization of the full traditional list of جواب types requiring fā’;
- automatic inclusion of طلبية / `لن` / `قد` / `سوف` / جامد and other categories;
- omitted jawab;
- oath–condition competition;
- nested conditionals;
- disputed response classification.

## Assessment prompt

`ما نوع جواب الشرط؟ هل تتوقع دخول الفاء عليه؟ ثم تحقق من النص.`

Expected core reasoning: nominal response → predict fā’ → verify overt marker.

## Gate results

- distinctness from K48: PASS;
- predictive/generative operation: PASS;
- anti-list: PASS;
- single-type scope: PASS;
- cumulative dependency: PASS.

**Decision: K54 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

## Architectural significance

K54 introduces a new direction of reasoning. Earlier competencies primarily move from observed form to analysis; K54 asks the learner to use a structural classification to **predict an expected surface marker** and then verify it in Qur'anic evidence.
