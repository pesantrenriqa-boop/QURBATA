## K16 — validated conjunction recognition

Canonical anchor:
- `دَاوُدَ وَسُلَيْمَانَ` — 27:15 (`و`)

Family target remains recognition of validated conjunction tokens. `و` is strongly evidenced. `فـ` and `ثم` require occurrence-level classification because the same surface markers can carry discourse, response, consequence, or other functions later in the ladder.

Candidate family policy:
- `و`: clean recognition evidence may be promoted broadly.
- `فـ`: only count after excluding جواب الشرط / consequence / other specialized functions.
- `ثم`: only count as K16 recognition when the operation is marker recognition; temporal sequencing interpretation belongs to K59.

**Decision:** retain K16 at E1/E2-candidate until at least one clean normalized `فـ` occurrence and one clean normalized `ثم` occurrence pass the exclusion gate. No premature promotion.
