# Clean Bank + Stress Test K65 — `إلا` Exception / Restriction v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K64 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

Learner identifies the relevant discourse/domain scope and the element singled out by overt `إلا`, without needing the full traditional taxonomy of exception constructions.

Core relation:

`[DOMAIN] + إلا + [EXCLUDED / RESTRICTED ELEMENT]`

## PASS requirements

- overt `إلا`;
- local domain/scope is recoverable;
- excluded/restricted element is overt;
- no new post-`إلا` case-rule decision is required;
- no need to classify the construction as `تام/ناقص` or `موجب/غير موجب` to solve the item;
- no nested exclusions;
- surrounding syntax is analyzable ≤ K64.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — transparent exclusion
General domain + overt `إلا` + clear excluded member.

### PASS-B — transparent restriction
The construction narrows a general proposition/domain to a singled-out member or set, with overt and local scope.

### REVIEW-I‘RAB
The exception relation is clear, but solving the item would require a new inflection rule after `إلا`.

### REVIEW-SCOPE
The marker is overt but the scope/domain is too distant or ambiguous.

### PREMATURE
- implicit exception without overt `إلا`;
- advanced idiomatic uses not reducible to exception/restriction;
- disputed scope;
- multiple exclusions;
- full exception paradigm required.

## Stress test 1 — Is K65 just vocabulary recognition?

No. Translating `إلا` as “except” is insufficient. The learner must identify **what set/proposition is being restricted** and **which element is singled out**.

## Stress test 2 — Distinctness from K62 contrast

K62 recognizes corrective/contrastive relation between propositions.

K65 recognizes set/scope restriction with a marked excluded or singled-out element. The learner operation is domain→exception mapping, not proposition-to-proposition correction.

## Stress test 3 — Distinctness from negation

Negative contexts may co-occur, but negation itself is not K65. The target operation is how `إلا` changes inclusion within a scope.

## Stress test 4 — Must traditional exception subtypes be taught first?

No. Core K65 can be occurrence-specific. Traditional subtype labels are later mastery depth unless needed for a separate learner operation.

## Stress test 5 — Does every `إلا` occurrence qualify?

No. Surface `إلا` alone is insufficient. The occurrence must support a clean, local exception/restriction analysis without importing later i‘rāb theory.

## Assessment design

Core prompt:

`ما المجال العام؟ وما الذي خصصته أو استثنته «إلا»؟`

Expected response identifies the domain and the excluded/restricted element.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH STRICT SCOPE FILTER.**

K65 is independently assessable as a scope-restriction operation and does not require teaching the full `باب الاستثناء`.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K65 — Mengenali relasi pengecualian/pembatasan melalui `إلا` eksplisit dengan menentukan domain yang dibatasi dan unsur yang dikecualikan/dikhususkan dalam konstruksi Qurani terkontrol.**
