**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE

## Purpose

Menentukan operasi baru paling ringan setelah K55 tanpa sekadar mengikuti urutan التقليدي لعلامات الرفع.

---

## Candidate A — Raf‘ via `ثبوت النون`

### Foundation
- K51: deletion of inflectional nūn under jazm;
- K52: explicit nasb environment;
- K55: transparent raf‘ by ḍammah.

### New operation
Recognize that in a familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ pattern, **retention of the inflectional nūn** is positive evidence of raf‘.

### Dependency burden
Moderate but controlled:
- base nūn-bearing form must already be familiar;
- learner already knows that the same nūn can disappear under jazm from K51;
- no hidden segment needs reconstruction;
- recognition is positive rather than purely default.

### Distinctness risk
Could be dismissed as mere “absence of deletion.” To count as a K, learner must actively use the retained nūn as a morphological sign of raf‘ and contrast it with a familiar deleted form.

**Judgement: HIGH candidate if contrastive evidence is strong.**

---

## Candidate B — Raf‘ via `الضمة المقدرة`

### New operation
Infer raf‘ on a weak-final mudhari‘ where the ḍammah is not overtly realized.

### Dependency burden
High:
- invisible ending;
- phonological/morphological blocking explanation;
- weak-final classification;
- risk of conflating several reasons for estimated endings.

**Judgement: important but heavier than overt nūn retention.**

## Candidate C — Mood contrast/generalization

Learner compares raf‘/nasb/jazm surface signs across known examples.

### Distinctness result
This is a consolidation/mastery task based on K49–K55, not a genuinely new linguistic operation.

**Judgement: do not create K56 for summary contrast.**

## Candidate D — Additional predictive `فاء جواب الشرط` response type

After K54, moving to another traditional response category risks list inflation. Unless a new generative abstraction is demonstrated, this should remain transfer/application breadth.

**Judgement: not preferred for K56.**

## Head-to-head result

**K56-CAND — raf‘ via `ثبوت النون` on a familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ pattern.**

Reason:
1. overt positive evidence;
2. directly contrasts with K51 deletion;
3. lower inferential burden than estimated ḍammah;
4. independently assessable if base-form familiarity is controlled;
5. does not require a full `الأفعال الخمسة` paradigm.

## Provisional definition

Learner can recognize a familiar nūn-bearing mudhari‘ in a validated raf‘ environment and identify **the retained inflectional nūn (`ثبوت النون`)** as the local sign of raf‘.

## Hard exclusions

K56 does not teach:
- full `الأفعال الخمسة` paradigm;
- complete person/number morphology;
- nasb/jazm deletion tables beyond already-frozen competencies;
- estimated ḍammah;
- weak-final raf‘;
- full i‘rāb production.

## Evidence gate requirement

Freeze only if:
- contrast with K51 is pedagogically clean;
- nūn-bearing base is already familiar or controlled;
- retained nūn is truly diagnostic in the admitted occurrence;
- no new person/number derivation is required;
- surrounding syntax is ≤ K55.

## Next

Open K56 evidence gate for `ثبوت النون`. If distinctness collapses into simple transfer, reject K56 and move to the next corpus-driven operation.
