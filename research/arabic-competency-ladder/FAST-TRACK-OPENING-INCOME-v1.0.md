# FAST TRACK — OPENING & INCOME v1.0

**Status:** ACTIVE EXECUTION PRIORITY  
**Goal:** open usable RIQA/QURBATA program and begin revenue without waiting for full research perfection.  
**Principle:** production launch readiness and research completion are separate tracks.

## A. Immediate rule

Do not block opening on:
- full psychometric validation;
- complete evidence maturity for every K;
- perfect 180-item production bank;
- full 21-level automation;
- all advanced branches/features.

Do block opening on:
- unsafe authentication/data exposure;
- broken payment/registration flow;
- no usable participant record;
- no minimal learning/placement path;
- no operator/admin recovery path;
- claims of validated assessment that are not yet validated.

## B. Opening MVP

Minimum opening package:
1. landing/registration;
2. participant account/profile;
3. program selection;
4. payment status integration/manual fallback;
5. placement-lite using reviewed low-ambiguity items only;
6. assignment to starting level/program;
7. teacher/admin dashboard minimum;
8. attendance/progress recording;
9. certificate/report placeholder only when eligibility is verified;
10. audit/log + manual correction path.

## C. Revenue-first program order

### Wave 1 — can launch first
- QURBATA classes / institutional adoption;
- Tahsin / placement / tashih services;
- Akademi Trainer / teacher upgrade cohorts;
- paid non-regular online/offline classes.

### Wave 2 — connect after opening
- Diniyah;
- General Subjects;
- Academy 21 levels full routing;
- Umrah and bonus modules;
- advanced reporting/certification.

## D. Placement policy for opening

Opening does not use the entire research bank automatically.

Use only:
- LOW ambiguity items;
- canonical-aligned targets;
- reviewed prompts/expected responses;
- manual reviewer override;
- provisional placement labels, not final psychometric claims.

Production flag remains false for unreviewed items.

## E. Parallel lanes

### Lane 1 — OPENING/INCOME (highest priority)
- registration;
- payment;
- participant DB;
- minimal placement;
- class assignment;
- admin/teacher operations;
- launch monitoring.

### Lane 2 — CONTENT/QUALITY
- canonical registry;
- item review;
- QURBATA mapping;
- curriculum refinement;
- content production.

### Lane 3 — VALIDATION/RESEARCH
- evidence maturity;
- pilot data;
- item statistics;
- cut scores;
- final freeze.

Lane 2 and Lane 3 may not block Lane 1 unless a safety, integrity, or legal requirement is implicated.

## G. Governance

Opening-ready does not mean research-complete.

Allowed launch statement:
> Program is operational with provisional placement and controlled human review while research validation continues.

Disallowed statement before validation:
> Placement/cut scores are fully psychometrically validated.

## H. Success condition

The program is considered OPENING-READY when a real participant can:

`register → select program → payment confirmed/manual verified → receive placement/start level → join class → attendance/progress recorded → admin can correct exceptions`

without developer intervention for the normal path.
