## Boundary rules
1. No S5 item may be scored from translation intuition alone.
2. Morphological evidence and syntactic dependency evidence do not automatically prove a discourse-semantic relation.
3. K57, K60, K62, K64 and K65 require uncertainty/fallback handling when the target is not uniquely recoverable.
4. K65 must sample multiple prerequisites rather than functioning as a single-feature question.
5. Higher-stage placement items should be sparse and information-rich; failure must route to prerequisite diagnostics rather than simply assigning a low global level.

## Level design constraints
- K is not equal to a level.
- A level may contain multiple compatible competencies.
- A difficult competency may require more than one instructional encounter without becoming multiple canonical K items.
- Placement may skip instructional levels only when mastery evidence for their prerequisite K set is observed.
- QURBATA pages/meetings are delivery units and must remain downstream of the competency and level architecture.
