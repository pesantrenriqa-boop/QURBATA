# Clean Bank + Stress Test K48 — `فاء جواب الشرط` v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K47 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K48 operation under test

Within a simple conditional construction already analyzable by K47, learner identifies an overt **`فـ` functioning specifically as `فاء جواب الشرط`** and uses it to mark the beginning of the response clause.

The new operation is **functional disambiguation + response-boundary marking**, not recognition of the letter `ف`.

## Clean-bank admission rules

PASS only when:
- conditional relation is already clear under K47;
- `فـ` occurs at the response boundary;
- its function as `فاء جواب الشرط` is stable for the occurrence;
- no oath/conditional competition is required;
- no omitted response reconstruction;
- internal grammar of both clauses is ≤ K47 or can be ignored without blocking K48 identification.

## Stress contrasts

K48 must distinguish the target from superficially similar `فـ`:

1. **عطف / sequencing fā’** — joins events/constituents but is not the conditional-response marker.
2. **سببية or discourse fā’** — semantic consequence alone is insufficient; the occurrence must sit in validated conditional syntax.
3. **فاء جواب الشرط** — opens the response in the relevant conditional configuration.

Thus a token-initial `فـ` is never enough evidence by itself.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
Clear K47 condition; overt target fā’; response begins immediately after it; no new operation.

### PASS-B
Additional cumulative-clean material is present, but target function remains locally transparent.

### REVIEW-FA
Fā’ is overt but its function cannot be securely separated from another fā’ function without later theory.

### REVIEW-JAWAB-TYPE
The response type may explain why fā’ appears, but doing so would require teaching the full obligatory-fā’ rule set. Keep outside core K48.

### PREMATURE
- oath + condition interaction;
- omitted condition/response;
- nested conditionals;
- disputed response boundary;
- advanced discourse reconstruction.

## Distinctness stress result

K48 survives the anti-inventory test because assessment is relational:

> Given a validated conditional construction containing one or more `فـ`, can the learner identify which `فـ` marks the beginning of the جواب الشرط?

This cannot be solved by merely knowing the grapheme or generic conjunction meaning of `فـ`.

## Jazm dependency test

Full jazm analysis is **not a prerequisite** for K48. The learner can locate the condition and response from K47 and identify the overt response marker without explaining all mood changes in the conditional verbs.

Therefore jazm remains a separate frontier.

## Rule-theory boundary

K48 core teaches **recognition in validated occurrences**. It does not yet require memorizing the complete traditional list of response types that require fā’ (`الجملة الاسمية`, طلبية, جامدة, etc.). That explanatory generalization may be introduced only after evidence shows it deserves a separate operation/layer.

## Stress-test verdict

**PASS — K48 is independently assessable and cumulative-clean under strict occurrence validation.**

## Freeze recommendation

Freeze K48 as:

> **K48 — Mengenali `فاء جواب الشرط` yang eksplisit pada struktur syarth sederhana dan menggunakannya untuk menentukan awal jawab syarth.**

## Next frontier

After freeze, rescan K49 among:

- other corpus-discovered local operations.

Do not automatically assign K49 to jazm without a dependency/distinctness comparison.
