## K30 — prohibitive `لا` recognition

**Decision: PROMOTE TO E2.**

Clean positive occurrences:
- QS 2:11 `لَا تُفْسِدُوا` — `لا` is explicitly a prohibition particle; the following imperfect `تفسدوا` is jussive.
- QS 2:22 `فَلَا تَجْعَلُوا` — `لا` is prohibition; following imperfect is governed as negative imperative.
- QS 2:222 `وَلَا تَقْرَبُوهُنَّ` — prohibition particle with jussive imperfect.

Negative control:
- QS 2:12 `لَا يَشْعُرُونَ` — `لا` is negative, not prohibitive.

This establishes the learner operation as recognition of prohibitive `لا` by occurrence, not merely recognition of the surface token `لا`.

**New maturity: E2.**

## K33 — `إنّ` recognition

**Decision: HOLD E1+ / targeted recovery continues.**

Important control recovered:
- QS 2:11 `إِنَّمَا` contains accusative `إنّ` plus preventive `ما`; it is useful for family recognition but is not the cleanest canonical `إنّ + ism + khabar` beginner item.
- QS 2:12 `إِنَّهُمْ` is a clearer member of the `إنّ` family.

Still required before E2:
- several normalized `إنّ` occurrences;
- explicit contrast against conditional `إِنْ`;
- recognition-only scope, leaving governance analysis to later competencies.
