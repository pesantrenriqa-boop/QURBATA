# Evidence Gate K42 — Isim Maushul + Silah Minimal v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K41 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K42 target

Learner can identify an explicit **isim maushul** and delimit the **minimal silah** that completes it in a simple Qur'anic occurrence.

Core operation:

`اسم موصول` → identify the completing unit `صلة الموصول`

K42 is about **linkage and boundary**, not a full chapter on all relative constructions.

## Why K42 is genuinely new

Earlier competencies may recognize the relative pronoun itself, and K1–K41 already provide many internal nominal/verbal relations. K42 adds a new operation: treating a following clause/unit as a dependent completion of the relative head.

This is therefore not lexical inventory expansion.

## Hard scope

PASS examples should prefer:
- explicit, already-recognized isim maushul;
- short silah;
- silah whose internal grammar uses only K1–K41;
- clear end boundary;
- no nested relative clause;
- no conditional construction;
- no new nawasikh pattern;
- no advanced ellipsis.

## The `عائد` problem

The resumptive relation (`عائد`) is the main dependency risk.

### K42 core policy

The learner does **not** need a full taxonomy of `عائد` at K42.

But an occurrence can count as PASS only when the linkage remains pedagogically transparent under one of these conditions:
1. the internal relation is understandable with already-mastered pronoun/subject structure; or
2. the silah is verbal and its relation to the maushul can be delimited without teaching a new resumptive mechanism.

If explaining why the silah relates back to the maushul requires a new omitted/resumptive analysis, classify REVIEW-AID or PREMATURE.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — pure minimal silah
- explicit maushul;
- short verbal/nominal silah;
- internal dependencies all ≤ K41;
- boundary clear;
- no new `عائد` operation required.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
Additional material is allowed only when all required operations are already frozen.

### REVIEW-AID
Boundary is clear, but full explanation of `عائد` would require a new operation. Retain for later layer, not core quota.

### REVIEW-BOUNDARY
Candidate appears simple but the end of silah is not pedagogically obvious without higher clause analysis.

### PREMATURE
Nested clause, conditional linkage, complex ellipsis, later nawasikh behavior, advanced object/resumptive structure, or any K43+ operation is required.

## Anti-leak rule

At K42, learner should be able to answer only:

> `ما الاسم الموصول؟ وأين تبدأ صلته وأين تنتهي في هذا المثال البسيط؟`

Do not require:
- full i'rab of the entire silah;
- taxonomy of `عائد`;
- omitted `عائد` reconstruction;
- advanced semantic restriction;
- nested-clause parsing.

## Pedagogical dependency

K41 helps K42 because many short verbal silah may contain a subject encoded implicitly in the verb. Since basic fa'il mustatir is now frozen, those occurrences no longer automatically force a later competency—provided morphology is within K41 scope.

## Freeze criteria

K42 can freeze when:
1. clean examples prove stable head→silah boundary recognition;
2. internal silah grammar is cumulative through K41;
3. `عائد` complexity is not silently imported;
4. examples show enough structural diversity to establish the operation;
5. no numeric quota is forced at the expense of cumulative purity.
