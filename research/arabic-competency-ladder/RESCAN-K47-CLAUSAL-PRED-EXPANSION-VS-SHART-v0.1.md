# Rescan K47 — Clausal Predicate Expansion vs Simple Shart v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K46 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan apakah setelah K46 learner sebaiknya langsung masuk relasi syarth dua klausa, atau terlebih dahulu menggeneralisasi operasi **clause-as-predicate** ke host yang struktur dasarnya sudah dikuasai.

---

## Candidate A — Verbal clause as `خبر إنّ`

### Foundation
- K38: simple `إنّ + اسم + خبر`;
- K41: basic hidden subject;
- K44: restricted emphatic lām inside validated `إنّ` construction;
- K46: verbal clause as predicate of mubtada'.

### New operation
Recognize that the khabar slot governed by `إنّ` can be filled by an already-analyzable short verbal clause.

### Dependency burden
Low–moderate:
- host construction already known;
- clause-as-predicate abstraction introduced at K46;
- internal verbal syntax already known;
- only host transfer is new.

### Risk
If treated merely as an example of K46, we may under-model transfer across nawāsikh. But if every host gets a separate K automatically, the ladder becomes artificially granular. Distinctness must therefore be tested pedagogically.

**Judgement: VERY HIGH candidate, subject to anti-fragmentation test.**

---

## Candidate B — Verbal clause as `خبر كان/ليس`

### Foundation
- K39/K40 host constructions;
- K46 clause-as-predicate.

### Dependency burden
Moderate:
- same host-transfer principle;
- `كان`/`ليس` morphology and predicate configurations are broader;
- clean Qur'anic yield may be more difficult.

**Judgement: useful expansion, but likely after simpler `إنّ` host transfer.**

---

## Candidate C — Simple conditional relation

### New operation
Identify a condition clause and its response and link them as a two-clause dependency.

### Dependency burden
High:
- two boundaries;
- condition marker classification;
- protasis/apodosis relation;
- possible jawab marker;
- possible jazm/mood behavior.

**Judgement: still heavier.**

---

## Anti-fragmentation test

Question: should `خبر جملة فعلية` under mubtada', `إنّ`, `كان`, and `ليس` each become separate competencies?

Provisional answer:
- K46 was necessary because it introduced the **general operation** clause-as-predicate.
- After K46, repeating the same operation under each host should **not automatically create a new K** unless the host changes the learner operation or introduces a distinct dependency.

Therefore Candidate A may be better treated as **K46 transfer/application evidence**, not K47.

## Revised frontier

Because host transfer alone may not satisfy distinctness, the current strongest genuinely new operation remains:

**K47-CAND — simple conditional relation (boundary + linkage only).**

But K47 must be narrower than a full `أسلوب الشرط` chapter.

## K47 provisional definition

Learner can identify in a validated simple occurrence:
1. an explicit conditional marker already disambiguated for that occurrence;
2. the minimal condition clause;
3. the minimal response/result clause;
4. that the two clauses form one condition→result relation.

## Hard exclusions

Do not yet require:
- full inventory of أدوات الشرط;
- full jazm theory;
- all جواب الشرط markers;
- omitted jawab;
- oath + condition competition;
- counterfactual/rhetorical conditional semantics;
- nested conditionals.

## Decision

**Do not create K47 merely for transferring K46 to `إنّ/كان/ليس`.** Record those as transfer/application layers under existing competencies.

Proceed to an evidence gate for **K47 simple conditional boundary + linkage**, but keep particle and mood theory outside the core unless evidence proves they are unavoidable.
