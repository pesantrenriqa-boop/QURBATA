## E3 gate
A competency may move E2 → E3 only when its Qur'anic evidence demonstrates diversified coverage rather than repeated instances of one memorized pattern. Wave-01 gate requires:
1. multiple independent Qur'anic occurrences;
2. meaningful lexical/morphological/structural diversity appropriate to the competency;
3. at least one explicit boundary or negative-control rule;
4. same canonical learner operation across the bank;
5. minimal target spans usable for assessment;
6. no dependency on an unmastered later competency for scoring.

## Wave-01 decisions

### K04 — frequent preposition recognition
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
Diversified forms already include `في`, `من`, and prefixed `بـ`, across independent nominal hosts and verses. Boundary remains recognition of the preposition itself; complement structure is separately scored.

### K09 — preposition + overt noun
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
The bank contains `في + overt noun` and `من + overt noun` families across independent environments. Direct preposition+pronoun constructions are excluded to K20, providing a clean boundary.

### K10 — verb + overt fa'il
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
Independent lexical verbs and overt nominal subjects are already represented (`جاء الحق`, `زهق الباطل`, `وعد الله`). The operation remains identification of the overt fa'il, not broader clause parsing.

### K17 — simple na'at–man'ut
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
The bank contains definite and indefinite adjective agreement with distinct lexical material (`الصراط المستقيم`, `الفضل المبين`, `أجرا عظيما`). Chains/nested modification remain excluded.

### K21 — attached pronoun as direct object
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
Multiple verb-host object suffixes are supported, with same-environment negative controls distinguishing possessive/genitive suffixes and preposition-governed pronouns.

### K27 — generic negation recognition
**HOLD E2 / E3-CANDIDATE.**
The existing clean bank is heavily concentrated on `لا`. E3 requires broader validated negative-marker diversity without leaking into K30 prohibition or later operator analysis.

### K29 — imperative recognition
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
Independent imperative occurrences provide lexical and morphological diversity (`اقتده`, `قل`, `واصنع`). K30 prohibitive `لا + jussive imperfect` remains an explicit negative boundary.

### K32 — `قد` recognition
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
The bank spans bare and conjunction-prefixed `قد` (`قد`, `فقد`, `وقد`) and both perfect/imperfect verbal environments. Fine-grained aspectual semantics remain deferred.

### K35 — `ليس` recognition
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
Multiple independent occurrences provide complement/attachment diversity while learner operation remains recognition of `ليس`; governance is deferred to syntax competencies.

### K36 — `لو` recognition
**PROMOTE E2 → E3.**
Independent occurrences include `لو + verb`, `لو + كان`, and `لو + أن` environments. Counterfactual/conditional interpretation is not required at this recognition node.
