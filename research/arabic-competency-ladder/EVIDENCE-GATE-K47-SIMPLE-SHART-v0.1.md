# Evidence Gate K47 — Simple Conditional Linkage v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K46 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K47 target

Learner can identify a **simple conditional relation** in a validated Qur'anic occurrence:

`أداة الشرط` → `جملة الشرط` → `جواب الشرط`

The new operation is **two-clause linkage**: recognizing that one clause establishes a condition and the other gives the corresponding result/response.

## Core scope

K47 is deliberately narrower than a full chapter on `أسلوب الشرط`.

PASS candidates should prefer:
- one explicit conditional marker whose function is unambiguous in that occurrence;
- one short condition clause;
- one short explicit response clause;
- both clause internals analyzable through K1–K46;
- no omitted response;
- no oath–condition competition;
- no nested conditional;
- no advanced discourse ellipsis.

## Marker policy

K47 does **not** require memorizing the full inventory of conditional particles.

Each occurrence is classified first. The learner may work with a small validated set where the condition function is clear.

Recognition-only of a token such as `إنْ` is insufficient to constitute K47. The competence is relational, not lexical.

## The jazm problem

Verb mood/jazm may be visible in many conditional structures, but K47 does not automatically teach the full theory of jussive government.

### K47 policy

If a clean condition→response relation can be taught while treating the mood form as already validated morphology, the occurrence may remain PASS.

If understanding why the verb form changes is essential to the target explanation, classify REVIEW-JAZM and reserve the governing effect for a later competence.

## The jawab-marker problem

Some responses contain markers such as `فـ` or other devices. K47 core should prefer examples where the response boundary is clear without introducing a new marker theory.

If identifying the response depends on a not-yet-mastered jawab marker, classify REVIEW-JAWAB-MARKER.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — pure simple conditional
- explicit marker;
- clear condition clause;
- clear explicit response;
- internal grammar ≤ K46;
- no new governing explanation required.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
Additional material allowed only when all non-K47 operations are already frozen.

### REVIEW-JAZM
Condition relation is clear, but teaching the verb-form effect would require a new jussive/government competence.

### REVIEW-JAWAB-MARKER
Response is explicit but its marker requires separate analysis.

### REVIEW-BOUNDARY
Condition/response segmentation is not locally obvious.

### PREMATURE
- omitted jawab;
- nested condition;
- oath + condition competition;
- rhetorical/counterfactual structure requiring semantic theory;
- multiple clause dependencies beyond K47;
- disputed parsing.

## Anti-leak rule

At K47 the learner should answer only:

> `ما أداة الشرط؟ أين جملة الشرط؟ أين جواب الشرط؟ وما العلاقة بينهما؟`

Do not require yet:
- full list of أدوات الشرط;
- full theory of الجزم;
- all forms of اقتران جواب الشرط بالفاء;
- omitted jawab reconstruction;
- rhetorical classification of all conditionals.

## Freeze criteria

K47 can freeze only if:
1. enough clean Qur'anic occurrences establish explicit condition→response linkage;
2. clause boundaries are pedagogically clear;
3. internal clause grammar is cumulative through K46;
4. jazm and jawab-marker theory are not silently imported;
5. examples are structurally diverse enough to generalize the relation.
