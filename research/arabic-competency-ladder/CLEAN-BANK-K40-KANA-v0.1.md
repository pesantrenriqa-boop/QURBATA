# Clean Bank K40 — Simple `كان + اسمها + خبرها` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K39 DRAFT-FROZEN.  
**Parent:** `EVIDENCE-GATE-K40-KANA-v0.1.md`

## 1. Target Operation

K40 is not “learn the verb كان”. K37 already introduced limited recognition of the `كان` family.

K40 adds one distinct operation:

> identify a simple transformed nominal predication under `كان`, and map `اسم كان` + `خبر كان`.

Pedagogical contrast:

`مبتدأ + خبر` → `كان + اسم كان + خبر كان`

## 2. Clean-bank admission rule

A candidate may count toward K40 core only when all required analysis is <= K40.

### PASS-A
- recognized `كان` form;
- explicit اسم كان;
- explicit simple nominal/adjectival خبر كان;
- no hidden subject inference;
- no relative clause;
- no conditional clause;
- no new governing particle;
- no omitted predicate.

### PASS-B
Same target relation plus only already-frozen cumulative structures, e.g. simple idhafah, na'at, or PP already available before K40.

### REVIEW-MORPH
Subject recovery depends materially on person/number morphology encoded in the `كان` form.

### REVIEW-PRED
Predicate type is structurally analyzable but more complex than core: PP-heavy, coordinated, nested nominal phrase, etc.

### PREMATURE
Any occurrence requiring:
- fa'il/subject inference not yet unlocked;
- silah al-maushul;
- clausal khabar;
- conditional relation;
- ellipsis;
- advanced tense/aspect interpretation as part of the grammatical explanation.

## 3. Why many Qur'anic occurrences do not qualify as core

High frequency is not the same as pedagogical cleanliness. The Qur'an frequently uses `كان` in forms where:
- the subject is encoded in the verb morphology;
- the predicate is a PP or clause;
- the nominal component is itself structurally layered;
- discourse meaning depends on aspect/continuity/habituality;
- one argument is omitted or understood.

These remain useful corpus evidence, but they do not belong to the first teaching set for K40.

## 4. Diversity requirement

A valid K40 clean bank should not consist of twenty near-duplicates. Diversity should be measured across:
- noun vs pronoun-like explicit nominal subject where already permitted;
- simple adjective vs simple noun predicate;
- definiteness patterns;
- singular/plural forms only when morphology itself does not introduce a new operation;
- different surah contexts where available.

## 5. Core structural finding

The `كان` construction is sufficiently distinct from both K8 and K39:
- K8: ordinary nominal predication;
- K39: negative transformed predication with `ليس`;
- K40: `كان`-based transformed predication.

The relation is pedagogically stable provided the initial evidence bank excludes morphology-dependent subject recovery and clausal predicates.

### Dependency integrity: PASS
