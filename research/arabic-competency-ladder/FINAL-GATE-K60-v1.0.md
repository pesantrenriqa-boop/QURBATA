# FINAL GATE K60 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K59 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K60 — Mengenali `صلة الموصول` sebagai klausa yang melekat pada `اسم موصول` eksplisit, dengan menentukan batas awal dan akhirnya dalam konstruksi Qurani sederhana.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify the overt `اسم موصول`;
2. locate the beginning of its `صلة`;
3. locate the end of that relative clause;
4. classify the clause as `صلة الموصول` attached to the relative marker.

## Included

- overt familiar relative markers;
- short verbal or nominal `صلة` clauses;
- locally stable boundaries;
- cumulative clause-internal analysis through K59;
- occurrence-specific relative attachment.

## Excluded

- full `الأسماء الموصولة` inventory;
- advanced/omitted `العائد` analysis;
- nested relative clauses;
- ambiguous clause span;
- disputed attachment;
- simultaneous new morphology.

## Assessment prompt

`أين الاسم الموصول؟ وأين تبدأ صلة الموصول وأين تنتهي؟`

Expected core response identifies both the marker and the full `صلة` span.

## Gate results

- distinctness from lexical recognition: PASS;
- attachment/boundary operation: PASS;
- `العائد` containment: PASS;
- anti-fragmentation across relative forms: PASS;
- one-operation rule: PASS.

**Decision: K60 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

## Architectural significance

K60 adds a new asymmetric attachment relation: an overt nominal relative marker is completed by a following clause. This expands the ladder from peer clause relations and temporal sequencing into relative-clause structure.
