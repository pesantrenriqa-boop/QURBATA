# FINAL GATE K53 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K52 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K53 — Merekonstruksi satu `أن` مقدرة yang tervalidasi sebagai nāṣib bagi fi‘il mudhari‘ dalam konstruksi Qurani terkontrol.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence where the relevant nasb governor is not overt, learner can:
1. identify the controlled trigger/context;
2. recognize the following word as mudhari‘;
3. reconstruct one locally licensed hidden `أن`;
4. connect that reconstructed governor to the verb's nasb.

## Core staging

Initial teaching uses one maximally transparent trigger family. Additional trigger families are application breadth only when they preserve the same one-step learner operation.

## Included

- one hidden `أن`;
- stable occurrence-specific analysis;
- overt/recognizable trigger context;
- preferably transparent fatḥah already supported by K52;
- one-step syntactic reconstruction.

## Excluded

- full taxonomy of `أن المضمرة`;
- memorization of all obligatory/permissible environments;
- disputed ellipsis analyses;
- multiple hidden governors;
- trigger types requiring an additional unmastered semantic/syntactic operation;
- advanced morphology as a prerequisite.

## Assessment prompt

`ما العامل المقدّر قبل الفعل المضارع؟ وما أثره؟`

Expected core response: `أن` مقدرة, with its local nasb effect identified.

## Architectural sequence

K52 → K53 intentionally progresses from:
- overt governor and visible effect;
- to hidden governor reconstructed from a controlled environment.

The ladder therefore increases inferential depth without importing the full traditional rule inventory at once.
