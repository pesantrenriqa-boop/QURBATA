# RESCAN K67 — Concessive Relation vs Elaboration vs Omitted `العائد` v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE SELECTION  
**Baseline:** K1–K66 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K67 must add a genuinely new learner operation after the discourse-semantic layer developed in K62–K66. It must not merely add another conjunction or reopen advanced relative-clause ellipsis prematurely.

---

## Candidate A — concessive relation

Potential operation:

`A (expected obstacle/condition) + overt concessive marker + B (statement that still holds)`

Learner recognizes that B remains valid **despite** A.

### Strengths
- adds a discourse relation not yet covered by contrast, result, cause, purpose, or exception;
- relation is directional and inferentially meaningful;
- potentially represented by overt constructions.

### Risks
- Arabic concessive meaning can be expressed through several constructions rather than one simple marker;
- some cases overlap with conditional semantics (`لو`, `إن`, etc.);
- may require counterexpectation reasoning beyond local syntax.

### Verdict
**PROMISING BUT MARKER-SENSITIVE.** Needs strict evidence scan before selection.

---

## Candidate B — elaboration/specification

Potential operation:

`A (general proposition) → overt explanatory/specifying relation → B (more specific content)`

### Strengths
- useful for Qur'anic discourse comprehension;
- moves beyond coordination into informational structure.

### Risks
- marker family is diffuse;
- many examples depend on punctuation/tafsir-level discourse analysis rather than overt grammar;
- could overlap with apposition, بدل, تفسيرية, or lexical restatement.

### Verdict
**DEFER.** Evidence architecture is currently less stable than candidate A.

---

## Candidate C — omitted `العائد` in a tightly controlled relative-clause subtype

Potential operation:

`اسم موصول + صلة → detect a structurally required but unexpressed resumptive element`

### Strengths
- clear progression from K60–K61;
- introduces controlled null-element inference.

### Risks
- still requires transitivity/governance knowledge to justify omission;
- omitted position may vary;
- high risk of turning into a rule list about deletion licensing;
- harder than current discourse layer in terms of hidden representation.

### Verdict
**DEFER AGAIN.** Not the best K67 core while cleaner overt-relation candidates remain.

---

## Candidate D — explicit concessive/counterexpectation with one tightly validated construction

A narrower version of Candidate A:

> identify a construction where proposition B is asserted as holding **despite** proposition A or an expected obstacle introduced overtly.

### Strengths
- one new semantic operation;
- can be assessed directly by asking what expectation is overridden;
- distinct from K62 contrast because K62 compares/corrects propositions, while concessive relation says B remains true in spite of A.

### Risks
- requires careful marker validation;
- must exclude ordinary conditionals and mere contrast.

### Verdict
**STRONGEST K67 DIRECTION.**

---

# Selection

## K67-CAND

> **Mengenali relasi konsesif/counterexpectation dalam konstruksi Qurani eksplisit: suatu proposisi tetap berlaku meskipun terdapat keadaan atau faktor yang secara normal dapat menghalanginya.**

Core representation:

`A = obstacle / contrary expectation`

`B = still holds despite A`

## Distinctness

### vs K62 contrast
K62: B corrects/contrasts with A.

K67: B is asserted **despite** A; the relation explicitly overrides an expectation generated by A.

### vs K47 conditional
Condition expresses dependency: if A, then B.

Concession expresses persistence against expectation: even though / despite A, B.

### vs K65 exception
Exception removes or restricts a member from a domain.

Concession does not alter set membership; it overrides an expected consequence.

## Initial scope

PASS only when:
- both proposition spans are analyzable ≤ K66;
- concessive/counterexpectation function is overtly signaled and independently validated;
- no new conditional rule must be taught;
- no long-distance rhetorical inference is required;
- one obstacle→still-holds relation solves the item.

REVIEW/PREMATURE:
- ambiguous `لو`/conditional forms;
- marker requiring new advanced particle taxonomy;
- concessive meaning inferred only from tafsir;
- nested counterfactuals;
- rhetorical concession requiring balaghah analysis.

## Decision

**Advance K67-CAND to marker-specific evidence/distinctness gate.**

Elaboration and omitted `العائد` remain in the frontier queue.
