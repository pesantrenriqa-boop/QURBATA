# Evidence Gate K55 — Transparent Raf‘ on Mudhari‘ v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K54 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K55 target

Learner can recognize a familiar `فعل مضارع` in a validated environment where no active nāṣib or jāzim governs it, and identify a **transparent final ḍammah** as the local sign of raf‘.

Core reasoning:

`فعل مضارع مألوف + لا ناصب ولا جازم فعّال → ضمة ظاهرة = رفع`

K55 introduces positive recognition of the default raised state, not merely absence-checking.

## Why K55 is distinct

Earlier mood competencies establish:
- K49–K51: jussive effects;
- K52: overt nasb with visible fatḥah;
- K53: hidden governor for nasb.

K55 adds the third contrastive state for familiar sound-final mudhari‘: **raf‘ with visible ḍammah**.

## Hard scope

PASS examples should prefer:
- familiar sound-final mudhari‘;
- no overt or reconstructed nāṣib/jāzim required by the occurrence;
- transparent final ḍammah;
- no weak-final تقدير;
- no `ثبوت النون` as the target sign;
- no ambiguous particle function;
- no advanced clause dependency merely to establish mood.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — direct transparent raf‘
Familiar sound-final mudhari‘ + stable non-nasb/non-jazm environment + visible ḍammah.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean context
Additional syntax is permitted only when all required operations are ≤ K54 and do not obscure mood classification.

### REVIEW-GOV
The verb appears raised, but proving that no governor is active requires a later syntactic distinction.

### REVIEW-MORPH
Raf‘ is valid, but the surface sign is not transparent ḍammah.

### PREMATURE
- `ثبوت النون` as the primary raf‘ sign;
- weak-final estimated ḍammah;
- disputed governor analysis;
- advanced orthographic explanation;
- multiple competing mood triggers.

## Anti-leak rule

At K55 the learner should answer:

> `ما الفعل المضارع؟ وهل سبقه ناصب أو جازم فعّال؟ وما علامة رفعه الظاهرة؟`

Do not require:
- full raf‘–nasb–jazm paradigm production;
- complete governor inventory;
- estimated case/mood marks;
- five-verb raf‘ rules;
- weak-verb morphology.

## Positive-state requirement

K55 must not collapse into the statement “there is no nāṣib/jāzim.” A PASS item requires a positive morphological cue: **visible ḍammah** on a familiar mudhari‘.

## Freeze criteria

K55 can freeze only if:
1. visible ḍammah examples are stable and teachable;
2. absence of active governor is locally verifiable;
3. the learner does not need hidden-rule reconstruction beyond K54;
4. raf‘ is assessed positively, not by elimination alone;
5. examples remain morphologically simple.
