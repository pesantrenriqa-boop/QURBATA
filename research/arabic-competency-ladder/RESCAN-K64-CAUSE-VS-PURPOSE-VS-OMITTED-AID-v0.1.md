# RESCAN K64 — Cause vs Purpose vs Omitted `العائد` v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE SELECTION  
**Baseline:** K1–K63 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K64 must add one new operation after result/consequence (K63). We compare three candidate families by dependency cost, distinctness, and Qur'anic teachability.

## Candidate A — explicit cause/reason relation

Potential operation:

`Proposition B = reason/cause for Proposition A`

The learner identifies an overt, validated causal/reason marker and determines which proposition gives the reason for the other.

### Strengths
- highly useful for Qur'anic comprehension;
- semantically distinct from K63 result/consequence;
- directionality can be contrasted cleanly:
  - cause/reason: `WHY A? → because B`
  - result/consequence: `A → therefore B`;
- can use overt markers in tightly controlled evidence.

### Risks
- Arabic causal marking is heterogeneous;
- some particles are multifunctional;
- lexical causality may masquerade as grammatical relation.

### Verdict
**STRONGEST CANDIDATE**, provided core evidence uses overt and occurrence-validated reason/cause marking.

---

## Candidate B — explicit purpose/غاية relation

Potential operation:

`Action A → intended goal B`

### Strengths
- useful semantic distinction;
- overt purpose markers can be available;
- clear directed relation.

### Risks
- often entangled with hidden `أن`, nasb, and purpose-lām analysis;
- could reactivate K53 hidden-governor complexity;
- may require distinguishing purpose from consequence and causality.

### Verdict
**DEFER after cause/reason.** Dependency burden is higher than Candidate A.

---

## Candidate C — omitted `العائد`

Potential operation:

`اسم موصول + صلة → infer one omitted resumptive dependency`

### Strengths
- real structural operation;
- natural continuation from K60–K61.

### Risks
- null-element reconstruction;
- deletion licensing varies by syntactic position;
- often requires transitivity/governance knowledge;
- easy to turn into a traditional exception list.

### Verdict
**DEFER.** Still heavier than an overt cause/reason relation.

---

## Candidate D — purpose/result contrast as a meta-skill

### Verdict
**MASTERY TASK, NOT K.** Comparing already-learned relations does not itself introduce a new linguistic operation.

# Selection

## K64-CAND

> **Mengenali relasi sebab/alasan antara dua proposisi Qurani melalui marker eksplisit yang tervalidasi, lalu menentukan proposisi mana yang menjadi alasan bagi proposisi lainnya.**

Core representation:

`A ← because/reason ← B`

or pedagogically:

`لماذا A؟ لأن/بسبب B`

The exact surface marker is evidence-specific; K64 is the relation, not a particle inventory.

## Distinctness from K63

K63 asks:
- what is the result/consequence of A?

K64 asks:
- what is the reason/cause for A?

The relation direction and learner question are different.

## Initial evidence policy

PASS only when:
- both propositions are analyzable ≤ K63;
- reason/cause marker is overt;
- causal function is occurrence-specifically validated;
- no purpose/result ambiguity remains;
- no hidden `أن` is needed as a new operation;
- no rhetorical inference requiring tafsir is necessary.

## Decision

**Advance K64-CAND to marker-specific evidence/distinctness gate.**

Purpose and omitted `العائد` remain in the frontier queue.
