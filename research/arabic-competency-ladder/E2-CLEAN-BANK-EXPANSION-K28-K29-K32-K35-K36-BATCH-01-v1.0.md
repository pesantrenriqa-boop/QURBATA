## K28 — vocative recognition
**Decision: PROMOTE TO E2.**

Clean evidence:
- QS 2:21 — `يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ`; grammar explicitly identifies `يا` as أداة نداء and `أيها` as the vocative addressee construction.
- The same occurrence also supplies a useful separation from the following imperative `اعْبُدُوا`: recognition of the vocative marker is K28; imperative recognition is K29.

Boundary:
- Do not require full i‘rab of `أيها الناس` at K28.
- Do not merge vocative recognition with command recognition.

**New maturity: E2.**

## K29 — imperative recognition
**Decision: E2 candidate, not yet promoted.**

Clean positive evidence:
- QS 2:21 — `اعْبُدُوا` is explicitly annotated as a 2nd-person masculine plural imperative.
- QS 2:23 — `فَأْتُوا` provides another imperative environment in the challenge passage.

Need before promotion:
- at least one additional morphologically distinct imperative form;
- keep imperative recognition separate from prohibitive `لا` (K30).
