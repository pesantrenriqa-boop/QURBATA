# Rescan K52 — Jazm Consolidation vs `فاء جواب الشرط` Rule vs Nasb Effects v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K51 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan frontier K52 berdasarkan **operasi belajar yang benar-benar baru**, bukan sekadar rangkuman materi lama atau urutan kitab nahwu.

Kandidat utama:
1. konsolidasi tiga tanda jazm yang sudah dipelajari;
2. generalisasi kaidah kapan `جواب الشرط` memakai `فـ`;
3. efek nashab pada fi‘il mudhari‘;
4. kandidat corpus-local lain yang lebih ringan jika ditemukan.

---

## Candidate A — Consolidation of three jazm signs

### Foundation
- K49: jazm by transparent sukūn;
- K50: jazm by `حذف حرف العلة`;
- K51: jazm by `حذف النون`.

### New operation?
Learner classifies which of the three already-mastered realizations appears in a new occurrence.

### Distinctness analysis
This is valuable as **integration/mastery**, but it does not add a fundamentally new grammatical relation or morphological mechanism. It is a cross-case consolidation of K49–K51.

**Judgement:** important assessment layer, but **not strong enough for a new K** under anti-inflation policy.

Record as mastery/consolidation, not K52.

---

## Candidate B — Rule-level generalization for `فاء جواب الشرط`

### Foundation
- K47: simple condition→response linkage;
- K48: identify explicit `فاء جواب الشرط` in validated occurrences.

### New operation
Move from occurrence-level recognition to rule-level prediction: determine when a response type requires/accepts fā’ based on the structure of the jawab.

### Dependency burden
High:
- nominal sentence responses;
- talab/request forms;
- جامد/non-inflecting predicates;
- markers such as `قد`, `لن`, `ما`, `لا`, etc. depending on scope;
- possibly wider inventory of response types.

This is not one local operation unless aggressively restricted.

**Judgement:** DISTINCT, but currently too broad for K52 core.

---

## Candidate C — Basic nasb effect on familiar mudhari‘ by transparent fatḥah

### Foundation
K49–K51 established the general abstraction:

> syntactic environment can produce a visible/reconstructive change at the end of a mudhari‘.

### New operation
Recognize in a validated simple environment that a familiar `فعل مضارع` is **manṣūb** and that its most transparent surface sign is final fatḥah.

### Dependency burden
Moderate and locally controllable if the governing particle is already recognized or explicitly supplied.

### Scope control
K52 would **not** teach all `نواصب المضارع` at once. It can begin with one or a very small validated set of overt particles, while the operation remains:

`familiar mudhari‘ + validated nasb environment → final fatḥah as nasb effect`.

### Main risk
If particle recognition itself is new, K52 may accidentally bundle two operations:
1. identify governing particle;
2. identify nasb effect.

Therefore K52 evidence should prefer environments where the particle has already been recognized earlier or can be treated as supplied context.

**Judgement:** STRONGEST genuinely new operation among current candidates.

---

## Head-to-head result

**K52-CAND — basic nasb effect on familiar mudhari‘ via transparent fatḥah in a validated simple environment.**

Reason:
1. extends the morphology-from-syntax bridge established at K49–K51;
2. adds a genuinely new mood/effect category rather than summarizing old signs;
3. can be kept atomic if particle inventory is tightly controlled;
4. lighter than rule-level `فاء جواب الشرط` generalization.

## Provisional definition

> **K52 — Mengenali efek nashab dasar berupa fatḥah yang transparan pada fi‘il mudhari‘ familiar dalam lingkungan nashab sederhana yang tervalidasi.**

## Hard exclusions

K52 does not yet teach:
- full inventory of `نواصب المضارع`;
- `حذف النون` under nasb;
- hidden/estimated fatḥah;
- `أن` المضمرة theory;
- complex purpose/result clauses;
- full i‘rāb mood system;
- rule-level comparison of all raf‘/nasb/jazm states.

## Evidence gate requirements

Classify occurrences as:
- **PASS-A:** familiar sound-final mudhari‘ + overt/validated nasb environment + transparent final fatḥah;
- **PASS-B:** additional material fully ≤ K51;
- **REVIEW-PARTICLE:** nasb is clear but governor recognition is not yet within frozen scope;
- **REVIEW-ORTH:** Qur'anic orthography/phonology obscures the sign;
- **PREMATURE:** deletion-based nasb, hidden `أن`, complex clause relation, or multiple new operations.

## Decision

Open K52 evidence gate for **transparent fatḥah nasb**. Keep jazm consolidation as a mastery layer and postpone full `فاء جواب الشرط` rule generalization.

## Next

After K52 evidence/freeze, rescan whether the next distinct operation should be:
- `حذف النون` under nasb;
- restricted governor recognition (`لن`, `أن`, etc.) if not already frozen elsewhere;
- hidden `أن` only much later;
- rule-level fā’ generalization;
- other corpus-derived structural operations.
