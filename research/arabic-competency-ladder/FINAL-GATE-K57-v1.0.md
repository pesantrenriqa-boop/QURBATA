## Competency

**K57 — Mengenali raf‘ pada fi‘il mudhari‘ mu‘tal akhir melalui `الضمة المقدرة`, dimulai dari bentuk alif-final yang paling transparan dan diperluas secara terkontrol.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify a familiar weak-final mudhari‘;
2. validate its local raf‘ environment;
3. recognize that no overt ḍammah is surfaced;
4. classify the raf‘ sign as `ضمة مقدرة`.

## Internal staging

- alif-final: core entry stage;
- wāw-final: controlled extension;
- yā’-final: controlled extension.

These are not separate K numbers unless later evidence proves a distinct learner operation.

## Included

- familiar weak-final mudhari‘;
- validated raf‘ environment;
- one estimated ḍammah;
- alif-first pedagogy;
- cumulative analysis through K56.

## Excluded

- full theory of `التعذر` and `الثقل`;
- nominal estimated i‘rāb;
- multiple simultaneous estimated signs;
- disputed parsing;
- full weak-verb taxonomy;
- explanation of every phonological cause as a core requirement.
