# FINAL GATE K50 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K49 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K50 — Mengenali jazm melalui `حذف حرف العلة` pada fi‘il mudhari‘ mu‘tal akhir yang bentuk dasarnya familiar, dengan satu rekonstruksi lokal.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated simple jussive occurrence, learner can:
1. identify the word as a familiar weak-final mudhari‘;
2. recognize the jussive environment;
3. detect that the expected final weak segment is absent;
4. restore only that local segment mentally and relate its deletion to jazm.

## Internal variants

Underlying final alif-, wāw-, and yā’-type forms are staged inside K50. They do not receive separate K numbers unless future evidence shows a genuinely different learner operation.

## Included

- familiar weak-final mudhari‘;
- one final weak-letter deletion;
- locally transparent reconstruction;
- validated jussive context;
- cumulative syntax/morphology through K49.

## Excluded

- `حذف النون`;
- full weak-verb taxonomy;
- complete conjugation paradigms;
- advanced Qur'anic orthographic reconstruction;
- ambiguous lexical base;
- multiple simultaneous ellipses/deletions;
- advanced i‘rāb theory.

## Assessment prompt

`ما الفعل المضارع؟ وما الحرف المعتل المحذوف من آخره بسبب الجزم؟`

The learner need not recite a full weak-verb paradigm.

## Gate results

- distinctness from K49: PASS;
- one-step reconstruction: PASS;
- alif/wāw/yā’ anti-fragmentation: PASS;
- morphology containment: PASS;
- dependency control: PASS.

**Decision: K50 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

## Architectural note

K49 → K50 forms a deliberate progression:
- visible morphological effect (`السكون`);
- locally reconstructed morphological effect (`حذف حرف العلة`).

This progression does not imply that every traditional jazm sign must immediately become the next K.
