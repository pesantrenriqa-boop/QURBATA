# Head-to-Head K24–K26 v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — NON-AUTHORITATIVE

## Candidates

- A — isim isyarah sebagai mubtada' + khabar nominal sederhana
- B — jar–majrur sebagai pelengkap/attachment fi'il sederhana
- C — fa'il mustatir dasar
- D — isim maushul + silah minimal

## A. Demonstrative as Mubtada'

Dependencies:
- K22 recognition isim isyarah;
- K8 jumlah ismiyyah core.

Core target:
`اسم إشارة + خبر اسمي بسيط`

Strength:
- integrasi langsung dua K yang sudah frozen;
- unit dapat sangat lokal;
- tidak menuntut hidden element;
- tidak membutuhkan relative clause atau verbal inference.

Risk:
- contoh harus dibedakan dari demonstrative phrase/apposition;
- jangan memakai contoh yang analisisnya bergantung pada badal/'athaf bayan yang belum diajarkan.

**Judgement:** VERY HIGH.

## B. Verbal + Jar–Majrur Attachment

Dependencies:
- K10 verbal relation;
- K9/K20 jar–majrur;
- perlu memastikan PP benar-benar attached ke verb target.

Strength:
- sangat produktif;
- membuka keterangan arah/tempat dan argument/complement verbal.

Burden:
- attachment ambiguity;
- PP kadang melekat ke noun/adjective/clause, bukan verb;
- unit target dapat membawa subject/object tambahan.

**Judgement:** HIGH, but more parsing burden than A.

## C. Fa'il Mustatir

Dependencies:
- K6/K7 verb recognition;
- K10 verbal relation;
- person/number/gender inference from verb morphology.

Burden:
- target tidak tampak sebagai token;
- memerlukan inferensi morfosintaksis;
- lebih abstrak daripada A dan B.

**Judgement:** MODERATE-HIGH / LATER.

## D. Isim Maushul + Minimal Silah

Dependencies:
- K23 recognition isim maushul;
- struktur jumlah yang menjadi silah harus sudah dikuasai;
- batas silah dan return/reference relation dapat menjadi isu.

Even a minimal verbal silah can require hidden subject or object structure. Therefore this is heavier than mere demonstrative integration.

**Judgement:** IMPORTANT BUT LATER THAN A/B.

## Head-to-Head Result

Ordering strength:

1. **A — demonstrative as mubtada'**
2. **B — verbal PP attachment**
3. **C — fa'il mustatir**
4. **D — relative pronoun + silah** (position relative to C still needs evidence, but not before A/B)

## Revised Frontier

- **K24-CAND — isim isyarah sebagai mubtada' + khabar nominal sederhana**
- **K25-CAND — jar–majrur sebagai pelengkap/attachment fi'il sederhana**
- **K26-CAND — fa'il mustatir dasar**
- **K27-CAND — isim maushul + silah minimal**

## Freeze Conditions for K24

- demonstrative occurrence validated;
- syntactic role = mubtada';
- khabar nominal simple and within prior K set;
- no apposition/badal analysis required for target unit;
- no nested idhafah/na'at/coordination above allowed set.

## Freeze Conditions for K25

- attachment relation to verb must be occurrence-specific;
- PP internal structure must be <= K25;
- subject/object structures must already be within frozen K set;
- ambiguous attachment = REVIEW, not PASS.

## Decision

K24 remains strongest immediate frontier. K25 follows. K26 stays behind both. K27 opened as relative-clause integration but not promoted ahead of K26 without evidence.
