# FINAL GATE K55 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K54 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K55 — Mengenali raf‘ dasar pada fi‘il mudhari‘ familiar melalui ḍammah yang transparan dalam lingkungan tanpa nāṣib atau jāzim aktif.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify a familiar sound-final mudhari‘;
2. verify that no active nāṣib or jāzim governs it locally;
3. identify the overt final ḍammah;
4. relate that visible ending to raf‘.

## Included

- familiar sound-final mudhari‘;
- overt final ḍammah;
- locally validated absence of active nasb/jazm governor;
- cumulative syntax/morphology through K54.

## Excluded

- `ثبوت النون` as raf‘ sign;
- `الضمة المقدرة`;
- weak-final forms with non-visible ending;
- complex/disputed governance;
- full mood paradigm production.

## Assessment prompt

`ما الفعل المضارع؟ وما علامة رفعه الظاهرة؟ ولماذا لم ينصب أو يجزم هنا؟`

Expected core response identifies the verb, visible ḍammah, and locally validates the absence of an active nāṣib/jāzim.
