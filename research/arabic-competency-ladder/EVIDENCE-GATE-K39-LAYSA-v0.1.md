# Evidence Gate K39 — Simple `ليس + اسم + خبر` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K38 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Target

K39-CAND teaches the simple transformed nominal construction:

`ليس + اسم ليس + خبر ليس`

Only clean Qur'anic occurrences count toward the core bank.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — pure core
Requirements:
- `ليس` occurrence already recognizable from K35;
- explicit اسم ليس;
- explicit simple خبر ليس;
- no new particle/government phenomenon;
- no hidden subject/predicate inference required;
- no relative/clausal structure beyond frozen competencies.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
Same K39 relation, but may include structures already frozen K1–K38, such as:
- prior nominal morphology;
- idhafah already learned;
- adjective relation already learned;
- jar–majrur already learned, if its role does not introduce a new special government analysis.

### REVIEW-BA
Occurrence contains `بـ` associated with the khabar or another `ليس`-specific surface pattern whose grammatical treatment is not yet unlocked.

These remain corpus evidence but do not count toward pure-core teaching yield.

### REVIEW-MORPH
`ليس` carries person/number morphology whose analysis would force the learner into hidden/attached subject interpretation beyond the K39 target.

### PREMATURE
Requires one or more of:
- omitted اسم or خبر;
- clause-level khabar not yet unlocked;
- relative-clause linkage;
- conditional linkage;
- complex coordination/scope;
- later `كان` family construction as required explanation;
- other higher dependency.

## Main finding

The earlier concern is confirmed: a substantial portion of Qur'anic `ليس` occurrences are not ideal pure-core examples because surface morphology and `بـ`-marked predicates complicate the construction.

However, this does **not** invalidate K39. It means the competency definition must remain narrow and the teaching bank must privilege explicit, minimally loaded occurrences.

## Pedagogical decision

K39 should not attempt to teach all Qur'anic uses of `ليس`.

The core operation is only:
1. recognize that `ليس` transforms an underlying nominal predication;
2. identify an explicit اسم ليس;
3. identify a simple explicit خبر ليس;
4. compare the structure to ordinary mubtada'–khabar already known.

`باء` in the khabar is a later extension, not a prerequisite.

## Freeze gate

K39 may freeze if:
- the structural operation is stable;
- a diversified clean bank exists even if smaller than 20–30 pure-core items;
- REVIEW-BA and REVIEW-MORPH are excluded from the core quota;
- no K40+ dependency is required to explain the selected teaching examples.

**Quality-over-quota rule applies.** A smaller authentic core bank is preferable to contaminating the level.

## Preliminary verdict

**STRUCTURALLY READY / EVIDENCE-CONSTRAINED.**

The relation itself is pedagogically distinct and appropriately follows K38. The main issue is corpus clean-yield, not dependency reversal.
