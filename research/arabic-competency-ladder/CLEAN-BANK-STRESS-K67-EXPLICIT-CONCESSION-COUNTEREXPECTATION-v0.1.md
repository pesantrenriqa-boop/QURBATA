# Clean Bank + Stress Test K67 — Explicit Concession / Counterexpectation v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K66 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

Learner recognizes a validated Qur'anic construction in which proposition B remains true despite circumstance/proposition A creating a normal expectation to the contrary.

Core reasoning:

`A creates expectation X → nevertheless B holds`

## Positive-evidence requirement

A core K67 item must provide enough overt linguistic support that the learner can identify the counterexpectation relation without depending primarily on tafsir, rhetorical reconstruction, or broad world knowledge.

## PASS requirements

- A is overt/local;
- B is overt/local;
- concessive/counterexpectation construction is overt and validated;
- B remains valid despite A;
- all clause-internal syntax is analyzable ≤ K66;
- the relation is not reducible to ordinary contrast;
- no new conditional subtype must be learned;
- no hidden element reconstruction is required;
- one semantic relation solves the item.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — transparent concession
The occurrence straightforwardly supports the reading “although/even though A, B.”

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean concession
A or B contains prior-level complexity, but the defeated-expectation relation remains transparent.

### REVIEW-CONTRAST
A and B differ or oppose one another, but there is no clear expectation defeated by B. This remains K62 territory.

### REVIEW-CONDITION
The construction is primarily hypothetical/conditional and requires new structural analysis.

### REVIEW-RHETORIC
Counterexpectation is recoverable only through broad tafsir or balaghah.

### PREMATURE
- implicit concession with no overt signal;
- nested concessive structures;
- several competing expectation triggers;
- disputed relation scope;
- multiple new semantic operations.

## Stress test 1 — Is K67 just a stronger K62?

No. K62 requires contrast/correction. K67 requires an additional expectation model:

`A normally licenses expectation X, but B holds contrary to X.`

A learner who only says “A and B are different” has not demonstrated K67.

## Stress test 2 — Is K67 secretly a conditional competence?

No. Conditional form may appear in some concessive constructions, but any item requiring a new condition-marker rule is excluded. K67 targets the semantic relation of persistence despite an expectation-triggering circumstance.

## Stress test 3 — Can tafsir knowledge substitute for linguistic evidence?

No. If the concessive force is only evident after importing external exegetical explanation, the occurrence is unsuitable for the core clean bank.

## Assessment design

Core prompt:

`ما الأمر الذي كان يمكن أن يوقع خلاف النتيجة في التوقع؟ وما الذي ثبت مع ذلك؟`

Indonesian support:

`Apa keadaan yang seharusnya menimbulkan dugaan berbeda, dan apa yang tetap berlaku meskipun demikian?`

Expected response must state both the expectation-triggering circumstance and the proposition that nevertheless holds.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH STRICT OVERT-EVIDENCE FILTER.**

K67 is independently assessable and distinct from K62 provided the clean bank excludes merely contrastive, primarily conditional, or tafsir-dependent occurrences.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K67 — Mengenali relasi konsesif/counterexpectation dalam konstruksi Qurani eksplisit dengan menentukan keadaan yang menimbulkan ekspektasi tertentu dan proposisi yang tetap berlaku meskipun keadaan tersebut.**
