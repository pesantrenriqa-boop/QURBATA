# Final Gate K47 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN — RESEARCH LAYER  
**Baseline:** K1–K46 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K47 — Simple Conditional Boundary + Linkage

### Competency statement
Learner can identify, in a validated simple Qur'anic conditional occurrence:

1. the explicit conditional marker;
2. the minimal `جملة الشرط`;
3. the minimal `جواب الشرط`;
4. the relation between them as **condition → result**.

Core representation:

`أداة الشرط` → `جملة الشرط` → `جواب الشرط`

## Why K47 is distinct

K47 introduces a genuinely new operation: **linking two clauses into one conditional dependency**. Earlier competencies analyze words, phrases, single-clause roles, relative linkage, omitted resumptive recovery, and clause-as-predicate; none require identifying a protasis–apodosis relation.

## Hard scope

Core K47 examples must have:
- explicit, occurrence-validated conditional marker;
- short, locally delimited condition clause;
- short, locally delimited response clause;
- internal syntax fully supported by K1–K46;
- no omitted response;
- no nested conditional;
- no advanced discourse reconstruction.

## Locked phenomena

K47 does **not** unlock:
- full `أدوات الشرط` taxonomy;
- jussive (`جزم`) morphology/rules;
- full theory of `فاء الجواب` or other response markers;
- omitted `جواب الشرط`;
- oath/condition competition;
- counterfactual/rhetorical conditional semantics;
- nested conditionals.

Occurrences requiring these are REVIEW/PREMATURE, not core K47 evidence.

## Evidence classes retained

- **PASS-A:** pure boundary/linkage;
- **PASS-B:** cumulative-clean extra material;
- **REVIEW-JAZM:** relation clear but jussive explanation is a later operation;
- **REVIEW-JAWAB-MARKER:** response identification depends on an unlocked marker;
- **REVIEW-AMBIG:** particle/function ambiguity;
- **PREMATURE:** omitted/nested/complex conditional structure.

## Final stress-test findings

1. **Jazm is not a hard prerequisite** for recognizing condition→result architecture.
2. **Special جواب markers are not universally prerequisite**; clean examples can establish the relation without teaching them.
3. Boundary + linkage should remain **one competence**, not split into two artificial sub-Ks.
4. K47 remains cumulative-only when evidence restrictions are enforced.

## Assessment prompt

For a clean K47 example, learner should be able to answer:

> `ما أداة الشرط؟ أين جملة الشرط؟ أين جواب الشرط؟ وما العلاقة بينهما؟`

## Freeze decision

**K47 is DRAFT-FROZEN.**

This freeze applies only to the research competency ladder. It does not modify production curriculum registries or downstream teaching assets.

## Next frontier

Rescan K48 among genuinely distinct operations revealed after K47, especially:
- jussive effect in simple conditional verbs;
- explicit response-marker function;
- other corpus-discovered clause relations;
- remaining local structures lighter than advanced conditional theory.

Do not assume K48 automatically equals jazm.
