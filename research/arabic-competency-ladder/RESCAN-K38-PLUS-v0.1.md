# Rescan K38+ v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — NON-AUTHORITATIVE  
**Baseline:** K1–K37 DRAFT-FROZEN.  
**Anti-inflation:** new K only for a distinct learnable operation/category/relation, not lexical inventory expansion.

## Frontier candidates

### A. Full `إنّ` construction: `إنّ + اسمها + خبرها`

Already available:
- K33 recognizes `إنّ`;
- K8 provides nominal predication foundation.

New operation:
- identify the governed nominal relation after `إنّ`;
- distinguish اسم إنّ and خبر إنّ;
- observe case effect only within clean examples.

Burden:
- case/government is genuinely new;
- khabar may be nominal, PP, or clause; core must restrict to simplest nominal pattern.

**Judgement: VERY HIGH as next structural integration.**

### B. Full `ليس` construction

Already available:
- K35 recognizes `ليس`;
- K8 nominal predication.

New operation:
- identify اسم ليس / خبر ليس in simple clean pattern.

Burden:
- agreement/person forms;
- khabar can vary structurally.

**Judgement: HIGH.**

### C. Full `كان` construction

Already available:
- K37 limited `كان` family recognition;
- K8 nominal predication.

New operation:
- identify اسم كان / خبر كان;
- observe transformed nominal predication.

Burden:
- larger inflection family than `ليس`;
- multiple surface configurations.

**Judgement: HIGH, but after simpler governed construction.**

### D. Conditional `إِنْ` recognition

Distinct category recognition is possible, but recognition-only is low-yield unless contrasted with K33 `إنّ`. Its real pedagogical value is clause linkage/jazm, which is structurally heavier.

**Judgement: MODERATE; do not inflate K merely for homographic token recognition.**

### E. Basic fa'il mustatir

New operation is inference of an unexpressed subject from verb morphology/context.

**Judgement: HIGH pedagogical value but abstract.**

### F. Isim maushul + minimal silah

K23 recognition exists. New operation is linking relative pronoun to its silah and delimiting the relative clause.

**Judgement: HIGH but clause-boundary burden significant.**

## Head-to-head result

The lightweight recognition frontier is now sufficiently exhausted that the ladder should begin **structural integration**, rather than keep adding isolated particles.

Strongest K38 candidate:

- **K38-CAND — simple `إنّ + اسم + خبر` construction**

Why before `ليس/كان`:
1. K33 recognition already established;
2. core construction can be restricted to explicit noun + simple nominal khabar;
3. introduces government/case effect in a controlled environment;
4. provides a reusable concept for later nawāsikh.

Provisional continuation:
- K39-CAND — simple `ليس + اسم + خبر`;
- K40-CAND — simple `كان + اسم + خبر`;
- K41-CAND — fa'il mustatir dasar;
- K42-CAND — isim maushul + silah minimal;
- conditional `إنْ` full relation later after verb mood/jazm readiness.

## Critical cumulative rule

K38 teaching examples must contain no unmastered structure. Therefore initial `إنّ` evidence should prefer:
- explicit اسم إنّ;
- simple nominal/adjectival khabar already analyzable from earlier Ks;
- no embedded relative clause;
- no complex PP/clausal khabar unless those relations are already within the allowed dependency set;
- no additional particle-government phenomenon requiring later K.

## Decision

Move frontier from recognition accumulation to structural integration. Next action: build occurrence-specific Qur'anic evidence gate for K38 and test whether clean examples are sufficient for 20–30+ teaching items without importing K39+ structures.
