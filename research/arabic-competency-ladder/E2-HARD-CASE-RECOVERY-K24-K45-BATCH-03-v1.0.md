## K24 — demonstrative predication / local demonstrative relation
Decision: PROMOTE TO E2.

### Clean-bank rule
A positive item must contain an overt demonstrative (DEM) whose local syntactic role can be distinguished from mere deictic recognition (K22) and from apposition/nominal adjacency. The target span must support a locally recoverable predicational/relational reading without requiring a later discourse competency.

### Boundary controls
- DEM recognition alone is insufficient: e.g. isolated ذلك / هؤلاء tests K22, not K24.
- A demonstrative embedded in a prepositional phrase or broader discourse frame is not automatically a K24 positive.
- Apposition and adjacent nominals must be excluded unless the target relation is independently recoverable.

### Evidence normalization
The Quranic Arabic Corpus POS system explicitly separates DEM from personal and relative pronouns. Independent Qur'anic environments such as 6:90 (`أُولَٰئِكَ الَّذِينَ هَدَى اللَّهُ`) and 4:143 (`بَيْنَ ذَٰلِكَ ... إِلَى هَٰؤُلَاءِ`) provide distinct demonstrative hosts useful for positive/negative discrimination. The K24 bank uses only locally segmentable predicational/relational cases; non-predicational DEM occurrences are retained as negative controls.

Result: K24 satisfies E2 diversity + boundary gate.

## K45 — relative clause with deleted resumptive ('ā'id maḥdhūf)
Decision: HOLD E1+ / E2-candidate.

### Closure gate retained
Promotion requires multiple independent relative-clause occurrences where:
1. the relative pronoun is overt;
2. the syntactic slot of the resumptive is uniquely recoverable locally;
3. no competing parse changes the recovered role;
4. the example does not depend on tafsir-level ellipsis;
5. an overt-resumptive relative clause is available as a contrast control.

### Why not promoted
The corpus reliably tags REL forms, but POS tagging alone does not prove a deleted resumptive. Because Arabic relative clauses may realize the linking element overtly or implicitly depending on syntactic role, K45 requires clause-level reconstruction evidence beyond simple REL identification. The present bank is not yet sufficiently normalized for assessment-grade E2.
