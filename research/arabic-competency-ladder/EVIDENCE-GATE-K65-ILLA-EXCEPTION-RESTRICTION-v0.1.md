# EVIDENCE GATE K65 — Explicit `إلا` Exception / Restriction v0.1

**Status:** ACTIVE EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K64 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Candidate

> **K65-CAND — Mengenali relasi pengecualian/pembatasan melalui `إلا` eksplisit, kemudian menentukan domain yang dibatasi dan unsur yang dikecualikan/dikhususkan.**

## Core learner operation

The learner identifies a set/domain and the element or proposition singled out by overt `إلا`:

`[DOMAIN] + إلا + [EXCLUDED / RESTRICTED ELEMENT]`

Core questions:
1. what is the relevant domain or general scope?
2. what does `إلا` remove from or restrict within that scope?

## PASS requirements

- `إلا` is overt;
- left scope/domain is locally recoverable;
- right-side excluded/restricted element is overt;
- the relation is semantically clear without teaching full `باب الاستثناء`;
- all internal morphology/syntax is analyzable ≤ K64;
- no new case-rule choice after `إلا` is required;
- no long-distance scope ambiguity;
- one restriction/exception relation solves the item.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — transparent exception
A clear general domain is followed by overt `إلا` and an overt exception.

### PASS-B — transparent restriction
The construction is naturally understood as restricting a previously available set of alternatives, with overt `إلا` and clear local scope.

### REVIEW-I‘RAB
The relation is clear but solving the item would require a new i‘rāb rule for the post-`إلا` element.

### REVIEW-SCOPE
`إلا` is overt but the exact scope/domain is not locally transparent.

### REVIEW-TYPE
Traditional classification (`تام/ناقص`, `موجب/غير موجب`) becomes necessary to decide the analysis.

### PREMATURE
- implicit exception without `إلا`;
- advanced `إلا` functions not reducible to the core relation;
- disputed scope;
- multiple nested exclusions;
- full exception-paradigm analysis.

## Distinctness tests

### vs K62 contrast/correction
K62: proposition B corrects/limits an expectation raised by A.

K65: an overt `إلا` establishes a **set/scope restriction or exception**. The learner must identify domain and excluded/restricted member, not merely semantic contrast.

### vs negation
Negation changes truth/polarity. K65 changes the **scope of inclusion** by carving out or restricting an element from a domain. Negative environments may occur, but negation itself is not the target.

### vs lexical recognition of `إلا`
Simply translating `إلا` as “kecuali” does not pass. Learner must identify both structural-semantic roles: domain and exception/restriction.

## Anti-fragmentation rule

Do not create separate K numbers for every traditional exception subtype. `تام`, `ناقص`, `موجب`, `غير موجب`, and post-`إلا` i‘rāb choices remain later staging/mastery unless they demonstrably require a new learner operation.
