# Evidence Gate K43 — Explicit `عائد` in Simple Silah v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K42 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K43 target

Learner can identify an **explicit resumptive element (`عائد`)** inside a simple `صلة الموصول` and connect it back to the already-identified `اسم موصول`.

Core operation:

`اسم موصول` → `صلة الموصول` → **explicit `عائد` returning to the maushul**

K42 established head–silah boundary. K43 adds one relation inside that already-bounded unit.

## Scope: explicit only

K43 deliberately excludes omitted/estimated resumptives.

PASS candidates require:
- explicit isim maushul already within earlier recognition scope;
- silah boundary analyzable by K42;
- explicit pronoun or other overt resumptive relation inside the silah;
- internal grammar otherwise limited to K1–K42;
- no nested relative clause;
- no conditional linkage;
- no advanced ellipsis.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — pure explicit `عائد`
Short silah, explicit resumptive, transparent antecedent relation, no new operation beyond K43.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
Additional structure allowed only when fully analyzable through K1–K42.

### REVIEW-ROLE
The resumptive is explicit but its syntactic role (object, PP complement, possessive relation, etc.) would require an operation not securely frozen.

### REVIEW-MORPH
The overt resumptive is embedded in morphology whose person/number/gender analysis exceeds current scope.

### PREMATURE
- omitted `عائد`;
- disputed/complex resumptive analysis;
- nested maushul;
- complex clausal embedding;
- conditional relation;
- ellipsis or reconstruction beyond K43.

## Anti-leak rule

At K43, learner only needs to answer:

> `ما الاسم الموصول؟ ما صلته؟ وأين العنصر الظاهر الذي يعود إليه؟`

Do not require:
- taxonomy of حذف العائد;
- conditions for deleting resumptive pronouns;
- full i'rab of every element;
- semantic/discourse antecedent resolution beyond the local relative construction.

## Distinctness

K43 is not merely a refinement of K42. K42 asks for **boundary/linkage of the relative unit**; K43 asks for **internal co-reference** between an overt element and the relative head. These are separable learner operations and can be independently assessed.

## Yield requirement

Aim for 20–30+ examples over the mature corpus bank, but do not force the number. A smaller structurally diverse PASS bank is preferable to examples that silently introduce omitted `عائد` or higher dependencies.

## Freeze criteria

Freeze K43 only if:
1. explicit `عائد` examples are sufficiently numerous and diverse;
2. the local co-reference is transparent;
3. no omitted-resumptive theory is needed;
4. all non-K43 operations are ≤ K42;
5. K43 remains independently assessable from K42.
