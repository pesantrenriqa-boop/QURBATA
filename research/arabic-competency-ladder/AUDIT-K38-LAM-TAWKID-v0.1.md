# Audit K38 — Lām al-Tawkīd Dependency v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — NON-AUTHORITATIVE  
**Baseline:** K1–K37 DRAFT-FROZEN; K38 `إنّ + اسمها + خبرها` structurally ready but evidence-gated.

## Question

Apakah `لام التوكيد` yang sering muncul bersama `إنّ` harus menjadi kompetensi tersendiri **sebelum** K38, atau cukup dikunci sebagai fitur lanjutan sehingga occurrence yang mengandungnya tidak dipakai sebagai core evidence K38?

## Distinctness test

`لام التوكيد` memang membawa fungsi semantik/pragmatik distinct: penguatan/tawkid. Namun target K38 adalah operasi sintaksis mengenali konstruksi `إنّ + اسم + خبر` sederhana. Secara dependency, memahami lām tersebut **tidak diperlukan** untuk memahami core relation K38 apabila contoh tanpa lām tersedia.

Therefore:
- lām is a distinct later feature;
- it is **not a hard prerequisite** for K38;
- occurrences with lām must not be used as pure-core K38 teaching evidence unless lām is explicitly treated as locked/ignored metadata;
- preferred K38 bank should prioritize examples without lām.

## Anti-inflation decision

Do **not** insert a new K before K38 solely because many `إنّ` occurrences contain lām. Frequency of co-occurrence is not equivalent to prerequisite status.

A later K may teach:
- recognition of emphatic lām;
- relation with `إنّ`;
- scope/semantic emphasis;
subject to a separate distinctness/evidence gate.

## K38 evidence policy update

### PASS-A
`إنّ` + explicit/simple اسم + simple خبر; no unmastered marker.

### PASS-B
Same core construction plus only structures already frozen K1–K37.

### REVIEW-LAM
Contains emphatic lām whose function is not yet taught. May be retained in corpus bank but not counted toward pure-core quota.

### PREMATURE
Requires later construction such as:
- full `كان/ليس` relation;
- relative clause linkage not yet taught;
- conditional linkage;
- genuinely complex clausal khabar;
- other new government relation.

## Freeze threshold recommendation

K38 should freeze only when:
1. core operation is structurally stable;
2. a sufficiently diverse clean bank exists without depending on REVIEW-LAM examples;
3. morphology of اسم/خبر is occurrence-validated;
4. examples span multiple surahs/contexts where possible;
5. no example introduces K39+ as a required explanation.

The pedagogical target remains 20–30+ usable examples where corpus yield allows, but **quality outranks quota**. If only fewer truly clean examples exist, do not contaminate K38 merely to reach a number.

## Decision

`لام التوكيد` does **not** move before K38. K38 remains next frontier. Continue occurrence-level clean-bank expansion and freeze only after sufficient PASS-A/PASS-B evidence.
