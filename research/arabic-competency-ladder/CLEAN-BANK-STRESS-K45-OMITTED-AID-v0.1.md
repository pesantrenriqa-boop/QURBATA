# Clean Bank + Stress Test K45 — Omitted `عائد` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K44 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Goal

Menguji apakah basic omitted `عائد` dapat berdiri sebagai satu kompetensi yang:
- cumulative-clean;
- local-reconstruction only;
- lebih ringan daripada simple conditional linkage;
- tidak berubah menjadi teori lengkap حذف العائد.

## Core reconstruction pattern

K45 core hanya menerima occurrence di mana:

1. isim maushul sudah jelas;
2. batas silah sudah jelas dari K42;
3. tidak ada `عائد` eksplisit seperti pada K43;
4. ada satu slot sintaksis lokal yang secara stabil dibutuhkan oleh silah;
5. slot itu dapat dihubungkan kembali ke isim maushul tanpa analisis wacana luas;
6. seluruh unsur lain menggunakan K1–K44.

## Clean classes

### PASS-A — single-slot transparent omission
- exactly one missing resumptive relation;
- role locally recoverable;
- no competing parse;
- no new clause relation.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean
- extra PP/object/nominal material permitted only if already mastered;
- omission remains the only new operation.

### REVIEW-ROLE
- omitted relation is likely but grammatical role requires an operation not yet frozen.

### REVIEW-ANALYSIS
- more than one plausible reconstruction;
- classical parsing disagreement materially changes learner operation.

### PREMATURE
- nested maushul;
- multiple ellipses;
- conditional linkage;
- omitted elements beyond the resumptive slot;
- advanced clause-as-predicate analysis;
- discourse reconstruction beyond local syntax.

## Stress test against simple shart

### K45 omitted `عائد`
Cognitive operations:
1. inspect already-bounded silah;
2. detect one missing local slot;
3. connect that slot back to known maushul.

### Simple shart candidate
Cognitive operations:
1. identify conditional marker;
2. delimit protasis;
3. delimit apodosis;
4. link two clauses;
5. potentially distinguish jawab markers;
6. eventually account for verbal mood/jazm.

### Result

Even though omitted `عائد` involves reconstruction, its **dependency graph is still narrower** than simple shart when K45 is restricted to one-slot local recovery.

Therefore no dependency reversal is found.

## Atomicity result

K45 remains one competence only if learner task is restricted to:

> `أين الموصول؟ أين صلته؟ وما الموضع الواحد الذي يفهم منه عائد غير ظاهر؟`

Do not ask deletion-rule taxonomy or full i‘rab reconstruction.

## Evidence sufficiency judgement

A mature K45 bank should prioritize structural diversity over raw count. Acceptable diversity should include more than one recovered local role where the analysis is stable, but difficult or disputed roles are not needed to freeze the core.

**Judgement:** READY FOR DRAFT-FREEZE, subject to the narrow scope above.
