## Candidate A — `الضمة المقدرة` on weak-final mudhari‘

### Foundation
- K50: reconstruct deleted weak-final segment under jazm;
- K55: raf‘ via overt ḍammah;
- general familiarity with weak-final mudhari‘ patterns.

### New operation
Recognize that a weak-final mudhari‘ is marfū‘ although the ḍammah is **not overtly realized**, and classify the ending as `ضمة مقدرة` for a locally transparent reason.

### Dependency burden
Moderate:
- requires distinction between grammatical state and visible surface form;
- requires weak-final morphology familiarity;
- no new clause relation;
- no hidden governor reconstruction beyond what has already appeared in K53.

### Risk
If all weak-final classes are bundled at once, K57 turns into a taxonomy of taqdīr causes rather than one operation.

**Judgement: VERY HIGH candidate if restricted to one transparent weak-final class first.**

## Head-to-head decision

**K57-CAND — Recognize `الضمة المقدرة` on a tightly controlled weak-final mudhari‘ in raf‘.**

Reason:
1. introduces a genuinely new inferential move: grammatical state exists without overt surface harakah;
2. directly extends K55 while leveraging weak-final awareness from K50;
3. remains local to one word rather than adding new clause relations;
4. more substantive than adding another list category.

## K57 provisional definition

Learner can identify a familiar weak-final mudhari‘ in a validated raf‘ environment and state that its raf‘ is realized by a **locally justified estimated ḍammah (`ضمة مقدرة`)**, even though the ḍammah is not visible.

## Core scope policy

Initial K57 should use **one maximally transparent weak-final class only**. Additional weak-final classes are admitted later only if the learner operation remains unchanged.

## Hard exclusions

K57 does not teach:
- full taxonomy of `التعذر` / `الثقل` across all categories;
- all estimated case/mood endings in Arabic;
- nominal i‘rāb with estimated vowels;
- hidden governors;
- multiple simultaneous morphology operations;
- disputed orthographic analyses.

## Evidence classes for next gate

- **PASS-A:** familiar weak-final mudhari‘ + clear raf‘ environment + one locally transparent reason for non-overt ḍammah.
- **PASS-B:** cumulative-clean extra context only.
- **REVIEW-CAUSE:** raf‘ is clear, but explaining why the ḍammah is estimated requires a broader morphology rule.
- **REVIEW-BASE:** weak-final base is not familiar enough.
- **PREMATURE:** multiple taqdīr mechanisms, disputed morphology, or advanced orthographic explanation.

## Decision

Open K57 evidence gate for **restricted `الضمة المقدرة` on weak-final mudhari‘**. Freeze only if the operation can remain one-step and occurrence-specific without expanding into the full theory of estimated endings.
