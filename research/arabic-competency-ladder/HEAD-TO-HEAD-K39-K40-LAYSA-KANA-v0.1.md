# Head-to-Head K39–K40 — `ليس` vs `كان` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K38 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Candidate A — simple `ليس + اسم + خبر`

Prerequisites:
- K35 recognition `ليس`;
- K8 nominal predication;
- nominal/adjectival/PP relations already available where explicitly permitted.

New learnable operation:
- identify `ليس` as transforming a nominal predication;
- identify explicit اسم ليس and simple خبر ليس;
- distinguish this construction from ordinary mubtada'–khabar.

### Core restrictions

PASS core should prefer:
- explicit اسم ليس;
- explicit simple خبر;
- no `بـ` الزائدة in khabar unless that feature has been separately unlocked;
- no relative-clause or complex clausal complement;
- no hidden subject as required analysis.

### Main risk

Qur'anic `ليس` frequently occurs with:
- pronominal/person morphology;
- prepositional-looking khabar;
- `بـ` with khabar;
- constructions whose surface simplicity hides higher analysis.

Thus occurrence-specific validation is mandatory.

**Complexity judgement: HIGH, but family is relatively narrow.**

---

## Candidate B — simple `كان + اسم + خبر`

Prerequisites:
- K37 limited `كان` family recognition;
- K8 nominal predication.

New learnable operation:
- identify the transformed nominal relation;
- identify اسم كان and خبر كان in explicit/simple occurrences.

### Core restrictions

PASS core should prefer:
- explicit form from already-recognized limited family;
- explicit اسم كان;
- simple explicit خبر;
- no complex temporal/aspect interpretation required;
- no hidden subject;
- no relative/clausal khabar unless previously unlocked.

### Main risk

`كان` has much broader inflectional and syntactic distribution than `ليس`. Surface occurrences often include:
- attached subject morphology;
- plural/person forms;
- omitted/understood elements;
- PP/adjectival/clausal khabar;
- semantic/aspectual uses that should not be collapsed into a single gloss.

**Complexity judgement: HIGHER than `ليس`.**

---

## Head-to-head dimensions

| Dimension | `ليس` | `كان` |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition prerequisite already frozen | K35 | K37 |
| Family breadth | narrower | broader |
| Inflection burden | moderate | high |
| Risk of hidden subject | moderate | high |
| Core relation distinctness | strong | strong |
| Transfer from K38 governance | strong | strong |
| Clean-example risk | high | higher |

## Decision

Keep provisional order:

- **K39-CAND — simple `ليس + اسم + خبر`**
- **K40-CAND — simple `كان + اسم + خبر`**

The ordering is not based on traditional chapter order, but on dependency burden and clean-evidence probability.

## Important caveat

`ليس` must not be frozen merely because it is narrower. Before final gate, its evidence bank must prove that enough Qur'anic occurrences can be taught without requiring:
- `باء` الزائدة analysis;
- omitted subject/predicate inference;
- later relative/clausal relations.

If clean yield is too low, K39 may need a narrower definition or another structural competence may precede it.

## Next

Build occurrence-specific evidence gate for K39. Classify candidates as:
- PASS-A: explicit اسم + simple explicit خبر;
- PASS-B: only prior-K cumulative structure;
- REVIEW-BA: khabar with `بـ` or related special behavior;
- PREMATURE: hidden/complex/clausal structure requiring K40+.
