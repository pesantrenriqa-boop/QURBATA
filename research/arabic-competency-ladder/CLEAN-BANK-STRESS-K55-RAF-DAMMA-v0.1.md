# Clean Bank + Stress Test K55 — Transparent Raf‘ by Ḍammah v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K54 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

Learner recognizes a familiar sound-final `فعل مضارع` as marfū‘ in a validated environment with no active nāṣib or jāzim, and identifies a **transparent final ḍammah** as positive surface evidence of raf‘.

Core reasoning:

`فعل مضارع + لا ناصب ولا جازم + ضمة ظاهرة → رفع`

## Positive-evidence requirement

K55 must not be solved only by saying “nothing governs it.” Every core occurrence requires an overt, pedagogically readable ḍammah. Absence of nāṣib/jāzim is contextual validation; the visible ending is the positive morphological evidence.

## PASS requirements

- target is a familiar mudhari‘;
- sound-final morphology;
- final ḍammah is overt and readable;
- no active nāṣib/jāzim in the relevant dependency;
- no estimated ending;
- no nūn-retention analysis needed;
- no advanced orthographic issue;
- surrounding syntax is cumulative-clean ≤ K54.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-B

### REVIEW-GOV

### REVIEW-MORPH

## Stress test 1 — Is raf‘ merely the default residue after K49–K53?

No. K55 requires a positive classification: the learner identifies an overt ending and relates it to a validated raf‘ state. This is not simply “not nasb and not jazm.”

## Stress test 2 — Distinctness from K49 and K52

The learner now differentiates three environment→surface mappings:
- jazm → transparent sukūn (K49);
- nasb → transparent fatḥah (K52);
- raf‘ → transparent ḍammah (K55).

K55 therefore completes a controlled three-way contrast without making the contrast itself a separate K.

## Stress test 3 — Should `ثبوت النون` enter K55?

No. Nūn retention requires recognizing a particular inflectional pattern and contrasting it with K51 deletion. That is a separate transfer/generalization question and should not broaden K55 core.

## Yield policy

No numeric quota is forced. Qur'anic examples qualify by transparency, stable parsing, and cumulative cleanliness. Complex examples are rejected rather than used to inflate the bank.

## Verdict
