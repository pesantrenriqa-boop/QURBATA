# Head-to-Head K35–K36 v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K34 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Candidates

- K35-CAND — recognition `لَيْسَ` family with limited scope
- K36-CAND — recognition `كَانَ` family with limited scope

## K35 — `لَيْسَ`

Target recognition:
- identify `ليس` and occurrence-validated inflected forms as belonging to the same special verbal/copular family;
- do **not** yet analyze `اسم ليس` / `خبر ليس`;
- do **not** require full conjugational paradigm production.

Advantages:
- functional identity is relatively distinctive;
- family is narrower than `كان`;
- pedagogically integrates existing nominal predication without yet altering its case analysis.

Risks:
- suffix/person morphology appears on some forms;
- negation semantics may tempt premature comparison with K27 nafi particles.

Judgement: **STRONG, LIMITED-FAMILY REC**.

## K36 — `كَانَ`

Target recognition:
- identify a deliberately limited set of high-frequency forms from the `كان` family;
- postpone full paradigm and `اسم كان / خبر كان` analysis.

Advantages:
- extremely productive in Quranic Arabic;
- central to later nominal clause transformation.

Risks:
- much wider inflectional family (`كان`, `كانت`, `كانوا`, `كنت`, `كنا`, `يكون`, etc.);
- easy for a recognition K to silently become a morphology/tasrif K;
- semantic/aspectual uses are broader than a single simple copular gloss.

Judgement: **STRONG BUT HEAVIER**.

## Head-to-head criteria

| Criterion | `ليس` | `كان` |
|---|---:|---:|
| Family breadth | narrower | wider |
| Surface variability | lower | higher |
| Risk of hidden tasrif burden | lower | higher |
| Immediate syntactic effect if unlocked | high | high |
| Recognition atomicity | stronger | moderate-strong |

## Decision

**K35 remains `لَيْسَ`; K36 remains limited `كَانَ` family.**

No dependency reversal found. `ليس` should be taught first because the recognition boundary can be kept narrower and cleaner.

## Scope lock

### K35 lock
- no اسم ليس / خبر ليس;
- no i'rab change;
- no full negation-system comparison.

### K36 lock
- no اسم كان / خبر كان;
- no full conjugational paradigm;
- no aspect/tense generalization beyond occurrence-level recognition.
