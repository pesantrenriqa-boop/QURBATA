# Evidence Gate K41 — Fa'il Mustatir Dasar v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K40 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K41 target

Learner can identify that a simple finite verb has a grammatical subject that is not expressed as a separate visible noun, and can recover the basic grammatical person from already-mastered verbal morphology.

This competence is **not** a full conjugation paradigm and does not require discourse antecedent reconstruction.

## Hard prerequisites

- prior verb recognition;
- prior verb–subject relation with explicit fa'il;
- pronoun recognition;
- distinction between visible noun and attached morphology;
- cumulative structures K1–K40.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — pure hidden-subject core

Occurrence qualifies when:
- verb is active and syntactically simple;
- no explicit fa'il follows or precedes in the target relation;
- subject slot is recovered directly from morphology already in scope;
- no relative clause, conditional linkage, passive, or ellipsis analysis is required;
- the learner need not identify a difficult discourse antecedent.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean

Additional objects, PP complements, or nominal material are allowed only if every such structure is already frozen by K1–K40.

### REVIEW-MORPH

Verb form requires person/number/gender morphology beyond what has been safely introduced. Keep in corpus bank but do not count toward core evidence.

### REVIEW-REF

Grammatical subject is morphologically recoverable, but identifying who the pronoun refers to requires wider discourse. This can later serve reinforcement but not first-layer teaching.

### PREMATURE

Exclude if target analysis depends on:
- passive voice;
- relative-clause linkage;
- conditional relation;
- nested clause;
- advanced ellipsis;
- multiple competing subject analyses;
- full tasrif paradigm not yet taught.

## Atomicity rule

The new operation at K41 is only:

> infer that the grammatical subject is implicit in a simple verbal form.

Do not add at the same time:
- full table of pronouns;
- all verb-person endings;
- discourse-coreference analysis;
- semantic role taxonomy.

## Pedagogical sequence inside K41

1. contrast explicit fa'il vs no explicit noun;
2. identify the finite verb;
3. recover person/number from familiar morphology;
4. mark the fa'il slot as implicit;
5. only later connect to wider discourse referent where useful.

## Clean-yield expectation

K41 should have high Qur'anic yield, but the bank must privilege diversity of simple patterns over raw frequency. Many superficially easy verbs occur inside relative/conditional clauses and therefore must be filtered out.

## Freeze criteria

K41 may freeze when:
- enough PASS-A/PASS-B occurrences establish the operation robustly;
- core examples do not rely on K42+ clause linkage;
- morphology scope is explicit and bounded;
- at least several different person/number patterns are represented where already supported;
- examples demonstrate that hidden subject detection is distinct from merely memorizing verb endings.

## Next frontier

If K41 freezes, perform a new head-to-head for K42 among:
- isim maushul + silah minimal;
- emphatic lām as a distinct operation;
- simple conditional relation;
- other clause-linking operations revealed by corpus evidence.
