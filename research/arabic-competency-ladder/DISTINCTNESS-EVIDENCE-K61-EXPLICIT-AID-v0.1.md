**Status:** PRE-FREEZE GATE

## Competency under test

> **Mengenali `العائد` eksplisit di dalam `صلة الموصول` dan menghubungkannya kembali kepada `اسم الموصول` dalam konstruksi Qurani sederhana.**

## New learner operation

K60 established relative-clause attachment and boundary. K61 adds a different task:

`اسم موصول → صلة الموصول → ضمير عائد ظاهر → resolve reference back to اسم الموصول`

The learner must not merely find a pronoun. The learner must determine **which overt pronoun functions as the resumptive link and what antecedent it refers to**.

## Distinctness test

### K60
- identify `اسم الموصول`;
- identify beginning/end of `صلة الموصول`.

### K61
- search inside that already-identified `صلة`;
- identify one overt pronoun;
- classify it as the relative resumptive (`العائد`);
- resolve its antecedent back to the `اسم الموصول`.

**Result: PASS.** K61 adds reference resolution/anaphoric linkage, not another span-boundary task.

## Anti-inventory test

K61 is not “learn all pronoun forms.” A core item is solved by a relational judgment:

`هذا الضمير يعود إلى أي اسم موصول؟`

Knowing the pronoun form alone is insufficient.

## PASS requirements

- overt familiar `اسم موصول`;
- K60-clean `صلة` boundary;
- exactly one pedagogically salient overt pronoun functioning as `العائد`;
- antecedent local and unique;
- pronoun form already familiar;
- no deleted resumptive;
- no competing antecedents;
- no nested relative dependency needed.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — direct local resumptive
Short relative clause, overt attached/separate pronoun, unique return to the relative antecedent.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean
Additional internal clause structure is ≤ K60 while the resumptive relation remains local and transparent.

### REVIEW-REF
Pronoun is overt, but more than one plausible antecedent exists.

### REVIEW-FUNCTION
Pronoun occurs inside the relative clause, but proving it is the required resumptive needs later case/governance theory.

### PREMATURE
- `العائد` deleted;
- long-distance reference;
- nested relative clauses;
- multiple competing pronouns/antecedents;
- advanced ellipsis or discourse reconstruction.

## Pronoun-position policy

Do not split K61 by pronoun position (subject/object/attached to preposition, etc.) if the learner operation is unchanged. Treat these as internal staging only after local transparency is validated.

Expected response identifies the overt resumptive and its antecedent.
