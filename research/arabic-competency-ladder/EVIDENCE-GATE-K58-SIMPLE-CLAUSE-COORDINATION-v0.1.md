# Evidence Gate K58 — Simple Clause Coordination v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K57 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K58 target

Learner can recognize two independently analyzable clauses that are linked by one overt coordinator in a simple Qur'anic construction.

Core representation:

`[جملة 1] + رابط/حرف عطف ظاهر + [جملة 2]`

The new operation is **clause-to-clause coordination recognition**.

## Why K58 is distinct

Earlier competencies already cover:
- internal nominal and verbal clause structure;
- clause-as-predicate;
- relative clause linkage;
- simple conditional linkage;
- mood effects and local reconstruction.

K58 adds a different interclausal relation: neither clause is subordinate as a condition/result or relative modifier. The learner must recognize that two clause units are coordinated at the same structural level.

## Core scope

Initial K58 evidence should use one maximally transparent overt coordinator, preferably a functionally stable occurrence of `و` or another validated coordinator where the relation is clearly clause-to-clause.

K58 does **not** teach the full traditional chapter of `حروف العطف`.

## PASS requirements

- both coordinated units are clauses;
- each clause is independently analyzable through K1–K57;
- one overt coordinator sits at the clause boundary;
- its local coordinating function is stable;
- no cross-clausal ellipsis is required;
- no nested coordination;
- no disputed scope;
- no new semantic relation must be mastered to identify the coordination.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — transparent two-clause coordination
Two short clauses + one overt coordinator + clear same-level linkage.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean clauses
Either clause may contain material already mastered ≤ K57, provided the coordination boundary remains transparent.

### REVIEW-SCOPE
Coordinator is overt, but determining whether it links clauses or smaller constituents requires later theory.

### REVIEW-SEM
Coordination is syntactically plausible but interpretation depends on a semantic distinction not yet mastered.

### PREMATURE
- nested coordination;
- omitted shared material requiring reconstruction;
- coordination interacting with oath/condition competition;
- disputed attachment;
- long discourse-level coordination where local clause boundary is unclear.

## Anti-inventory rule

K58 is not “learn all conjunctions.” The learner operation is relational:

> identify clause 1, identify clause 2, identify the overt linker, and state that the two clauses are coordinated at the same level.

Different coordinators count as application breadth unless they introduce a genuinely different learner operation.

## Contrast with K47

K47:
`condition clause → response clause` = asymmetric dependency.

K58:
`clause 1 ↔ clause 2` = coordinated same-level relation.

This relation is therefore structurally distinct.

## Assessment prompt

`أين الجملة الأولى؟ وأين الجملة الثانية؟ وما الرابط بينهما؟ وهل الثانية تابعة للأولى أم معطوفة عليها؟`

Expected core response identifies both clause boundaries and the overt coordinator, classifying the relation as coordination rather than subordination.

## Freeze criteria

K58 may freeze only if:
1. clean Qur'anic examples show clause-to-clause coordination clearly;
2. coordinator scope is stable;
3. no hidden shared material is required;
4. both clauses stay cumulative-clean ≤ K57;
5. the competence remains relation-based rather than conjunction-list based.
