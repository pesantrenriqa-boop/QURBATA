## Stage decision
K01–K10 form the core S1 foundation cluster.
K11–K12 are bridge competencies: instruction may begin in late S1 but mastery is recorded at the S2 boundary.
K13–K15 belong to S2 controlled morphosyntax.

## Placement design consequence
The first placement section should not ask for full i‘rab. It should progressively sample:
- recognition;
- minimal contrasts;
- local relation analysis;
- unseen transfer only where E3 evidence exists.

A learner who demonstrates K01–K10 mastery can bypass their teaching units and enter the S1→S2 bridge. A learner who fails a prerequisite contrast is routed to the earliest unresolved K, not automatically to the beginning of the book.

## QURBATA consequence
Do not equate K01–K15 with fifteen pages or fifteen levels. The delivery model may bundle compatible recognition K into a page/meeting while preserving one target operation per newly introduced feature. Review items may combine only mastered K.
