# Clean Bank + Stress Test K49 — Jazm by Transparent Sukūn v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K48 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

In a simple conditional construction already analyzable through K47–K48, learner recognizes a familiar `فعل مضارع` whose **surface sukūn** is a transparent sign of jazm in that occurrence.

K49 adds morphological-effect recognition, not a full theory of jussive mood.

## Admission discipline

PASS requires all of the following:
- the verb is confidently mudhari‘;
- the conditional environment is already identifiable;
- the relevant final surface is a pedagogically transparent sukūn;
- no weak-final deletion is required;
- no deletion of nūn is required;
- no orthographic explanation is required to make the sign visible;
- analysis is stable and local.

## Clean-bank structural classes

### PASS-A — direct transparent sukūn
A familiar sound-final mudhari‘ appears in a validated conditional environment and its jazm is visible through sukūn.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean context
Extra material may occur around the verb, provided every other required operation is ≤ K48 and does not obscure the target sign.

### REVIEW-ORTH
The grammatical analysis may be correct, but Qur'anic orthography/phonology makes the surface sign unsuitable for first-stage teaching.

### REVIEW-MORPH
The occurrence requires a morphology distinction beyond K49 core.

### PREMATURE
Any occurrence whose pedagogical explanation depends on:
- `حذف حرف العلة`;
- `حذف النون`;
- irregular/complex morphology;
- non-conditional jazm theory;
- multiple new operations.

## Stress test 1 — Is K49 merely "spot a sukūn"?

No. Sukūn alone is not sufficient. The learner must connect:

`فعل مضارع` + validated jussive environment → **this final sukūn is the local effect of jazm**.

Thus lexical/orthographic recognition is not enough.

## Stress test 2 — Does K49 require the full set of `علامات الجزم`?

No. The three major surface realizations should not be bundled prematurely:
- sukūn;
- deletion of weak letter;
- deletion of nūn.

They differ in morphological burden. K49 intentionally introduces the concept through the least reconstructive surface sign.

## Stress test 3 — Is the scope too narrow to deserve a K?

The competence remains pedagogically meaningful because it introduces a new abstraction: **syntactic environment can alter the morphological ending of a verb**. This is a major bridge from clause recognition into i‘rāb/mood effects.

A restricted first exemplar is preferable to teaching the entire jazm paradigm at once.

## Stress test 4 — Dependency on K48

`فاء جواب الشرط` is not universally required for a K49 example. K49 depends primarily on K47's conditional structure. K48 is cumulative background when fā’ is present, not a hard prerequisite for every occurrence.

## Yield policy

Do not force a numeric quota. The clean bank may be smaller than earlier structural banks because morphological purity is stricter. Freeze depends on **structural validity and teachability**, not on padding the bank with deletion-based examples.

## Stress-test verdict

**PASS WITH NARROW SCOPE.**

K49 is distinct, cumulative, and pedagogically justified provided the final specification remains restricted to transparent sukūn cases.
