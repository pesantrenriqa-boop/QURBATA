# Evidence Gate K40 — Simple `كان + اسمها + خبرها` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K39 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K40 target

Learner can identify a **simple explicit `كان` construction** and distinguish:

1. a validated form of the `كان` family;
2. **اسم كان**;
3. **خبر كان**;
4. the relation to the underlying nominal predication already available from K8.

This is a structural-integration competence, not a full tasrif lesson.

## Hard prerequisites

- K8 nominal predication;
- K37 limited `كان` family recognition;
- prior nominal/adjectival/PP relations only when already frozen and explicitly permitted.

## Core evidence policy

### PASS-A — pure core

Occurrence has:
- `كان`-family form already inside K37 recognition scope;
- explicit اسم كان;
- explicit, simple خبر كان;
- no hidden subject required;
- no relative/clausal predicate requiring later competence;
- no new particle-government relation.

### PASS-B — cumulative clean

May contain additional material only when every required operation is already covered by K1–K39.

### REVIEW-MORPH

Occurrence requires morphology beyond the limited family scope, especially when attached person/number marking itself must be analyzed to recover the subject.

### REVIEW-PRED

Khabar is PP/adjectival/complex in a way that may be teachable cumulatively but needs explicit dependency verification.

### PREMATURE

Exclude from K40 core if required analysis includes:
- omitted/understood اسم كان;
- clausal khabar not yet unlocked;
- relative-clause linkage;
- conditional linkage;
- fa'il mustatir as a necessary inference;
- full tense/aspect discourse interpretation;
- other new government or ellipsis phenomenon.

## Why K40 is harder than K39

Unlike `ليس`, the `كان` family has broad Qur'anic inflectional distribution. A superficially familiar form may encode person/number information that makes the grammatical subject non-explicit. Therefore token recognition cannot be confused with construction analysis.

## Pedagogical representation

Preferred first-layer representation:

`كان` → `اسم كان` → `خبر كان`

Then contrast with K8:

`مبتدأ + خبر`  → transformed under `كان` → `اسم كان + خبر كان`

Do not introduce the whole `كان وأخواتها` family here. K40 is about `كان` itself in a controlled Qur'anic pattern.

## Yield rule

Aim for a broad bank (ideally 20–30+ usable occurrences over time), but freeze depends on **clean structural sufficiency**, not numeric quota. Repetition of near-identical morphology does not count as pedagogical diversity.

## Freeze criteria

K40 may freeze only when:
- enough PASS-A/PASS-B occurrences establish the pattern robustly;
- examples are distributed where possible across different Qur'anic contexts;
- no K41+ inference is needed to explain the core;
- the limited-family boundary from K37 remains intact;
- `كان` is not being used as a backdoor for full tasrif/aspect instruction.

## Next frontier after K40

Do not assume K41 automatically equals fa'il mustatir. After K40 evidence/freeze, rescan structural candidates including:
- fa'il mustatir dasar;
- isim maushul + silah minimal;
- emphatic lām;
- conditional relation;
- other genuinely distinct operations revealed by corpus evidence.
