# Clean Bank + Stress Test K57 — `الضمة المقدرة` on Weak-Final Mudhari‘ v0.1

**Status:** PRE-FREEZE STRESS TEST  
**Baseline:** K1–K56 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Operation under test

Learner identifies a familiar weak-final `فعل مضارع` in a validated raf‘ environment and concludes that its raf‘ is represented by a **non-overt estimated ḍammah (`ضمة مقدرة`)**.

Core reasoning:

`فعل مضارع معتل الآخر + بيئة رفع ثابتة + لا ضمة ظاهرة → ضمة مقدرة`

The new operation is **estimating an i‘rāb sign that is grammatically present but not surfaced**.

## Variant question: alif / wāw / yā’

The traditional explanations differ in detail. K57 must not silently import the full theory of `التعذر` and `الثقل`.

### Core staging decision

- **Stage A:** alif-final forms first, because non-realization of ḍammah is maximally transparent and can be taught without broad phonological theory.
- **Stage B:** wāw-final and yā’-final forms may enter as controlled extension only after occurrence-specific validation and only if the learner operation remains “recognize raf‘ → estimate ḍammah.”

The three endings do **not** automatically receive separate K numbers.

## PASS requirements

- target is confidently mudhari‘;
- weak-final base is familiar;
- local raf‘ environment is clear;
- no overt raf‘ sign is available;
- exactly one estimated ḍammah is required;
- no simultaneous deletion/reconstruction is needed;
- no disputed parsing;
- surrounding syntax is cumulative-clean ≤ K56.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — alif-final core
Familiar alif-final mudhari‘ + clear raf‘ environment + no visible ḍammah + stable estimated-ḍammah analysis.

### PASS-B — wāw/yā’ extension
Allowed only when no additional morphological theory is required to solve the item.

### REVIEW-CAUSE
The i‘rāb is clear but explaining why the vowel is not surfaced would require detailed `ثقل/تعذر` theory.

### REVIEW-MORPH
Weak-final identity or lexical base is not sufficiently familiar.

### PREMATURE
- multiple estimated signs;
- simultaneous hidden governor plus estimated ending as new operations;
- disputed analysis;
- full weak-verb taxonomy required;
- nominal i‘rāb taqdiri imported into the same competence.

## Stress test 1 — Distinctness from K55

K55 uses **visible positive evidence**: overt ḍammah.

K57 requires the learner to infer the same raf‘ state **without a visible ḍammah**. The evidence architecture is therefore distinct.

## Stress test 2 — Distinctness from K50

K50 reconstructs a **deleted lexical weak segment under jazm**.

K57 does not restore a missing lexical segment. The weak-final letter remains; what is estimated is the **grammatical ending**. This is a different representational operation.

## Stress test 3 — Should alif/wāw/yā’ become separate K?

**No at core architecture.** Their traditional explanatory causes may differ, but the learner operation targeted by K57 is the same. Differences are handled through internal staging unless future assessment evidence demonstrates genuinely separate cognitive operations.

## Stress test 4 — Must the learner name `التعذر` or `الثقل`?

Not for core K57. The learner must identify `ضمة مقدرة`. Naming and explaining the phonological cause belongs to later rule-depth/mastery work.

## Assessment design

Core prompt:

`ما حالة الفعل المضارع؟ وهل علامة رفعه ظاهرة أم مقدرة؟`

Expected response identifies raf‘ and `ضمة مقدرة`. A deeper prompt about the reason for estimation is outside core K57.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH ALIF-FIRST INTERNAL STAGING.**

K57 is distinct and teachable without splitting weak-final classes into separate competencies or importing the full theory of estimated i‘rāb.

## Freeze recommendation

> **K57 — Mengenali raf‘ pada fi‘il mudhari‘ mu‘tal akhir melalui `الضمة المقدرة`, dimulai dari bentuk alif-final yang paling transparan dan diperluas secara terkontrol.**
