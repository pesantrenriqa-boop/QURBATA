# Evidence Gate K50 — Jazm by `حذف حرف العلة` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K49 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K50 target

Learner can recognize, in a validated simple jussive environment, a familiar **weak-final mudhari‘** whose final weak letter is absent because of jazm.

Core abstraction:

`فعل مضارع معتل الآخر` + environment already known → final weak letter deleted as **علامة الجزم**.

K50 is a one-step local reconstruction competence, not a full lesson on weak verbs.

## Hard admission rules

PASS examples require:
- verb is confidently mudhari‘;
- its non-jussive/base weak-final form is already familiar enough to the learner or explicitly pre-established;
- jussive environment is already identifiable through prior K;
- only one final weak letter needs to be mentally restored;
- no deletion of nūn;
- no additional irregular morphology;
- no advanced Qur'anic orthographic explanation;
- no disputed parsing.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
Base weak-final form is transparent; jussive environment is simple; deleted weak letter is the only new operation.

### PASS-B
Additional material is cumulative-clean through K49.

### REVIEW-BASE
The jussive analysis may be valid, but the learner would not know the unreduced base form without a separate morphology lesson.

### REVIEW-ORTH
Qur'anic orthography/phonology obscures the simple pedagogical contrast.

### REVIEW-MORPH
The occurrence requires additional weak-verb morphology beyond one final deletion.

### PREMATURE
- deletion of nūn;
- irregular/compound morphology;
- multiple missing elements;
- non-local reconstruction;
- higher discourse dependency.

## Anti-leak rule

At K50, learner only needs to answer:

> `ما الفعل المضارع؟ وما حرف العلة المحذوف في آخره بسبب الجزم؟`

Do not require:
- classification of all weak-verb types;
- full sarf paradigm;
- all environments that cause jazm;
- comparative rules across waw/ya/alif beyond the validated occurrence;
- orthographic history.

## Distinctness from K49

K49 recognizes an overt visible sign: sukūn.

K50 adds a different cognitive operation: **recover a missing final segment from a familiar paradigm and connect the deletion to jazm**.

Thus K50 is not merely another example of K49.

## Freeze criteria

K50 can freeze only if:
1. enough examples have a transparent base form;
2. deleted weak letter can be recovered locally;
3. no full weak-verb theory is necessary;
4. every other operation is ≤ K49;
5. clean evidence is not padded with morphologically opaque examples.
