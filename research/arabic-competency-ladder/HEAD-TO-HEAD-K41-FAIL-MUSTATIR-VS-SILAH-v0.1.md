# Head-to-Head K41 — Fa'il Mustatir vs Isim Maushul + Silah v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K40 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Question

Setelah K40, operasi struktural mana yang paling ringan dan paling cumulative-clean:

A. inferensi **fa'il mustatir dasar**, atau  
B. menghubungkan **isim maushul dengan silah minimal**?

---

## Candidate A — Fa'il Mustatir Dasar

### New operation
Learner tidak lagi hanya membaca unsur yang tampak, tetapi menyimpulkan subjek gramatikal yang tidak dieja sebagai isim terpisah dari bentuk fi'il yang tervalidasi.

### Available foundation
- pengenalan fi'il sudah lebih awal;
- fa'il zhahir sudah tersedia;
- dhamir sudah tersedia;
- hubungan fi'il–fa'il sudah tersedia;
- K37/K40 sudah memperkuat disiplin membedakan morphology recognition dari full construction.

### Minimal scope
K41-A hanya boleh menggunakan occurrence di mana:
- fi'ilnya jelas dan sudah berada dalam morphology scope sebelumnya;
- tidak ada fa'il zhahir setelah fi'il;
- subjek dapat dipulihkan secara langsung dari bentuk verbal yang sudah dikuasai;
- tidak perlu ellipsis wacana, ta'wil, passive analysis, atau nested clause;
- referent discourse tidak perlu dianalisis untuk menetapkan fungsi sintaksis dasarnya.

### Main risk
Bila terlalu luas, K41 berubah menjadi full conjugation/person-paradigm lesson. Karena itu target bukan 'hafal semua dhamir mustatir', tetapi **mendeteksi bahwa slot fa'il terisi secara implisit pada occurrence sederhana**.

**Judgement: HIGH, manageable with strict morphology boundary.**

---

## Candidate B — Isim Maushul + Silah Minimal

### New operation
Learner menghubungkan isim maushul yang sudah dikenali dengan klausa/frasa silah yang melengkapinya dan menentukan batas minimal unit relatif.

### Available foundation
- recognition isim maushul sudah tersedia;
- basic nominal/verbal structures sudah tersedia;
- multiple relation types are now frozen.

### Minimal scope
Only examples where:
- isim maushul is explicit;
- silah is short and structurally simple;
- no complex embedded clause;
- no difficult resumptive pronoun analysis required.

### Main risk
Even a short silah introduces **clause-boundary/linkage**. Many occurrences also involve `عائد` explicit or implicit, prepositional relations, verbal subjects, or embedded dependencies. Thus surface shortness can hide a larger dependency graph.

**Judgement: HIGH, but structurally heavier than basic hidden-subject inference.**

---

## Head-to-head

| Dimension | Fa'il mustatir dasar | Isim maushul + silah |
|---|---|---|
| New operation | infer hidden subject slot | link head to relative clause |
| Dependency breadth | morphology + verb-subject | clause boundary + linkage + possible عائد |
| Risk of nested structure | low in curated core | medium/high |
| Clean occurrence potential | high | moderate/high |
| Abstraction | moderate | moderate/high |
| Natural continuation of earlier K | very strong | strong |

## Decision

**K41-CAND remains fa'il mustatir dasar.**

K42 provisionally remains isim maushul + silah minimal.

The decision is not a traditional nahwu sequence claim. It is a dependency claim: recovering one unexpressed argument in a tightly controlled verb pattern is lighter than delimiting and linking a relative clause.

## Evidence gate required before freeze

K41 must prove a bank of occurrences where the hidden-subject inference itself is the only new operation. Classify:

- **PASS-A:** simple verb, no explicit fa'il, subject recoverable directly from already-mastered morphology;
- **PASS-B:** additional material uses only K1–K40;
- **REVIEW-MORPH:** recovery requires morphology not yet explicitly inside scope;
- **REVIEW-REF:** grammatical subject is clear but discourse referent identification is nontrivial;
- **PREMATURE:** passive, nested clause, complex ellipsis, relative/conditional dependency, or other K42+ operation.

## Anti-leak rule

For K41 teaching, the learner only needs to answer:

> 'Apakah fi'il ini mempunyai fa'il yang tidak ditulis sebagai isim zhahir, dan siapa/apa grammatical person-nya menurut bentuk yang sudah dikuasai?'

They do **not** yet need to reconstruct full discourse antecedence, advanced i'rab, or all conjugational paradigms.

## Next

Build occurrence-specific K41 clean bank. If clean yield is robust, freeze K41; otherwise narrow morphology scope rather than importing K42+ dependencies.
