# FINAL GATE K58 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K57 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K58 — Mengenali koordinasi dua klausa Qurani yang sama-sama utuh melalui satu penghubung eksplisit, dimulai dari `و` yang tervalidasi sebagai koordinator antarklausa.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify the first complete clause;
2. identify the second complete clause;
3. locate the overt coordinator;
4. classify the relation as clause-level coordination rather than phrase-level attachment.

## Core staging

- Stage A: transparent inter-clausal `و`;
- Stage B: other overt coordinators only after transfer validation.

Different conjunction forms do not automatically receive separate K numbers.

## Included

- two independently analyzable clauses;
- one overt validated coordinator;
- clear same-level attachment;
- cumulative clause-internal analysis through K57.

## Excluded

- phrase-level coordination as K58 evidence;
- full `حروف العطف` inventory;
- nested coordination;
- cross-conjunct ellipsis;
- disputed wāw functions;
- advanced discourse-semantic distinctions.

## Assessment prompt

`أين الجملة الأولى؟ وأين الجملة الثانية؟ وما الرابط بينهما؟`

Expected response identifies the two clause spans and their overt coordinator.

## Gate results

- distinctness from conditional linkage: PASS;
- clause-vs-phrase boundary: PASS;
- overt-link containment: PASS;
- conjunction anti-fragmentation: PASS;
- one-relation rule: PASS.

**Decision: K58 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

## Architectural significance

K58 begins an inter-clausal relation layer. Earlier competencies can analyze material inside a clause and selected asymmetric relations such as condition→response; K58 adds recognition of two peer clauses connected overtly.
