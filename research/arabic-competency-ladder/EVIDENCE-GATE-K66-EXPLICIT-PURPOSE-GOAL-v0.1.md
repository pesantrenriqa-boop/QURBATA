# EVIDENCE GATE K66 — Explicit Purpose / Goal Relation v0.1

**Status:** ACTIVE EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K65 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Candidate

> **K66-CAND — Mengenali relasi tujuan/غاية antara suatu tindakan atau proposisi Qurani dan tujuan yang dinyatakan secara eksplisit melalui konstruksi yang tervalidasi.**

## Core operation

`ACTION / PROPOSITION A → PURPOSE / GOAL B`

Learner identifies:
1. the action/proposition whose purpose is being expressed;
2. the overt purpose-bearing construction;
3. the intended goal/end expressed by B.

## Critical containment

K66 is relation-based, not a lesson on one particle. A surface `لـ`, `كي`, or other candidate marker does not automatically pass. Its occurrence-level function must be validated as purposive.

The learner must not be required to discover a new hidden `أن`, derive a new naṣb mechanism, or learn a new particle taxonomy in order to solve the core item.

## PASS requirements

- A is overt and locally identifiable;
- B is overt enough to state the intended goal;
- purpose relation is explicitly signaled by a validated construction;
- all morphology/mood needed to read the construction is already ≤ K65;
- no cause-vs-purpose ambiguity remains;
- no result-vs-purpose ambiguity remains;
- no ellipsis reconstruction is required;
- one semantic operation solves the item.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — transparent overt purpose
A clear action/proposition is followed by an overt, validated purpose construction and an explicit goal.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean purpose
A or B contains prior-level complexity, but the purposive dependency remains local and unambiguous.

### REVIEW-CAUSE
The construction can reasonably be read as reason/cause rather than intended goal without later knowledge.

### REVIEW-RESULT
B could be consequence rather than intended purpose.

### REVIEW-MOOD
The semantic relation is clear, but solving it requires teaching a new naṣb/subjunctive mechanism not yet available.

### PREMATURE
- purpose inferred only from tafsir/context;
- hidden-marker reconstruction;
- long-distance purpose scope;
- rhetorical purpose requiring advanced balaghah;
- several purposes or nested dependencies at once.

## Distinctness

### vs K64 cause/reason
K64 asks: **why is/was A so?** — B supplies grounds/reason.

K66 asks: **for what intended end is A done?** — B supplies goal.

### vs K63 result/consequence
K63 interprets B as what follows as a result of A.

K66 interprets B as the intended end motivating/directing A. A goal need not actually be realized, which distinguishes intention from consequence.

### vs mood morphology
Verb mood is form-level grammar. K66 is a semantic relation between action and intended end. Mood evidence may support the construction but is not the competency target.
