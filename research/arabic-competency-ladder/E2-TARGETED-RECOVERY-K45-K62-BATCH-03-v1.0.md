## K45 — deleted resumptive (‘ā’id maḥżūf)
Decision: HOLD at E1+/E2-candidate.

Reason:
- A relative pronoun and its ṣilat al-mawṣūl can be verified structurally, but K45 specifically requires evidence that an expected resumptive element is omitted and locally reconstructable.
- Presence of REL + relative clause alone is not sufficient evidence for deletion.
- E2 promotion requires multiple independent Qur'anic cases in which the omitted element has a unique syntactic slot and reconstruction does not depend on translation or broad tafsir inference.

Closure gate:
1. explicit antecedent/relative head;
2. complete relative-clause boundary;
3. identifiable empty argument slot;
4. unique local reconstruction of the resumptive;
5. at least one negative control with overt resumptive;
6. independent traditional-i‘rāb/tafsir confirmation for deletion-sensitive cases.

## K62 — high-level semantic/discourse relation
Decision: PROMOTE E1+ → E2.

Rationale:
- The normalized bank now restricts K62 to explicit, locally recoverable relation cues rather than inferred thematic similarity.
- Positive evidence must expose the relation through an overt connective/structural cue and survive removal of translation glosses.
- Negative controls include adjacent/co-occurring propositions without the target relation and semantically related propositions whose relation is not structurally licensed.
- This keeps K62 below capstone K65 and prevents ontology-level co-occurrence from being treated as proof.

Assessment boundary:
A learner is assessed on recognizing the licensed relation in a minimal Qur'anic span, not on producing a global tafsir or discourse interpretation.
