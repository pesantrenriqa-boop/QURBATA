# RESCAN K68 — Strict Stop Gate v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE / STOP-GATE REVIEW  
**Baseline:** K1–K67 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K68 is not presumed to exist. The ladder must stop if remaining candidates are only transfer, taxonomy, or higher-theory variants of already frozen operations.

A K68 candidate must satisfy all of the following:
1. genuinely new learner operation;
2. independently assessable;
3. Qur'anic evidence with stable local interpretation;
4. dependency cost contained by K1–K67;
5. not merely a new marker for an existing relation;
6. not merely a traditional subcategory;
7. not better classified as mastery/transfer.

# Stop-gate finding

After K67, the remaining obvious candidates are increasingly one of three types:

1. **transfer breadth** — new marker, same operation;
2. **rule depth** — traditional subtype of an existing operation;
3. **advanced inference** — genuinely new but dependency-heavy enough to justify a later advanced tier.

No candidate currently satisfies the strict K68 criteria strongly enough to freeze immediately.

## Decision

**DO NOT ASSIGN K68 YET.**

Instead, transition from expansion mode to **closure audit mode**.

The working horizon K70 was never a required target; it was only a planning horizon. The corpus-driven ladder may legitimately end at K67 if closure audit confirms:
- coverage is broad enough;
- major operation families are represented;
- unresolved candidates are advanced/transfer rather than missing core prerequisites;
- no dependency inversion exists;
- no frozen K is redundant.

## Required next artifact

Create a **CORE-LADDER-CLOSURE-AUDIT-K1-K67** covering:
- coverage by domain;
- dependency integrity;
- overlap/redundancy;
- frontier candidates not promoted;
- stop criterion;
- whether K67 should be treated as the provisional endpoint of the core ladder.

## Provisional conclusion

> **K67 is now the provisional endpoint of the core ladder pending closure audit.**

Do not create K68 simply to approach K70.
