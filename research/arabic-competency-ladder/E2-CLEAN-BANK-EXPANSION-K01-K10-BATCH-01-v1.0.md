## Promotion rule

No K is promoted to E2 merely because several surface examples exist. Promotion requires:
1. multiple exact surah:ayah references;
2. minimal target spans;
3. same learner operation across examples;
4. no hidden later prerequisite required to solve the target;
5. at least one boundary/negative-control note;
6. enough variation to show repeatability rather than memorization of one phrase.
