# Head-to-Head K26–K27 v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — NON-AUTHORITATIVE

## Candidates

- K26-CAND — fa'il mustatir dasar
- K27-CAND — isim maushul + silah minimal

## A. Fa'il Mustatir Dasar

Dependencies:
- fi'il madhi/mudhari' recognition;
- verbal relation already known;
- awareness that subject need not be an overt noun;
- person/number/gender cues on the verb become relevant.

Strengths:
- extremely common in Arabic verbal clauses;
- unlocks many Qur'anic verbal examples previously excluded from early stages;
- logically extends K10 after overt fa'il.

Burdens:
- target element is not an independent token;
- requires inference from morphology/context;
- must distinguish hidden subject from attached subject morphology where relevant;
- passive structures must remain excluded.

**Judgement:** HIGH VALUE, HIGH INFERENCE BURDEN.

## B. Isim Maushul + Minimal Silah

Dependencies:
- K23 relative-pronoun recognition;
- a complete minimal clause after the relative pronoun;
- clause boundary recognition;
- returning pronoun/linkage may be explicit or implicit depending on construction.

Possible minimal types:
- relative pronoun + simple verbal clause whose internal grammar is already <= current K;
- relative pronoun + simple nominal clause if occurrence supports it.

Burdens:
- silah is not optional semantically/syntactically;
- requires clause segmentation;
- many Qur'anic examples quickly bring hidden subject, object suffix, PP attachment, coordination, or more complex verbal morphology;
- relative linkage can be structurally less transparent than a single hidden subject.

**Judgement:** VERY IMPORTANT, BUT CLAUSE-COMPLEX.

## Head-to-Head

| Criterion | Fa'il Mustatir | Minimal Silah |
|---|---|---|
| New token category | no | no |
| Main burden | inference from verb | clause boundary + linkage |
| Locality | high at verb | multi-token clause |
| Prematurity risk | medium | high |
| Unlocks corpus examples | very high | very high |
| Clean extraction | moderate-high | moderate |

**Winner for earlier sequence: K26 fa'il mustatir.**

Before freezing K26, also compare against:
- fi'il amr recognition;
- simple negation particle recognition;

These are recognition nodes and may be lighter than fa'il mustatir. They should not be ignored merely because nominal/verbal relation track is already advanced.
