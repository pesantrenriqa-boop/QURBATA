# FINAL GATE K65 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K64 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K65 — Mengenali relasi pengecualian/pembatasan melalui `إلا` eksplisit dengan menentukan domain yang dibatasi dan unsur yang dikecualikan/dikhususkan dalam konstruksi Qurani terkontrol.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. locate overt `إلا`;
2. identify the relevant general domain/scope;
3. identify the excluded/restricted element;
4. explain how `إلا` narrows the domain.

## Included

- overt `إلا`;
- local, clear scope/domain;
- overt exception/restricted element;
- occurrence-specific exception/restriction reading;
- cumulative analysis through K64.

## Excluded

- full `باب الاستثناء` taxonomy;
- mandatory classification as `تام/ناقص` or `موجب/غير موجب`;
- new post-`إلا` i‘rāb rules as a prerequisite;
- implicit exception without overt marker;
- nested exclusions;
- disputed scope.

## Assessment prompt

`ما المجال العام؟ وما الذي خصصته أو استثنته «إلا»؟`

Expected response identifies both the domain and the excluded/restricted element.

## Gate results

- distinctness from contrast: PASS;
- distinctness from negation: PASS;
- overt-marker containment: PASS;
- anti-taxonomy: PASS;
- scope filter: PASS;
- one-operation rule: PASS.

**Decision: K65 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

## Architectural significance

K65 adds scope restriction to the discourse-semantic layer. It trains the learner to interpret how an overt marker changes the set of included alternatives, rather than merely translating a particle or classifying an exception subtype.
