# RESCAN K65 — Purpose vs Exception/Restriction vs Elaboration vs Omitted Reference v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE SELECTION  
**Baseline:** K1–K64 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K65 must introduce one genuinely new learner operation with clean Qur'anic evidence. It must not merely continue the discourse-relation list mechanically.

## Candidate A — purpose/goal relation

Potential operation:

`Action/statement A → overt purpose marker/construction → intended goal B`

### Strengths
- semantically distinct from cause/reason (K64) and result/consequence (K63);
- highly useful for Qur'anic comprehension;
- naturally assessed by asking “for what purpose?”

### Risks
- many purpose constructions interact with `لام التعليل`, nasb, hidden `أن`, or other morphology already distributed across K52–K53;
- could accidentally require both relation recognition and new mood analysis;
- some occurrences are semantically purpose-like but grammatically ambiguous.

### Verdict
**PROMISING BUT DEPENDENCY-SENSITIVE.** Advance only if a subtype can be isolated where the purpose relation is overt and all morphology is already mastered.

---

## Candidate B — exception/restriction with overt `إلا`

Potential operation:

`set/proposition domain → إلا → identify excluded/restricted member or proposition`

### Strengths
- overt, visually stable marker;
- introduces set-restriction rather than another clause-link type;
- strong Qur'anic utility;
- can begin with transparent positive complete structures;
- learner operation is explicit: establish domain, locate exception marker, identify excluded element.

### Risks
- Arabic exception theory has many subtypes (`تام/ناقص`, `موجب/غير موجب`, بدل, حصر, etc.);
- some Qur'anic `إلا` occurrences function closer to restriction (`حصر`) than textbook exception;
- case analysis after `إلا` could overload the core.

### Containment
K65 core can be restricted to **transparent overt exclusion/restriction** where:
1. the pre-`إلا` domain is explicit;
2. the post-`إلا` element is overt;
3. learner only identifies the restriction/exclusion relation;
4. no full i‘rab taxonomy is required.

### Verdict
**STRONGEST CANDIDATE.**

---

## Candidate C — elaboration/explanation relation

Potential operation:

`general statement A → elaborating statement B`

### Strengths
- important discourse relation;
- common in Qur'anic rhetoric.

### Risks
- often lacks a unique overt marker;
- can depend on tafsir/discourse interpretation;
- difficult to assess with one clean operation.

### Verdict
**DEFER.** Insufficiently marker-stable for K65 core.

---

## Candidate D — controlled omitted `العائد`

### Strengths
- natural extension from K60–K61;
- true inferential step.

### Risks
- still requires null-element reconstruction plus licensing knowledge;
- may become a rule-list about deletion positions;
- dependency burden higher than overt `إلا` restriction.

### Verdict
**DEFER AGAIN.** Keep in frontier queue.

---

# Selection

## K65-CAND

> **Mengenali relasi pengecualian/pembatasan melalui `إلا` yang eksplisit dalam konstruksi Qurani sederhana, dengan menentukan domain yang dibatasi dan unsur yang dikecualikan/dikhususkan.**

Core representation:

`[domain/set/proposition] + إلا + [excluded/restricted element]`

## Learner operation

1. identify the overt `إلا`;
2. determine the explicit domain/set/proposition before it;
3. identify the overt element after it;
4. state the exclusion/restriction relation without requiring full exception-case taxonomy.

## Initial PASS scope

- overt `إلا`;
- explicit pre-`إلا` domain;
- overt post-`إلا` element;
- relation semantically transparent;
- no new i‘rab rule needed;
- no disputed `إلا` function;
- no nested exception.

## Excluded from core

- full `باب الاستثناء` classification;
- case alternations after `إلا`;
- `إلا` analyses disputed between exception/restriction/other functions;
- incomplete/negative structures that require advanced theory;
- multiple exception layers.

## Decision

**Advance K65-CAND (`إلا` exclusion/restriction) to distinctness + evidence gate.**

Purpose/goal remains a high-value frontier candidate for K66 or later once a morphology-safe subtype is isolated.
