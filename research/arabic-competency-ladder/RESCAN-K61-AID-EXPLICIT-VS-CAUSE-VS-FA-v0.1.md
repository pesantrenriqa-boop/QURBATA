# RESCAN K61 — Explicit `العائد` vs Causal Relation vs `ف` Sequencing v0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE SELECTION  
**Baseline:** K1–K60 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

K61 must add a new operation after K60 relative-clause boundary recognition. It must not merely deepen terminology or enumerate conjunction functions.

## Candidate A — explicit `العائد` inside `صلة الموصول`

Potential operation:

`اسم موصول → صلة الموصول → ضمير عائد ظاهر → link pronoun back to antecedent`

### Strengths
- directly builds on K60;
- introduces anaphoric dependency, not just clause boundary;
- overt pronoun can provide positive surface evidence;
- highly useful for Qur'anic comprehension;
- learner operation is clear: identify the resumptive element and resolve its reference.

### Risks
- `العائد` may be subject, object, attached pronoun, prepositional complement, or omitted;
- full traditional theory would overload the K;
- some occurrences have reference ambiguity or long-distance dependencies.

### Containment
Core K61 should use only:
- already-familiar explicit pronoun forms;
- short relative clauses validated under K60;
- locally obvious antecedent (`اسم الموصول`);
- one explicit resumptive dependency;
- no omitted `عائد`.

### Verdict
**STRONGEST CANDIDATE.**

## Candidate B — causal/result relation between clauses

Potential operation:

`clause A → connector/context → clause B as result/consequence`

### Strengths
- semantically powerful;
- advances discourse understanding.

### Risks
- surface marking is heterogeneous;
- often overlaps with `ف`, condition-response, purpose, lexical causation;
- clean assessment may require more semantics than syntax.

### Verdict
**DEFER pending marker-specific evidence.**

---

## Candidate C — close sequencing with `ف`

Potential operation:

`clause 1 + ف + clause 2 → close succession`

### Strengths
- natural contrast with K59 `ثم`.

### Risks
- `ف` remains highly multifunctional;
- K48/K54 already use `فاء جواب الشرط`;
- classification burden may exceed the sequencing operation itself.

### Verdict
**DEFER.** First build a function-disambiguation gate for `ف` before numbering.

---

## Candidate D — full relative-pronoun inventory

### Verdict
**REJECT as competency.** Inventory memorization belongs to vocabulary/morphological mastery, not a distinct learner operation.

# Selection

## K61-CAND

> **Mengenali `العائد` eksplisit di dalam `صلة الموصول` dan menghubungkannya kembali kepada `اسم الموصول` dalam konstruksi Qurani sederhana.**

Core representation:

`[اسم موصول] + [صلة ... ضمير عائد ظاهر ...]`

Learner operation:
1. identify the relative marker already supported by K60;
2. locate the relative clause;
3. identify one overt pronoun inside it;
4. resolve that pronoun as referring back to the relative antecedent.

## Distinctness from K60

K60 answers:
- where does the relative clause begin and end?

K61 answers:
- which overt element inside the clause reconnects it to the relative antecedent?

Thus K61 adds **reference resolution/anaphoric linkage**, not another boundary task.

## Initial evidence policy

PASS only when:
- `اسم موصول` is overt and familiar;
- `صلة` boundary is clean;
- one overt resumptive pronoun is unambiguous;
- antecedent is local and unique;
- pronoun form itself is already familiar;
- no omitted resumptive or competing antecedent.

REVIEW/PREMATURE:
- deleted `العائد`;
- long-distance or ambiguous reference;
- nested relative clauses;
- multiple pronouns with competing antecedents;
- cases requiring advanced case/governance analysis merely to find the resumptive.

## Decision

**Advance Candidate A to K61 evidence/distinctness gate.**

Causal relations and `ف` sequencing remain frontier candidates, not discarded topics.
