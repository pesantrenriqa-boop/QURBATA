## K31 — hamzat al-istifham recognition

**Decision: HOLD E1 / TARGETED SEARCH CONTINUES.**

QS 2:6 `أَأَنذَرْتَهُمْ` is not accepted as a clean positive example for ordinary interrogative hamzah. The grammatical analysis identifies the initial hamzah here as **همزة التسوية** (hamzah of equalization), not a straightforward information-seeking question. This occurrence is therefore retained as a high-value negative/boundary example for later discrimination.

E2 promotion requires several unambiguous interrogative-hamzah occurrences plus lexical/root-hamzah exclusions.

Boundary control:
- `إِنَّمَا` remains a related but structurally modified environment because `ما` can block the normal governance pattern; it is not used as the beginner canonical item.
- conditional `إِنْ` remains excluded.

**New maturity: E2.**

## K37 — `كان` family recognition

**Decision: PROMOTE TO E2.**

The recognition bank now has family/form diversity rather than one memorized surface form. Controlled examples include:
- QS 24:62 `كَانُوا مَعَهُ` — finite plural form of `كان`;
- QS 2:64 `لَكُنْتُمْ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ` — inflected `كان` form in a conditional environment;
- established Qur'anic formulae of `وَكَانَ اللَّهُ ...` provide third-person singular recognition diversity.

K37 remains recognition-only. Governance of ism/khabar and syntactic effect belongs to later K40 and is not required for K37 assessment.

**New maturity: E2.**
