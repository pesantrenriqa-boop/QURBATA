# Rescan K49 — Basic Jazm vs `فاء جواب الشرط` Rule Generalization v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K48 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan operasi baru paling ringan setelah K48 dengan membandingkan:

1. efek **jazm dasar** pada fi'il mudhari' dalam struktur syarth sederhana;
2. generalisasi kaidah **kapan جواب الشرط memakai فاء**;
3. kandidat lokal lain yang mungkin lebih ringan.

Prinsip tetap:
- satu operasi baru per K;
- tidak mengubah K menjadi satu bab nahwu penuh;
- occurrence-specific evidence lebih penting daripada urutan kitab;
- transfer/application tidak otomatis menjadi K baru.

---

## Candidate A — Basic jazm effect in simple conditional verbs

### Foundation
- K47: identifies simple conditional structure;
- K48: identifies `فاء جواب الشرط` when overt;
- earlier verbal morphology competencies provide basic verb recognition.

### New operation
Observe that a mudhari' verb in a validated jussive conditional environment changes its surface ending/form because it is **majzum**.

### Critical problem
`جزم` is not one surface pattern. Qur'anic occurrences may show:
- **السكون**;
- **حذف حرف العلة**;
- **حذف النون**.

Teaching all three together risks turning K49 into a mini-paradigm rather than one atomic competency.

### Atomic option
Restrict K49 initially to **one transparent jazm realization**, ideally final sukun on an otherwise familiar sound-form mudhari', while other realizations remain later expansions.

### Dependency burden
Moderate:
- learner already knows the conditional relation;
- the new operation is morphology under governance;
- no new clause boundary is required.

**Judgement: VERY HIGH if scope is restricted to transparent sukun-type jazm.**

---

## Candidate B — Rule-level generalization: when jawab takes fā'

### Foundation
- K47: identifies condition and response;
- K48: recognizes overt `فاء جواب الشرط` occurrence-specifically.

### New operation
Generalize across response types and predict when the جواب should be linked by `فـ`.

### Dependency burden
High:
- requires categorizing several response-clause types;
- often references nominal clauses, طلبية, جامدة, particles such as `قد`, `لن`, `ما`, `سوف`, etc.;
- easily becomes a memorized rule list rather than one minimal operation.

**Judgement: DISTINCT but substantially heavier than restricted jazm recognition.**

---

## Candidate C — Other local operations

Potentially lighter items were checked conceptually:
- transferring K46 clause-as-predicate to other already-mastered hosts;
- more inventory of conditional particles;
- additional fā' lexical/function inventory.

These fail anti-inflation or anti-fragmentation unless they introduce a new relation. They should remain evidence/application expansions, not new K by default.

---

## Decision

**K49-CAND — mengenali efek jazm dasar dengan tanda sukun yang transparan pada fi'il mudhari' dalam struktur syarth sederhana.**

This is intentionally narrower than the traditional topic `علامات الجزم`.

## K49 provisional learner target

Given a validated K47 conditional occurrence containing a familiar sound mudhari' verb:
1. identify the verb;
2. observe the final sukun/change relevant to the occurrence;
3. connect that surface form to the simple conditional jussive environment.

## Hard exclusions

K49 does not yet teach:
- all signs of jazm;
- حذف حرف العلة;
- حذف النون;
- full i'rab terminology beyond what is needed;
- all أدوات الجزم;
- jazm outside the restricted conditional environment;
- advanced morphological alternations.

## Evidence classes for next gate

- **PASS-A:** sound mudhari', transparent final sukun, simple K47 conditional relation, no higher dependency.
- **PASS-B:** cumulative-clean additional material only.
- **REVIEW-ELLISION:** jazm by حذف حرف العلة or حذف النون.
- **REVIEW-MORPH:** form requires morphology beyond prior scope.
- **PREMATURE:** nested conditional, disputed parsing, or another new syntactic operation.

## Research consequence

If the clean Qur'anic bank for sukun-type jazm is too small or pedagogically weak, do not force K49. Reconsider whether a broader but still atomic mood-recognition competence is possible, or whether another structural node should precede it.

## Next

Open occurrence-specific evidence gate for restricted sukun-type jazm and compare actual clean yield against the broader jazm alternatives before freeze.
