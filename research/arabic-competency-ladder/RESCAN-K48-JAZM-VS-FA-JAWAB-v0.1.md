# Rescan K48 — Jazm Effect vs `فاء جواب الشرط` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K47 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan operasi belajar paling ringan setelah K47, ketika learner sudah mampu mengenali `أداة الشرط → جملة الشرط → جواب الشرط` tanpa harus memahami efek morfologisnya.

---

## Candidate A — basic jazm effect in simple conditional verbs

### New operation
Recognize that, in a validated simple jussive conditional construction, the conditional environment can affect the form of the relevant mudari' verb(s).

### Dependency burden
Moderate–high:
- requires prior verb morphology awareness;
- surface realization of jazm varies;
- sukun, deletion of weak letter, and deletion of nun are not one surface pattern;
- if introduced broadly, K48 becomes a full `علامات الجزم` chapter.

### Minimal possible scope
A narrow K could begin with only **overt sukun-type jazm** in clean examples, while deletion-based signs remain later.

**Judgement: DISTINCT, but needs sub-scope discipline.**

---

## Candidate B — `فاء جواب الشرط` recognition

### New operation
Recognize overt `فاء` that introduces the response clause in a validated conditional construction.

### Dependency burden
Low–moderate:
- token is overt;
- K47 already provides condition→response linkage;
- learner only adds recognition of one boundary marker.

### Main risk
Not every `فـ` is `فاء جواب الشرط`; occurrence-specific disambiguation is mandatory.

Also, a marker-only K risks lexical inflation unless the presence of `فاء` changes a meaningful parsing operation (namely: response-boundary identification under conditions where direct verbal جواب is not used).

**Judgement: HIGH, but distinctness must be defended.**

---

## Head-to-head analysis

`فاء جواب الشرط` is lighter on surface dependency than jazm morphology. However, K47 already teaches answer-clause boundary/linkage. Therefore a new K is justified only if learner must perform a genuinely new operation: **identify a specialized response introducer and distinguish it from ordinary conjunction `فـ`**.

This distinction is meaningful and assessable, but still narrower than full conditional morphology.

## Provisional order

- **K48-CAND — recognize validated `فاء جواب الشرط` as a specialized response marker.**
- **K49-CAND — basic jazm effect in simple conditional mudari'**, probably beginning with the simplest visible sign rather than all علامات الجزم at once.

## Hard exclusions for K48

K48 does not teach:
- all uses of `فـ`;
- conditions requiring fa' jawab in full traditional taxonomy;
- semantic/rhetorical differences among response types;
- jazm morphology;
- omitted answer;
- nested conditionals.

## Distinctness rule

K48 survives only if evidence shows that identifying specialized `فاء الجواب` is not reducible to ordinary conjunction recognition already frozen. If it collapses into prior particle inventory, K48 must revert to basic jazm instead.

## Next

Run an evidence/distinctness gate for `فاء جواب الشرط` versus ordinary `فـ`. If the specialized parsing operation is robust, freeze K48; otherwise reject it and promote narrow jazm to K48.
