# Rescan K51 — `حذف النون` vs Rule-level `فاء جواب الشرط` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE

## Purpose

Menentukan operasi baru paling ringan setelah K50 dengan tetap menjaga:
- satu operasi baru per K;
- cumulative-only;
- anti-fragmentation;
- Qur'anic teachability.

---

## Candidate A — Jazm by `حذف النون`

### New operation
Recognize that a mudhari‘ form belonging to the five-verb pattern loses its final nūn in a validated jussive environment.

### Dependency burden
Moderate–high:
- learner must recognize the relevant person/number suffix pattern;
- distinguish the stable pronominal ending from the deleted nūn;
- connect deletion to jazm;
- avoid turning K51 into full `الأفعال الخمسة` paradigm instruction.

### Core possibility
K51 can remain atomic if restricted to already-familiar forms where the non-jussive comparator is supplied or firmly known.

**Judgement: HIGH candidate, but morphology-gated.**

---

## Candidate B — Generalization: when `جواب الشرط` takes `فـ`

### New operation
Move from occurrence-level recognition (K48) to rule-level prediction of when the conditional response requires fā’.

### Dependency burden
High:
- response type classification;
- nominal sentence, الطلب, جامد, particles, and other categories may be involved;
- several subrules would likely be bundled;
- risks becoming a traditional rule list rather than one atomic operation.

**Judgement: IMPORTANT but too broad for immediate K51 unless later decomposed.**

---

## Candidate C — Other local morphology/syntax operation

No currently identified candidate has lower dependency while remaining genuinely distinct and sufficiently evidenced. Therefore K51 frontier stays between A and B.

## Anti-overload test for `حذف النون`

K51 must **not** teach all five forms as a conjugation table. The competence can be defined as:

> Given a familiar mudhari‘ form from the five-verb pattern in a validated jussive environment, recognize that the expected final nūn is absent because of jazm.

The learner operation is local deletion recognition, parallel to K50 but with a different morphological dependency.

## Distinctness from K50

K50 restores a **lexical weak-final segment**.  
K51 restores a **grammatical nūn belonging to a finite inflectional pattern**.

These are not the same reconstruction. K51 therefore deserves separate testing.

## Decision

**K51-CAND — jazm by `حذف النون` in familiar five-verb forms.**

## Hard exclusions

K51 does not require:
- memorizing the complete `الأفعال الخمسة` table;
- nasb by deletion of nūn;
- all person/number paradigms;
- ambiguous suffix analysis;
- advanced orthography;
- rule-level `فاء جواب الشرط` generalization.

## Evidence classes for next gate

- **PASS-A:** familiar five-verb form; clear jussive environment; deleted nūn transparent.
- **PASS-B:** cumulative-clean extra material only.
- **REVIEW-PARADIGM:** form requires broader paradigm knowledge than core scope.
- **REVIEW-ORTH:** Qur'anic spelling obscures the target operation.
- **PREMATURE:** multiple morphological operations or nonlocal reconstruction required.
