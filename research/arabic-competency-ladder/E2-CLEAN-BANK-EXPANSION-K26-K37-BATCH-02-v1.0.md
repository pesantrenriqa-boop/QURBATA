## Promotion discipline

This batch does not promote a K merely because a familiar particle occurs often. Promotion requires discriminable recognition evidence and an explicit boundary against neighboring functions.

## Decisions

### K26 — interrogative recognition
**PROMOTE TO E2.**

Clean recognition families now include interrogative `مَنْ` and additional interrogative environments already established by the discovery/final-gate artifacts. QS 2:255 supplies a high-value discrimination span `مَنْ ذَا الَّذِي`: `مَنْ` is interrogative, `ذَا` demonstrative, `الَّذِي` relative. The learner operation is limited to recognizing the interrogative marker; the rest is masked/deferred.

Boundary: relative `مَنْ` is not automatically credited as interrogative.

### K27 — generic negation recognition
**PROMOTE TO E2.**

Repeated clean `لا` negative environments are available, including QS 2:255 `لَا تَأْخُذُهُ`, `وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ`, and `وَلَا يَئُودُهُ`. The bank explicitly distinguishes these from prohibitive `لا` targeted by K30.

Boundary: no jussive/prohibition analysis is required at K27.

### K30 — prohibitive `لا` recognition
**HOLD E1+ / E2 candidate.**

The surface marker overlaps K27. Promotion is withheld until the bank contains several normalized occurrences where prohibition is independently validated and the following imperfect is jussive. This avoids promoting by orthography alone.

### K31 — hamzat al-istifham recognition
**HOLD E1+ / E2 candidate.**

Promotion is withheld pending a compact normalized bank of genuine prefixed interrogative hamzah occurrences with lexical/root-hamzah counterexamples.

### K33 — `إنّ` recognition
**HOLD E1+ / E2 candidate.**

The family is strong in discovery evidence, but normalization must explicitly distinguish emphatic/accusative `إنّ` from conditional `إنْ` and other visually similar environments before E2.

### K37 — `كان` family recognition
**HOLD E1+ / E2 candidate.**

Recognition is strong, but formal E2 requires normalized diversity across more than one inflected member/form while governance remains deferred to K40.
