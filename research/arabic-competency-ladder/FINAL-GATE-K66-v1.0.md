# FINAL GATE K66 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K65 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K66 — Mengenali relasi tujuan/غاية antara suatu tindakan atau proposisi Qurani dan tujuan yang dinyatakan secara eksplisit melalui konstruksi yang tervalidasi.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify the action/proposition whose purpose is expressed;
2. identify the overt purposive construction;
3. identify the intended goal/end;
4. distinguish purpose from reason and consequence.

## Included

- overt/local action or proposition;
- overt validated purposive construction;
- explicit enough goal span;
- morphology and mood already supported ≤ K65;
- one action→goal dependency.

## Excluded

- hidden `أن` reconstruction as a new requirement;
- new naṣb mechanism;
- purpose inferred only from tafsir/context;
- cause/result ambiguity;
- multiple nested goals;
- advanced rhetorical purpose.

## Assessment prompt

`ما الفعل أو المعنى الذي ذُكرت له الغاية؟ وما الغاية المقصودة؟`

Expected response identifies the governed action/proposition and its intended goal.

## Gate results

- distinctness from K64 cause/reason: PASS;
- distinctness from K63 result: PASS;
- morphology containment: PASS;
- marker anti-fragmentation: PASS;
- one-relation rule: PASS.

**Decision: K66 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

## Architectural significance

The discourse-semantic layer now includes several directed relations:
- sequence;
- contrast/correction;
- result/consequence;
- cause/reason;
- exception/restriction;
- purpose/goal.

K66 adds intentional directionality: the learner recognizes what an action is aimed toward, not merely what caused it or resulted from it.
