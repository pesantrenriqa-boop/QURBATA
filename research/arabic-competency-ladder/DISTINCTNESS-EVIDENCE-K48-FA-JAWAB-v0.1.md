# Distinctness + Evidence Gate K48 — `فاء جواب الشرط` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K47 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Core question

Apakah mengenali `فاء جواب الشرط` merupakan kompetensi baru yang benar-benar distinct, atau hanya tambahan inventory untuk partikel `فـ` yang sudah dikenal?

## Distinctness test

Surface token `فـ` sendiri bukan alasan membuat K baru. K48 hanya layak jika learner melakukan operasi baru berikut:

1. sudah mengenali struktur syarth K47;
2. menentukan bahwa `فـ` tertentu bukan sekadar athaf/sequence marker;
3. mengidentifikasi `فـ` itu sebagai marker yang membuka **jawab syarth** pada occurrence tervalidasi;
4. menggunakan fungsi itu untuk menentukan boundary jawab.

Operasi ini bersifat **structural-functional disambiguation**, bukan lexical recognition.

**Result: DISTINCTNESS PASS.**

## Why it is not merely particle inventory

`فـ` dapat muncul dalam banyak fungsi. K48 menuntut learner memilih fungsi yang tepat berdasarkan posisi di dalam graph syarth yang sudah dikuasai. Jadi yang dipelajari bukan bentuk `فـ`, melainkan relasi:

`شرط` → **`فـ` marker** → `جواب الشرط`

## K48 provisional target

Learner can identify a validated `فاء جواب الشرط` in a simple conditional construction and use it to locate the beginning of the response clause.

## Hard exclusions

K48 does not teach:
- all semantic types of `فـ`;
- full rules governing when jawab must take `فـ`;
- full inventory of clause types requiring `فـ`;
- omitted jawab;
- oath–condition competition;
- advanced rhetorical relation;
- jazm morphology as a required analysis.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
Simple K47 conditional structure; `فـ` clearly opens an overt jawab; no new operation beyond K48.

### PASS-B
Additional material only from K1–K47.

### REVIEW-RULE
`فـ` is clearly جواب-related, but explaining *why* the rule requires it would need later theory. Can support recognition, not rule-production.

### REVIEW-AMBIG
Surface `فـ` could plausibly be another function without wider analysis.

### PREMATURE
Nested conditional, omitted jawab, complex oath interaction, disputed parsing, or dependence on advanced jazm/clause theory.

## Anti-leak assessment

For K48, learner only needs to answer:

> `أين فاء جواب الشرط؟ وأين يبدأ الجواب بعدها؟`

They do not yet need to state the full classical rule for obligatory conjunction with `فـ`.

## Comparison with jazm

Basic jazm remains heavier because its surface realization is morphologically variable (sukun, deletion of weak letter, deletion of nūn) and requires verb-form sensitivity. `فاء جواب الشرط` is overt and local once K47 boundary/linkage is available.

## Decision

**K48 remains `فاء جواب الشرط` recognition/function in simple validated conditions.**

Proceed to curated clean-bank stress test. Freeze only if false-positive control against ordinary `فـ` is robust.

## Provisional next frontier

- K49-CAND: basic jazm effect in simple condition verbs;
- other candidates must be rescanned after K48 to avoid automatic sequencing.
