## Purpose

Continue the recognition-node expansion after K26, K27 and K34 reached E2. This batch prioritizes K30, K31, K33 and K37, while preserving the distinction between surface recognition and later syntactic analysis.

## K30 — prohibitive `لا`

**Decision: HOLD E1 / strong E2 candidate.**

Required promotion evidence must show several occurrences where `لا` is specifically prohibitive and governs a jussive imperfect. Negative `لا` occurrences such as QS 2:255 `لَا تَأْخُذُهُ`, `وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ`, and `وَلَا يَئُودُهُ` are explicitly retained as **negative controls**, not positive K30 evidence.

This prevents leakage from K27 generic negation into K30 prohibition.

## K31 — hamzat al-istifham

**Decision: HOLD E1 / strong E2 candidate.**

Promotion requires several normalized interrogative-hamzah occurrences plus lexical/root-hamzah counterexamples. The bank must not promote a learner merely for recognizing initial `أ` orthography.

## K33 — `إنّ` recognition

**Decision: HOLD E1 / strong E2 candidate.**

The bank requires multiple explicit `إنّ` occurrences and contrast with conditional `إنْ`. Orthographic similarity alone is not sufficient. Full governance (`اسم إنّ` / `خبر إنّ`) remains outside this recognition node and belongs to later syntax.

## K37 — `كان` family recognition

**Decision: HOLD E1 / strong E2 candidate.**

The discovery/final-gate process supports family recognition, but formal E2 promotion still requires normalized diversity across more than one occurrence/member/form. Governance and case assignment remain deferred to K40.

## Verified negative-control laboratory: QS 2:255

QS 2:255 is retained as a high-value discrimination environment. Its grammar explicitly analyzes `مَن ذَا الَّذِي` as interrogative `مَن`, demonstrative `ذا`, and relative `الذي`; it also contains generic negative `لا` and repeated `إلا`. Therefore it is useful for separating K26/K27/K34 from neighboring recognition nodes, but it does **not** supply positive evidence for K30/K31/K33/K37.
