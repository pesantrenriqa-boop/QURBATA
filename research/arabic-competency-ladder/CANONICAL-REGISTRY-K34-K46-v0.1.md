## K34 — REC-ILLA
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali `إِلَّا` pada occurrence Qurani tervalidasi.
- **Domain:** recognition / particle.
- **Learner operation:** recognize the local invariant token without opening full istitsna'/hasr analysis.
- **Locked:** jenis istitsna', mustatsna, i'rab, hasr, clause scope.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K35 — REC-LAYSA
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali `لَيْسَ` sebagai family verbal/copular negatif dengan scope recognition terbatas.
- **Domain:** recognition / verbal-copular family.
- **Learner operation:** classify validated forms as members of the `ليس` family.
- **Locked:** اسم ليس, خبر ليس, resulting i'rab changes, full paradigm.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K36 — REC-LAW
- **Canonical competence:** recognition `لَوْ` pada occurrence tervalidasi sebagai marker conditional/counterfactual.
- **Domain:** recognition / conditional marker.
- **Learner operation:** recognize the token/function only.
- **Locked:** فعل الشرط, جواب الشرط, full clause-linking semantics.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K37 — REC-KANA-FAMILY
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali limited family `كان` pada occurrence Qurani tervalidasi.
- **Domain:** recognition / verbal-copular family.
- **Learner operation:** recognize selected `كان`-family forms without full tasrif or syntax.
- **Locked:** اسم كان, خبر كان, rafa'/nasb effect, full paradigm, full أخوات كان system.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K38 — REL-INNA-CORE
- **Canonical competence:** simple `إنّ + اسم إنّ + خبر إنّ` construction.
- **Domain:** clause / transformed nominal predication.
- **Learner operation:** identify `إنّ`, a clean اسم إنّ, a clean خبر إنّ, and the transformed nominal-predication frame.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K33 + K8 + cumulative nominal competencies as required.
- **Locked:** أخوات إنّ, emphatic lām, clausal khabar system, advanced fronting, full government paradigm.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K39 — REL-LAYSA-PRED
- **Canonical competence:** menganalisis konstruksi sederhana `ليس + اسم ليس + خبر ليس`.
- **Domain:** clause / negative nominal transformation.
- **Learner operation:** identify explicit اسم ليس and simple explicit خبر ليس and distinguish the frame from ordinary nominal predication.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K35 + K8.
- **Locked:** باء الزائدة in خبر ليس, omitted predicate, clausal khabar, broad nawāsikh theory.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K40 — REL-KANA-CORE
- **Canonical competence:** menganalisis konstruksi sederhana `كان + اسم كان + خبر كان`.
- **Domain:** clause / transformed nominal predication.
- **Learner operation:** identify `كان`, explicit اسم كان, simple explicit خبر كان, and relate the frame back to nominal predication.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K37 + K8.
- **Locked:** implicit اسم كان, clausal خبر كان, silah maushul, conditional linkage, advanced tense/aspect semantics, full tasrif.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K41 — REL-V-SUBJ-HIDDEN
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali fa'il mustatir dasar pada fi'il aktif sederhana.
- **Domain:** clause / verbal subject dependency.
- **Learner operation:** determine that the subject slot is grammatically filled despite no separate overt fa'il, with person/number recoverable from prior morphology.
- **Locked:** passive, full 14-slot paradigm, advanced hidden-pronoun classification, discourse-heavy antecedent recovery, nested/relative/conditional structures.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K42 — REL-MAWSUL-SILAH
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali hubungan isim maushul dengan silahnya dan menentukan batas silah sederhana.
- **Domain:** clause / relative-clause boundary.
- **Learner operation:** identify the explicit maushul and delimit the beginning/end of a minimal silah.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K23 + prior nominal/verbal relations; K41 when needed inside a short verbal silah.
- **Locked:** full `عائد` taxonomy, omitted resumptive, nested relative clauses, advanced antecedence, conditional linkage.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K43 — REL-RELATIVE-EXPLICIT-AID
- **Canonical competence:** mengidentifikasi `عائد` eksplisit dalam silah maushul sederhana.
- **Domain:** dependency / anaphoric linkage.
- **Learner operation:** identify the overt element inside an already-delimited silah that refers back to the maushul.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K42 + relevant prior attached-pronoun relation.
- **Locked:** omitted/estimated `عائد`, deletion conditions, advanced antecedent resolution, nested relatives.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K44 — EMPHATIC-LAM-INNA
- **Canonical competence:** identify a validated `لام التوكيد` inside an already-mastered simple `إنّ` construction and explain its emphatic function without changing K38 syntactic roles.
- **Domain:** discourse/semantic emphasis inside clause syntax.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K33 + K38.
- **Exclusions:** لام الأمر, lām jawab qasam, lām jawab syarth, prepositional/lexical lām, ambiguous lām.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K45 — BASIC-OMITTED-AID
- **Canonical competence:** detect in a validated simple relative construction that the resumptive relation is not overtly expressed and recover one local syntactic slot.
- **Domain:** dependency / controlled ellipsis reconstruction.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K42 + K43.
- **Locked:** full حذف العائد theory, disputed reconstructions, multiple ellipses, nested relatives, advanced discourse reconstruction, conditional linkage.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K46 — CLAUSAL-KHABAR-VERBAL
- **Canonical competence:** mengenali jumlah fi'liyah sederhana sebagai khabar bagi mubtada' eksplisit, dengan `رابط` lokal yang mekanismenya sudah dikuasai sebelumnya.
- **Domain:** clause embedding / nominal predication.
- **Learner operation:** identify explicit mubtada', delimit a short verbal clause, recognize the whole clause as khabar, and identify a transparent local link.
- **Hard prerequisites:** K8 + prior pronoun/subject/reference competencies as required by the occurrence.
- **Locked:** taxonomy of روابط الجملة الخبرية, nominal-clause khabar, clausal khabar under `إنّ/كان/ليس`, conditional relations, nested clauses, advanced discourse reference.
- **Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN.
