# Rescan K59 — Sequencing vs Subordination v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K58 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Menentukan operasi baru setelah koordinasi antarklausa K58 tanpa menjadikan setiap koordinator (`ف`, `ثم`, dll.) sebagai K tersendiri.

## Candidate A — Sequencing relation with `ف`

### New operation
Beyond recognizing clause coordination, learner interprets an overt connector as encoding **tight succession/result-like sequencing** between two complete clauses.

### Dependency
- K58 clause coordination;
- clause boundaries already known;
- connector is overt.

### Risk
`ف` has several functions. If K59 merely becomes “another conjunction,” it fails distinctness. It passes only if assessment requires identifying the **semantic-temporal relation** added by the connector.

**Judgement: HIGH candidate, but needs strict function validation.**

---

## Candidate B — Sequencing relation with `ثم`

### New operation
Recognize two coordinated clauses where the connector contributes **ordered succession with interval/delay**.

### Dependency
Similar to A, but semantic distinction may be easier to articulate than the many functions of `ف`.

### Risk
If teaching becomes lexical gloss memorization of `ثم = kemudian`, it is too shallow for a new K.

**Judgement: MODERATE-HIGH candidate.**

---

## Candidate C — Simple subordinate clause relation

### New operation
Recognize one clause as structurally dependent on another rather than peer-coordinated.

### Dependency burden
Potentially high because "subordination" covers several unrelated structures: causal, temporal, purpose, complement, relative, etc. A generic subordination K would be too broad.

**Judgement: TOO BROAD unless narrowed to one overt, transparent relation.**

---

## Candidate D — Relative-clause expansion

Relative-clause mechanics were already introduced earlier in the ladder. Additional relative forms are therefore likely transfer/application unless a genuinely new dependency appears.

**Judgement: NOT preferred for K59.**

## Distinctness rule

K59 should not be "recognize another conjunction." It must introduce a new **relation inference** on top of K58:

`two peer clauses + overt connector → classify the ordering relation encoded by the connector`.

## Provisional frontier

The strongest candidate is a **restricted sequencing competence**, beginning with the most semantically transparent overt coordinator.

### K59-CAND

> Learner recognizes that two already-analyzable clauses are coordinated in an explicit ordered sequence and identifies the connector as encoding that sequence.

Initial clean-bank comparison should test `ثم` versus a tightly validated `ف` set. Choose the one with lower function ambiguity for the core.

## Exclusions

Do not yet require:
- full semantic taxonomy of `ف`;
- all meanings of `ثم`;
- rhetoric/discourse timing nuance;
- cause-result inference where the text does not grammatically license it;
- nested coordination;
- ellipsis across clauses.

## Decision

Proceed to an evidence/distinctness gate comparing **`ثم`-based ordered sequencing** against a restricted `ف` sequencing set. Prefer the connector whose occurrence bank gives the clearest one-operation competence.
