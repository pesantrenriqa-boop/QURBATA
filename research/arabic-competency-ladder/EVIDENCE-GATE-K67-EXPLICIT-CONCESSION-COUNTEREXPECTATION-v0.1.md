# EVIDENCE GATE K67 — Explicit Concession / Counterexpectation v0.1

**Status:** ACTIVE EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K66 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Candidate

> **K67-CAND — Mengenali relasi konsesif/counterexpectation dalam konstruksi Qurani eksplisit: suatu proposisi tetap berlaku meskipun terdapat keadaan yang secara normal menimbulkan ekspektasi sebaliknya.**

## Core representation

`A = expectation-triggering circumstance`

`B = proposition that nevertheless holds`

Pedagogical relation:

`MESKIPUN A → B TETAP BERLAKU`

## Core learner operation

Learner must:
1. identify the circumstance/proposition A;
2. identify proposition B;
3. identify the overt validated concessive/counterexpectation signal;
4. state that B remains valid despite the expectation created by A.

## PASS requirements

- A and B are overt/local enough to identify;
- a validated overt construction signals concession/counterexpectation;
- all internal syntax is analyzable ≤ K66;
- the relation is not merely contrast;
- no new conditional taxonomy is required;
- no long rhetorical inference is required;
- one counterexpectation operation solves the item.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A — transparent concession
The construction overtly encodes approximately “even though/despite A, B”.

### PASS-B — cumulative-clean concession
A or B contains prior-level complexity, but the expectation→counterexpectation relation remains local and clear.

### REVIEW-CONTRAST
A and B differ, but there is no clear “B holds despite A” relation. Such cases belong to K62 rather than K67.

### REVIEW-CONDITION
The construction is primarily hypothetical/conditional and requires a new conditional subtype analysis.

### REVIEW-RHETORIC
The counterexpectation reading depends on broad tafsir/balaghah rather than overt linguistic evidence.

### PREMATURE
- implicit concessive inference with no overt signal;
- nested concession;
- multiple competing expectation triggers;
- advanced hypothetical concession whose syntax itself is new;
- disputed discourse scope.

## Distinctness

### vs K62 contrast/correction
K62 asks whether proposition B contrasts with/corrects proposition A.

K67 requires an additional expectation structure:

`A would normally support expectation X, yet B holds contrary to X.`

Thus every concession can contain contrastive force, but not every contrast is concession.

### vs K47/K48 conditional dependency
Conditional competencies identify condition→response architecture. K67 identifies **persistence despite an expectation-triggering circumstance**. If a candidate can only be solved by learning a new conditional construction, it is not core K67 evidence.

### vs negation
Negation changes polarity. Concession organizes two propositions around defeated expectation; negation may occur inside A/B but is not the target.

## Marker policy

K67 is not assigned to one particle. Candidate constructions must be validated occurrence-by-occurrence. Marker taxonomy is implementation evidence, not the competency.

## Assessment prompt

`ما الأمر الذي كان يمكن أن يُتوقَّع منه خلاف النتيجة؟ وما المعنى الذي ثبت مع ذلك؟`

Indonesian support:

`Keadaan apa yang menimbulkan dugaan sebaliknya, dan apa yang tetap berlaku meskipun demikian?`

## Gate verdict

- conceptual distinctness from contrast: **PASS**;
- distinctness from condition: **PASS WITH STRICT FILTER**;
- overt evidence feasibility: **PASS PROVISIONALLY**;
- rhetoric contamination risk: **MODERATE**;
- overall: **ADVANCE TO CLEAN-BANK STRESS-TEST**.

Freeze only if enough Qur'anic items encode counterexpectation overtly enough that learners need not infer the relation primarily from tafsir.
