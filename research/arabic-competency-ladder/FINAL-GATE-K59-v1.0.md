## Competency

**K59 — Mengenali urutan kejadian antara dua klausa Qurani yang utuh melalui `ثم` yang tervalidasi sebagai penghubung sekuensial.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify the first complete clause;
2. identify the second complete clause;
3. locate `ثم` as the overt connector;
4. state that the event in the second clause follows the event in the first.

## Included

- two independently analyzable clauses;
- overt validated `ثم`;
- clear ordered succession;
- cumulative clause-internal analysis through K58.

## Excluded

- full classical theory of `التراخي`;
- precise measurement of temporal delay;
- rhetorical/discourse uses of `ثم` requiring later analysis;
- nested coordination;
- cross-clause ellipsis;
- disputed event ordering.

Expected response identifies the two clause spans, `ثم`, and the direction of sequence.
