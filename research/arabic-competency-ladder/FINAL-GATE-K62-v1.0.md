# FINAL GATE K62 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K61 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K62 — Mengenali relasi pertentangan/koreksi antara dua proposisi Qurani melalui `لكن` eksplisit yang tervalidasi, dengan menjelaskan apa yang dibatasi atau dikoreksi oleh proposisi kedua.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify proposition A;
2. identify overt `لكن`;
3. identify proposition B;
4. explain how B corrects, restricts, or contrasts with an expectation established by A.

## Included

- two proposition spans analyzable through K61;
- overt validated `لكن`;
- locally transparent contrast/correction relation;
- semantic relation stated explicitly by learner.

## Excluded

- full taxonomy of `لكن`;
- disputed particle function;
- hidden or omitted proposition;
- nested contrast;
- discourse-dependent interpretations that cannot be resolved locally;
- full conjunction inventory.

## Assessment prompt

`ما القضية الأولى؟ وما القضية الثانية؟ وما الذي تصححه أو تستدركه الثانية بعد لكن؟`

Expected response identifies both propositions and states the contrastive/corrective relation.

## Gate results

- distinctness from K58: PASS;
- semantic-relation requirement: PASS;
- overt-marker containment: PASS;
- anti-inventory: PASS;
- one-operation rule: PASS.

**Decision: K62 DRAFT-FROZEN.**

## Architectural significance

K62 marks a shift from recognizing structural linkage between clauses to identifying an explicit discourse-semantic relation between propositions. The learner now explains not only that two spans are connected, but how the second reorients the interpretation of the first.
