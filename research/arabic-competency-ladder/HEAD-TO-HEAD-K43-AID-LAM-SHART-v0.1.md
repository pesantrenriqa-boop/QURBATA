# Head-to-Head K43 — `عائد` vs `لام التوكيد` vs Simple Conditional Relation v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K42 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Candidate A — Simple `عائد` Analysis

### New operation
Learner identifies the resumptive element/link inside a silah that points back to the isim maushul.

### Dependencies
- K42 maushul→silah boundary/linkage;
- K15/K18/K20/K21 pronoun morphology/functions;
- K41 basic hidden subject where relevant.

### Strength
- direct continuation from K42;
- genuinely distinct operation;
- can be tightly limited to **explicit pronominal `عائد`** first.

### Risks
- omitted `عائد` is substantially harder;
- resumptive can occur in object, genitive, prepositional, or other positions;
- examples may require deeper clause analysis.

**Judgement: VERY HIGH if restricted to explicit `عائد` only.**

---

## Candidate B — `لام التوكيد` in `إنّ` Construction

### New operation
Learner recognizes an emphatic lām associated with an already-known `إنّ` construction and identifies its scope/function in simple occurrences.

### Dependencies
- K38 full simple `إنّ` construction;
- particle recognition and nominal relation already stable.

### Strength
- highly local marker;
- low structural burden;
- prior audit established it is distinct but not prerequisite to K38.

### Risks
- must distinguish from other lām functions;
- some occurrences have clause-level complexity;
- if defined too broadly, polyfunctionality of `لـ` becomes a new ambiguity problem.

**Judgement: VERY HIGH with strict occurrence-specific tagging.**

---

## Candidate C — Simple Conditional Relation

### New operation
Learner links a conditional marker/protasis to a جواب/response in a simple Qur'anic construction.

### Dependencies
- particle recognition;
- clause boundary recognition;
- verbal/nominal relations;
- often mood/jazm effects or omitted جواب issues.

### Strength
- high pedagogical value.

### Risks
- inherently clause-linking;
- marker polyfunctionality (`إن`, `لو`, etc.);
- جواب may be explicit, delayed, omitted, or structurally complex;
- mood/government effects can leak in.

**Judgement: HIGH VALUE BUT HEAVIER; should not be K43.**

---

## Head-to-head result

The real competition is between A and B.

### Why `لام التوكيد` may be lighter
- token-level and local;
- builds on frozen K38;
- does not require relative-clause internal argument tracking.

### Why explicit `عائد` may still deserve earlier placement
- K42 immediately unlocks the conceptual need for it;
- explicit pronoun tracking reuses previously frozen pronoun functions;
- creates a coherent maushul progression.

## Preliminary decision

Provisional order:

- **K43-CAND — explicit `عائد` in simple silah**
- **K44-CAND — `لام التوكيد` in simple `إنّ` construction**
- conditional relation later.

### Tie-break rationale
K43 is chosen for **dependency continuity**, not because it is globally simpler than lām. Once K42 has taught boundary/linkage, explicit `عائد` is the next missing operation inside that same structure. This minimizes conceptual fragmentation.

However, K43 must be narrowly defined:
- explicit resumptive pronoun only;
- no omitted `عائد`;
- no advanced resumptive category taxonomy;
- no nested relative clause;
- internal structure must be ≤ K42.

## Evidence gate classes for K43

- **PASS-A:** explicit pronominal `عائد`, direct and obvious, all internal grammar ≤ K42.
- **PASS-B:** cumulative-clean variant with already-mastered PP/object/idhafah roles.
- **REVIEW-FUNC:** pronoun function itself is ambiguous or requires higher analysis.
- **REVIEW-OMIT:** resumptive is omitted or reconstructed; defer.
- **PREMATURE:** nested clause, complex ellipsis, conditional dependency, or other K44+ operation.

## Next

Build occurrence-specific clean bank for **explicit `عائد`**. In parallel, keep `لام التوكيد` ready as K44 candidate and run a later distinctness/ambiguity audit across lām functions.
