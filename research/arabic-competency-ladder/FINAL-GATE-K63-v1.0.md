## Competency

**K63 — Mengenali relasi hasil/akibat antara dua proposisi Qurani yang sudah dapat dianalisis melalui marker eksplisit yang telah tervalidasi, dengan menetapkan proposisi kedua sebagai hasil/konsekuensi dari proposisi pertama.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence, learner can:
1. identify proposition A;
2. identify proposition B;
3. locate the overt prevalidated linker;
4. classify B as the result/consequence of A.

## Included

- two overt proposition spans;
- one overt occurrence-specifically validated result/consequence linker;
- one directed A→B relation;
- cumulative syntax/discourse analysis through K62.

## Excluded

- broad taxonomy of `ف`;
- ambiguous temporal-vs-result readings;
- condition→response items whose only relation is already captured by earlier competencies;
- implicit causality without overt evidence;
- long-distance discourse inference;
- ellipsis plus consequence reconstruction.

Expected core response identifies proposition B as result/consequence of proposition A and points to the overt validated linker.
