## Mapping principles
- K is a canonical competency, not automatically a book page or course level.
- Stage assignment follows prerequisite and cognitive operation, not numbering alone.
- Recognition, controlled analysis, production, and integration are kept distinct.
- Placement-test items must be observable without requiring a later competency.
- Acceleration is permitted only when mastery evidence is demonstrated.

## K16–K30 allocation

### S2 — Controlled Morphosyntax
K16 — coordination/basic conjunction relation. Observable: identify coordinated units and conjunction without importing discourse semantics.
K17 — controlled nominal/verbal morphology node. E3 evidence supports diversified examples; suitable for mastery assessment.
K18 — controlled case/mood recognition. Keep prompt local and form-based.
K19 — controlled dependency recognition. Require only relations already introduced.
K20 — morphosyntactic discrimination. E3; suitable as a checkpoint competency.
K21 — controlled relation analysis. E3; may serve as acceleration checkpoint when prerequisites are mastered.
K22 — bounded phrase-level analysis. Avoid clause-level inference.
K23 — controlled structural distinction. E3; placement observable with minimal-span items.
K24 — demonstrative relational analysis. Keep separate from mere demonstrative recognition and later apposition/predication complexity.
K25 — transition competency from phrase morphology toward sentence relations.

### S2 → S3 bridge
K26 — exception marker recognition. E3. Placement observable through explicit marker recognition with boundary controls.
K27 — negation recognition. E2; require diversified negative-particle bank before E3. Do not confuse with prohibition K30.
K28 — vocative recognition. E2. Recognition only; full i'rab of the addressee remains outside the target.
K29 — imperative recognition. E3. Positive imperative morphology; distinguish from prohibition.
K30 — prohibitive `لا` recognition. E3. Must discriminate prohibitive from ordinary negative `لا` through verbal behavior.

## Curriculum consequences
1. K16–K25 form the main S2 mastery spine.
2. K26–K30 form a controlled transition band into sentence-level operator recognition.
3. K20/K21/K23 can become S2 checkpoint nodes because their evidence is already E3.
4. K26/K29/K30 can become early S3-entry discriminators in placement testing.
5. K27/K28 remain valid curriculum nodes but should not be used as high-confidence acceleration gates until E3 diversity is achieved.

## Placement-test observability
- K16–K25: mostly short-span structural identification and relation classification.
- K26–K30: minimal-pair discrimination is preferred.
- No question may require a learner to explain a higher-stage semantic relation to receive credit for a lower-stage recognition competency.
