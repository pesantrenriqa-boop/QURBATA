# Evidence Gate K51 — Jazm by `حذف النون` v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — EVIDENCE GATE  
**Baseline:** K1–K50 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## K51 target

Learner can recognize a familiar `فعل مضارع` form from the five-verb pattern whose expected final nūn is absent because of jazm in a validated simple conditional/jussive environment.

Core operation:

`known five-verb mudhari‘ form` → `jussive environment` → **expected nūn absent** → identify deletion as jazm effect.

K51 is not a full lesson on `الأفعال الخمسة`.

## Hard prerequisites

- K47 simple conditional relation;
- K49/K50 bridge from syntactic environment to morphological effect;
- prior pronoun/person-number recognition only to the extent needed to recognize the controlled base form.

## Scope control

PASS candidates require:
- base form is already familiar or supplied by controlled comparison;
- only the inflectional nūn is missing;
- the person/number pattern is locally transparent;
- no weak-final deletion competes;
- no imperative or prohibitive pattern ambiguity;
- no orthographic complication requiring a second lesson;
- jussive environment is independently clear.

## Evidence classes

### PASS-A
A familiar five-verb-pattern mudhari‘ in a clear jussive environment; expected nūn deletion is the only new operation.

### PASS-B
Additional cumulative-clean material only, with base form still recoverable locally.

### REVIEW-PARADIGM
The occurrence is grammatically valid but requires broader five-verb paradigm knowledge than K51 should assume.

### REVIEW-ORTH
Qur'anic spelling/phonology obscures the simple comparison and would create a second operation.

### REVIEW-AMBIG
Surface form could be confused with another verbal construction unless later morphology is taught.

### PREMATURE
- more than one morphological deletion/change;
- advanced person/number reconstruction;
- weak-final behavior interacting with nūn deletion;
- complex non-conditional jussive governance;
- disputed analysis.

## Anti-overload rule

At K51, learner should answer only:

> `ما الفعل المضارع؟ وما النون المتوقعة في صورته المعروفة؟ ولماذا حذفت هنا؟`

Do not require:
- naming all five forms from memory;
- full conjugation table;
- complete raf‘/nasb/jazm comparison;
- full theory of `الأفعال الخمسة`;
- all jussive particles.

## Distinctness from K50

K50 reconstructs a **lexical weak-final segment**.

K51 reconstructs an **inflectional nūn tied to person/number morphology**.

Therefore the learner operation is distinct even though both are deletion-based jazm signs.

## Freeze criteria

K51 may freeze only when:
1. base-form comparison remains transparent;
2. deletion of nūn is the only new morphological operation;
3. the examples do not force a full five-verb paradigm lesson;
4. occurrence-specific Qur'anic analysis is stable;
5. clean-bank quality is sufficient without quota forcing.
