# FINAL GATE K52 v1.0

**Status:** DRAFT-FROZEN  
**Baseline:** K1–K51 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Competency

**K52 — Mengenali efek nashab dasar berupa fatḥah yang transparan pada fi‘il mudhari‘ familiar setelah nāṣib eksplisit yang tervalidasi.**

## Learner operation

Given a validated occurrence with an overt simple nāṣib, learner can:
1. locate the explicit governor;
2. identify the target as a familiar mudhari‘;
3. recognize transparent final fatḥah;
4. relate that fatḥah to nasb in the local occurrence.

## Included

- overt validated nāṣib;
- familiar sound-final mudhari‘;
- transparent final fatḥah;
- restricted initial governor set;
- cumulative syntax/morphology through K51.

## Excluded

- full `نواصب المضارع` inventory;
- hidden/estimated `أن`;
- nasb via `حذف النون`;
- complex weak-final realization;
- disputed governance;
- complete raf‘–nasb–jazm paradigm.

## Assessment prompt

`ما الناصب؟ وما الفعل المضارع؟ وما أثر النصب الظاهر في آخره؟`

Expected core response identifies the explicit governor, target verb, and transparent fatḥah.
