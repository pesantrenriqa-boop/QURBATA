# Rescan K58 — Cross-Domain Frontier v0.1

**Status:** WORKING RESEARCH — PRE-FREEZE  
**Baseline:** K1–K57 DRAFT-FROZEN.

## Purpose

Setelah K57, jangan otomatis meneruskan seluruh varian i‘rāb taqdiri. K58 harus dipilih dari operasi yang benar-benar baru dan paling ringan secara dependency.

## Candidate A — estimated nasb on weak-final mudhari‘

Potential operation: identify nasb where surface fatḥah is not overt on a weak-final verb.

### Dependency burden
Moderate–high:
- requires K52/K53 nasb environment;
- requires K57 estimated-ending abstraction;
- risks duplicating the same inference pattern with a new mood label;
- some subclasses have different surface behavior.

**Judgement:** likely transfer/application rather than a new K unless corpus evidence shows a distinct learner operation.

## Candidate B — rule-depth on `التعذر` vs `الثقل`

Potential operation: explain *why* the i‘rāb vowel is estimated.

### Dependency burden
High and theory-heavy:
- phonological explanation;
- weak-final class distinctions;
- limited direct benefit for early Qur'anic parsing.

**Judgement:** mastery depth, not K58 core.

## Candidate C — explicit clause coordination relation

Potential operation: recognize that two already-analyzable clauses are linked by an overt coordinator and determine their local structural parallelism.

### Dependency burden
Moderate:
- clause boundaries already available;
- coordinator overt;
- no hidden morphology required;
- new operation is clause-to-clause coordination rather than intra-clause parsing.

**Judgement:** strong candidate if examples can be kept simple and coordinator function is stable.

## Candidate D — simple causal/result linkage beyond syarth

Potential operation: identify an overt marker connecting a cause/reason clause to a result or explanation clause.

### Dependency burden
Moderate–high:
- semantic classification can be subtle;
- particle multifunctionality risks disputed analysis.

**Judgement:** important, but likely heavier than simple coordination.

## Head-to-head result

**K58-CAND — simple explicit clause coordination**, provided the occurrence contains two already-analyzable clauses and an overt coordinator whose local function is stable.

Why this outranks estimated-nasb transfer:
1. estimated-nasb largely reuses K52/K57 inference architecture;
2. clause coordination introduces a genuinely new structural relation;
3. it expands the ladder back from morphology into inter-clausal syntax;
4. it can be assessed without full discourse theory.

## Provisional K58 definition

Learner can identify two simple clauses, recognize an overt coordinator linking them, and state that they stand in a coordinated relation without requiring advanced semantic taxonomy.

## Hard exclusions

Do not yet require:
- full taxonomy of Arabic conjunctions;
- rhetorical/discourse relations;
- ellipsis across coordination;
- shared omitted constituents;
- nested coordination;
- disputed scope.

## Evidence gate criteria

PASS only when:
- both clauses are independently analyzable ≤ K57;
- coordinator is overt;
- coordination scope is local and stable;
- no hidden element is required to establish the link;
- no advanced semantic distinction is necessary.

## Decision

Open K58 evidence gate for **simple explicit clause coordination**. Estimated mood variants remain transfer/mastery unless later evidence proves a distinct learner operation.
