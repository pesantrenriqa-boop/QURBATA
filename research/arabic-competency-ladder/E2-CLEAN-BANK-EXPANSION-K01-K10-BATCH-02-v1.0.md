Continue diversification without weakening the clean-bank rule. This batch adds independently referenced Qur'anic occurrences and separates positive evidence from transfer/boundary evidence.

## Verified additions

### K01 — simple isim recognition
Additional independently referenced nominal tokens:
- `الْحَيُّ` — QS 2:255
- `الْقَيُّومُ` — QS 2:255
- `سِنَةٌ` — QS 2:255
- `نَوْمٌ` — QS 2:255

Together with QS 112:1 and QS 17:81 seeds, K01 now has lexical and verse diversity.

**Decision:** PROMOTE K01 → E2.

### K02 — overt `الـ` recognition
Additional forms:
- `الْحَيُّ` — QS 2:255
- `الْقَيُّومُ` — QS 2:255
- `السَّمَاوَاتِ` — QS 2:255
- `الْأَرْضِ` — QS 2:255

Combined with `الْحَقُّ`, `الْبَاطِلُ`, `الصَّمَدُ`, the operation repeats across several lexical items and passages.

**Decision:** PROMOTE K02 → E2.

### K03 — simple tanwin / nakirah evidence
Additional clean forms:
- `سِنَةٌ` — QS 2:255
- `نَوْمٌ` — QS 2:255
- `بِشَيْءٍ` contains tanwin on `شَيْءٍ` — QS 2:255; use the noun target only, not the PP relation.

Together with `أَحَدٌ` — QS 112:1, this gives multiple case/tanwin surfaces.

**Boundary:** K03 tests recognition of the nominal indefiniteness/tanwin signal only; case-function analysis is not required.

**Decision:** PROMOTE K03 → E2.

### K04 — frequent preposition recognition
Verified distinct core forms in QS 2:255:
- `فِي` — `فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ`
- `فِي` — `فِي الْأَرْضِ`
- `بِـ` — `بِإِذْنِهِ`
- `مِنْ` — `مِنْ عِلْمِهِ`

Earlier recovery also established other core preposition occurrences. This batch confirms multiple marker identities in one stable passage, but a broader independent-verse family is still preferred before E2.

**Decision:** remain E1; strong E2 candidate.

### K05 — detached pronoun recognition
Verified:
- `هُوَ` — QS 2:255 (`إِلَّا هُوَ`; `وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ`)
- `هُوَ` — QS 112:1

These are independent passages but only one pronoun identity. Frozen K05 includes broader basic detached-pronoun recognition, so diversity of pronoun forms is still required.

**Decision:** remain E1.

### K06 — simple perfect verb recognition
Additional ordinary lexical perfects in QS 2:255:
- `شَاءَ`
- `وَسِعَ`

Together with `جَاءَ` and `زَهَقَ` — QS 17:81, the bank now spans independent verses and several lexical roots.

**Boundary:** `كان` remains excluded from ordinary K06 core evidence because of dedicated K37/K40 treatment.

**Decision:** PROMOTE K06 → E2.

### K07 — simple imperfect verb recognition
QS 2:255 supplies several ordinary lexical imperfects:
- `تَأْخُذُهُ`
- `يَشْفَعُ`
- `يَعْلَمُ`
- `يُحِيطُونَ`
- `يَئُودُهُ`

For K07, attached objects or surrounding particles are not analyzed; target is recognition of the imperfect verb form only.

**Decision:** PROMOTE K07 → E2.

### K08 — simple mubtada' + overt nominal khabar
QS 2:255 ends with `وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ`, but this invokes detached-pronoun mubtada' and therefore belongs to K11/transfer rather than a clean K08 core item. The existing `اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ` remains canonical.

**Decision:** remain E1 pending a second independently normalized noun-initial minimal nominal predicate.

### K09 — preposition + overt noun
Clean PP spans in QS 2:255:
- `فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ`
- `فِي الْأَرْضِ`
- `مِنْ عِلْمِهِ` — use only if possessive suffix is treated as already mastered/cumulative; otherwise reserve for transfer.

`بِإِذْنِهِ` is not preferred as early K09 core because the noun carries a possessive suffix. The two `في + overt noun` spans are clean but same verse/marker, so broader diversity is still required.

**Decision:** remain E1; E2 candidate.

### K10 — verb + overt fa'il
Additional independently referenced overt-subject structure:
- `وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ` — QS 2:255

The subject contains an attached possessive pronoun; therefore it is useful cumulative evidence but not the cleanest early-core K10 item. Batch 01 already has `جَاءَ الْحَقُّ` and `زَهَقَ الْبَاطِلُ` — QS 17:81.

**Decision:** remain E2 candidate; seek a third independent occurrence with a simple overt noun subject and no attached suffix before promotion.

## Quality controls

1. Recognition targets may occur inside structurally complex verses only when the learner operation can be isolated to the target token.
2. Relational competencies K08–K10 require stricter clean-span control than token-recognition competencies.
3. An attached pronoun may be ignored for a recognition-only target such as K07, but cannot be silently ignored when it changes the relation being tested.
4. Repetition of the same marker in one verse is not treated as full diversity.
