# QURBATA NIDOM — JILID 1 — CONTENT MAP VARIATIVE v2.2

Status: FROZEN FOR REBUILD
Baseline learner: beginner-zero.
Core rule: repeat the COMPETENCY, vary the learning experience.

## Architecture
Jilid 1 = 40 pages. 8 core akhlak competencies + cumulative evaluation/reflection. A theme is 3–5 encounters depending on complexity. A hadith is a source/anchor, not a page quota.

### THEME A — SALAM & SAPA BAIK (P001–P004) — 4 encounters
P001 Hadits inti + talqin: menyebarkan salam; recognize greeting.
P002 Kisah/illustration: child enters class and chooses how to greet; picture-like sequence + choose response.
P003 Dialog/role play: greeting teacher, parent, friend; complete short dialogue.
P004 Habit challenge: salam at home/school + parent/teacher observation + spiral recall.

### THEME B — BERKATA BAIK (P005–P008) — 4 encounters
P005 Hadits inti: فَلْيَقُلْ خَيْرًا أَوْ لِيَصْمُتْ — meaning and simple examples.
P006 Sort/match: words that make people happy vs hurt; no long prose.
P007 Case reasoning: friend makes a mistake / loses a game / is mocked; choose best words and one simple why.
P008 Practice + home challenge: replace three hurtful expressions with good words; observation.

### THEME C — MENGENDALIKAN MARAH (P009–P013) — 5 encounters
P009 Hadits anchor: strength is controlling oneself when angry.
P010 Supporting prophetic guidance: لا تغضب as a short reinforcement; recognize triggers.
P011 Illustrated story (explicitly pedagogical illustration): two children disagree over a toy/book; predict consequences.
P012 Case ladder: stop → calm → speak; order actions + choose response.
P013 Role play + 2-day habit challenge; teacher/parent check.

### THEME D — JUJUR (P014–P018) — 5 encounters
P014 Hadits anchor: truthfulness leads to righteousness; simple meaning.
P015 Supporting story/source: age-appropriate sourced story or clearly labelled pedagogical illustration about admitting a mistake.
P016 Case reasoning: broken item, unfinished homework, found object; choose truthful response.
P017 Dialogue/role play: admitting mistake to parent/teacher; complete dialogue + practice.
P018 Reflection + 3-day honesty challenge + observer signature.

### P019–P020 — CUMULATIVE CHECKPOINT I
P019 Mixed objective: multiple choice, match, order, true/false with one-word/simple reason across A–D.
P020 Transfer: 3 mini-cases combining greeting, good speech, anger, honesty; one observed role play; child reflection.

### THEME E — KASIH SAYANG (P021–P024) — 4 encounters
P021 Hadits anchor: mercy toward people; concrete examples.
P022 Story: caring for younger/sick/sad friend; comprehension via icons/choices.
P023 Case + action planning: what can I do when someone needs help? choose then draw/write one action.
P024 Mercy mission: one real act at home/school + parent/teacher observation; spiral recall.

### THEME F — AMANAH & MENJAGA BARANG (P025–P028) — 4 encounters
P025 Hadits/source anchor about trust; distinguish mine/yours/borrowed.
P026 Practical sorting: borrowed pencil, class key, message, found money; what should happen?
P027 Case reasoning + dialogue: borrowed item damaged/lost; honest and responsible response.
P028 Practice: return borrowed object correctly + simple observer rubric.

### THEME G — HORMAT ORANG TUA & GURU (P029–P032) — 4 encounters
P029 Qur'anic/hadith anchor appropriate to level; polite words and listening.
P030 Story/sequence: called by parent / teacher explains / elder needs help; choose first action.
P031 Role play: asking permission, answering call, receiving instruction; observed practice.
P032 Home-school mission: one service to parent + one adab to teacher; dual observation.

### THEME H — MENOLONG & BERBAGI (P033–P035) — 3 encounters
P033 Source anchor + concrete distinction between helping in good and joining wrongdoing.
P034 Cooperative cases/game: choose what to share/help and why; pair task.
P035 Real-action challenge: help/share once, then draw/write what happened; observer check.

### P036–P040 — FINAL INTEGRATION
P036 Cumulative game-like quiz: recognize behaviours from all 8 competencies.
P037 Reasoning cases: mixed situations; choose action + simple reason.
P038 Practical stations: greeting, good speech, anger response, honesty, amanah, respect/help; teacher rubric.
P039 Jejak Kebiasaanku: child self-assessment + parent + teacher observation; no punitive scoring.
P040 Rapor NIDOM Jilid 1: competency profile (Mulai Tumbuh / Berkembang / Membudaya), strengths, one next habit target, signatures.

## Variety gate
- Same primary page model may not appear on consecutive pages.
- Every 5-page window must include at least 3 distinct cognitive/behavioural modes.
- Every theme must include at least one DO/PRACTICE page.
- At least four themes include a story/illustration page.
- At least four themes include case reasoning.
- Hadith anchor may recur, but never by copying an identical page.
- Arabic text is not the child's reading burden; teacher may talqin. Jilid 1 language remains concrete and short.

## Source gate
Every attributed hadith, Qur'anic verse, prophetic/sahabah story must have verified source metadata before print final. Pedagogical invented stories must be visibly labelled `ILUSTRASI`.

## Print gate
KFGQPC Uthman Taha Naskh strict gate; QURBATA logo; 176×250 mm; no overflow; signatures in lower zone when observation is relevant. Evaluation pages may replace signatures with assessor/parent observation boxes where pedagogically better.
