Arabic title: السَّلَامُ

Meaning: Sebarkanlah salam di antara kalian.

Competency: Mengucapkan dan menjawab salam dengan santun.

## Typography
- Indonesian/UI: Noto Sans family, clean sans-serif hierarchy
- Arabic: Naskh-style Arabic typeface, high readability, substantially larger than body copy
- Arabic hadith is the focal typography; avoid dense commentary

## Decorative language
Minimal, calm, geometric, derived from circles/Arabic rhythm. Decoration must never compete with hadith text. Use generous whitespace.

## Pedagogy
One printed page has one dominant akhlak competency. Hadith is the value anchor; mastery is behavioral evidence, not memorization alone.

## Production rule
Do not mass-produce Jilid 1-8 until this page is visually approved/frozen. After approval, create reusable renderer/template and generate subsequent pages under version control.
