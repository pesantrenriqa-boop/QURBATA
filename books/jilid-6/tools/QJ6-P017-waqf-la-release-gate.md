## Mandatory acceptance sequence

1. Record selected **Mushaf Standar Indonesia edition**, publication/version, page and exact verse.
2. Inspect the **actual mark at the exact location** (direct scan or official digital mushaf) and capture its immediate preceding/following words. For P017-S02 verify whether the excerpt includes the reported mark location; if not, adjust only after source verification and a separate content review.
3. Transcribe a text excerpt that **actually includes the mark** without inventing a Unicode sequence; record glyph and position, plus direct source reference.
4. Check prerequisite order and Jilid 1–5 duplicate register before marking `competency_verified=yes`.
5. Obtain reviewer/tashih approval. Until then retain `source_verified=no`, `competency_verified=no`, `review_status=CANDIDATE` or `EMPTY`; never set print approval.
