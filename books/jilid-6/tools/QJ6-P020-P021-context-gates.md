**P021's nine examples refer to source slots on later pages P029–P030.** Thus the page-21 lesson draws on material that is registered eight to nine pages later. This is a *curriculum-sequence review flag*, not proof that the actual verses are linguistically too advanced. The teacher must confirm prerequisite coverage, or relocate the example sources / supply prior instruction. **Do not mark P021 ready** merely because source IDs are traceable.
