# QIES-001 — QURBATA International Education Standard

## Purpose
This document defines the master quality standard for all QURBATA educational products.

## Core Standards
1. Academic Standard
2. Qur'anic Standard
3. Arabic Standard
4. Teaching Standard
5. Assessment Standard
6. Production Standard
7. Governance Standard

## Principle
No curriculum, book, assessment, or digital product is released unless it complies with QIES.
