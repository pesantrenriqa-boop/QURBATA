## 1. Objective

Translate the current Standard-candidate corpus into a realistic eight-volume progression before page-level mapping.

Current Standard candidate:

- CORE WAJIB including Al-Fatihah;
- sequential Juz 30 → 29 → 28 → 27;
- selected corpus only when it does not damage sequential continuity or retention reserve.

This document does **not** freeze final jilid boundaries.

---

## 2. Design Rules

1. J1 and J2 must protect beginners and remain talqin-friendly.
2. Load must increase materially across J3–J8.
3. Juz boundaries do not need to match jilid boundaries.
4. A jilid may end in the middle of a juz if pedagogically cleaner.
5. CORE PILIHAN is inserted only after the sequential backbone is protected.
6. Al-Fatihah is treated as special priority and may appear before the sequential corpus.
7. Retention reserve remains external to the new-hifz map.

## 6. Why Juz 30 Is Spread Across J1–J3

Even though Juz 30 is only one juz, it contains many surahs and serves as the foundation for beginners.

Spreading it across J1–J3 allows:
- very small entry targets;
- gradual acceleration;
- strong familiarity and worship utility;
- enough time for tartil competence to catch up;
- avoidance of a steep memorization jump too early.

The later jilid can then carry substantially larger text volume per meeting.

## 7. Standard vs Plus Track

Candidate terminal corpus:
- CORE WAJIB;
- Juz 30, 29, 28, 27;
- limited selected external passages only if capacity remains.

### Plus

Candidate extension:
- begin or complete Juz 26;
- fewer external selected passages unless justified;
- requires stronger retention implementation.

### Accelerated

No separate fixed corpus yet. Learners who exceed Standard may continue sequentially beyond Juz 26 through the post-QURBATA 30-juz system.
