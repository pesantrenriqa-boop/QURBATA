## 1. Revision Objective

v0.2 removes the false assumption that every Jilid 1 page must carry new memorization. It preserves the approved hybrid corpus sequence while making P010, P020 and P030 explicit review/evaluation gates and treating P040 as terminal consolidation.

The central rule is dual-channel learning:

- **TARTIL:** the reading competency formally taught on the page.
- **TAHFIDZ:** authentic Qur'anic memorization, primarily through talqin in Jilid 1.

A tahfidz target may therefore contain features not yet independently readable by the learner, but it must never be counted as premature tartil mastery.

---

## 2. Operational Codes

- `TQ` = TALQIN dominant.
- `TV` = TALQIN + visual familiarity.
- `T3` = TALQIN + three-harakat awareness.
- `RG` = REVIEW GATE; no new hifz.
- `TC` = TERMINAL CONSOLIDATION; no new hifz.
- `PREVIEW` = Qur'anic orthography exceeds current independent tartil competence.
- `PARTIAL` = some visible features are increasingly familiar, but independent mushaf reading is not assumed.

## 5. Load Safety Note

The main pressure point is P039 (`Al-Ma'un 3–7`). This is intentionally marked as a **candidate stress point**, not automatically accepted. The five short ayat may be delivered as several talqin chunks within the meeting, but if classroom testing shows excessive burden, the preferred correction is to carry part of Al-Ma'un into Jilid 2 rather than sacrifice P040 terminal consolidation.

Therefore:

`P039_LOAD_STATUS = FIELD_TEST_REQUIRED`

No target should be compressed merely to satisfy an artificial 'finish the surah inside J1' rule.

---

## 6. Review Gate Policy

### P010 — Gate 1
No new hifz. Teacher validates An-Nas and the acquired portion of Al-Falaq using collective/paired/spot-check methods rather than checking every learner one-by-one for the entire corpus.

### P020 — Gate 2
No new hifz. Priority is retention of Al-Fatihah 1–4 plus completed short surahs.

### P030 — Gate 3
No new hifz. Cumulative retention gate before the closing J1 block.

### P040 — Terminal Gate
No new hifz. J1 ends with consolidation, correction, retention status and readiness signal for J2.

---

## 7. Large-Class Validation Principle

The map does not require full individual recitation on every page. Standard classroom validation should later bind to QTRVS using layered evidence:

1. collective recitation;
2. pair/small-group reciprocal check;
3. randomized teacher spot-check;
4. targeted individual check for flagged learners;
5. periodic formal gate validation.

This preserves instructional time while still producing evidence of retention.
