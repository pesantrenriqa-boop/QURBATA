## 1. Freeze Decision

The previous working assumption that QURBATA Jilid 1–8 should terminate at only Juz 30 is **REVOKED as a terminal-target assumption**.

Juz 30 remains an important corpus component, but it is not automatically the final ceiling of the eight-volume QURBATA tahfidz program.

The approved strategy is a **HYBRID CORPUS MODEL** whose actual terminal volume will be determined by measured learner capacity across the QURBATA progression.

---

## 2. Frozen Architecture

The QURBATA Tahfidz corpus is divided into three layers:

### A. CORE WAJIB — Fundamental / Worship Corpus

Mandatory memorization with the highest functional priority.

Includes:
- Al-Fatihah as a special primary corpus;
- short surahs and passages needed frequently in worship;
- other fundamental passages may be added only through documented corpus selection.

Al-Fatihah is not required to wait for normal juz sequence because of its primary function in salah.

### B. CORE BERURUT — Sequential Corpus

After/alongside the foundational corpus, memorization develops systematically through sequential juz blocks.

Default direction for simulation:

`Juz 30 → Juz 29 → Juz 28 → ...`

The sequence may be interleaved with CORE WAJIB and approved CORE PILIHAN, but sequential continuity must remain visible so that QURBATA becomes a genuine bridge into the later 30-juz tahfidz program.

### C. CORE PILIHAN — Selected High-Value Corpus

Selected surahs/passages may be inserted when they provide high functional, educational, memorization, or transition value.

Initial candidate pool for scoring includes, but is not limited to:
- Yasin;
- Al-Mulk;
- Ar-Rahman;
- Al-Waqi'ah;
- selected Al-Kahf corpus;
- selected Al-Baqarah corpus;
- other passages justified by the scoring system.

**Candidate status does not equal automatic inclusion.** Every selected corpus must pass scoring and capacity analysis.

---

## 3. Frozen Principle: Capacity Before Juz Quota

QURBATA will **not** first declare an arbitrary number of juz and then force learners to fit it.

The process is reversed:

1. measure pedagogical capacity per QURBATA level;
2. model the increasing memorization load from Jilid 1 through Jilid 8;
3. reserve adequate retention/murojaah capacity;
4. fit CORE WAJIB, sequential corpus, and selected corpus into that capacity;
5. derive the realistic terminal volume;
6. only then state the equivalent number of juz/pages/words.

Therefore the final outcome may be 3, 4, 5, 6, or another justified number of juz. **No terminal juz number is frozen yet.**

---

## 4. Frozen Progressive Load Principle

The approximately 320 QURBATA page/meeting slots are **pedagogical units**, not ayah counters.

Memorization load must increase with learner competence.

Conceptual curve:

`J1 very light → J2 light → J3 developing → J4 medium → J5 medium+ → J6 advanced → J7 high → J8 highest sustainable load`

A beginning page may contain only a few words or a very short ayah. A later page may contain several short ayat or a substantially larger passage.

No fixed word-count curve is frozen until corpus simulation is completed.

---

## 5. Frozen Corpus Scoring Framework

Every candidate selected surah/passage is evaluated on five dimensions:

- **F — Functional Value:** relevance to worship, daily religious practice, and frequent use.
- **E — Educational Value:** contribution to aqidah, akhlaq, adab, Qur'anic worldview, narrative, dua, and formation.
- **M — Memorability:** suitability of structure, repetition, rhythm, and textual burden for staged memorization.
- **T — Tartil Alignment:** compatibility with QURBATA reading/tajwid competence at the proposed level.
- **C — Continuity:** value for transition into systematic 30-juz memorization.

Working representation:

`Corpus Priority = f(F, E, M, T, C)`

Coefficients and thresholds remain open for simulation; the five dimensions themselves are FROZEN as the baseline selection framework.

## 7. Non-Negotiable Design Rules

1. Tahfidz remains integrated with QURBATA Tartil Jilid 1–8.
2. Every applicable QURBATA page will display a clear new-memorization target.
3. Target size is adaptive; `1 ayah = 1 page` is rejected as a universal rule.
4. Long ayat may be divided at pedagogically and semantically acceptable boundaries.
5. Several very short ayat may share one unit when appropriate.
6. New memorization and murojaah remain separate system layers.
7. Corpus quantity may not destroy retention quality.
8. Tartil competence must influence memorization load.
9. The eight-volume target is not capped at Juz 30.
10. QURBATA Tahfidz must provide a coherent bridge into the later 30-juz program.

## 8. Next Required Work

The next analytical artifact is **QURBATA TAHFIDZ CORPUS MASTER** containing:

Inventory and score Al-Fatihah and other fundamental worship corpus.

### Layer 2 — CORE BERURUT

### Layer 3 — CORE PILIHAN
Score candidate surahs/passages using F/E/M/T/C.

### Capacity simulation
Model a progressive word/phrase load across approximately 320 units, with retention reserve, and calculate how much corpus actually fits.

Only after that simulation may the system freeze:
- terminal corpus;
- number/equivalent of juz;
- allocation by jilid;
- allocation by page.
