# RIQA MARHALAH — SDM STUDENT-BOOK LANGUAGE & CONTENT STANDARD v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED BASELINE
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Applies to:** all participant-facing Marhalah books M01–M21

## Core identity
Buku Marhalah adalah **buku pelajaran versi SDM**. Pembaca adalah peserta MARHALAH yang sedang belajar dan memperdalam ilmu agar secara bertahap layak menjadi SDM RIQA/QURBATA. Buku ini bukan panduan mengajar, SOP, manual aplikasi, atau dokumentasi sistem.

## Participant-facing composition
As a default:
- 70–80%: materi, syarah, pemahaman, contoh, pendalaman ilmu;
- 15–20%: latihan/LKS/evaluasi;
- a small contextual portion: bekal penerapan sebagai SDM, including teaching implications when naturally relevant.

## Language
Use simple, mature, pesantren-friendly Indonesian. Explain technical religious/linguistic terms when first needed. Avoid software/developer language in participant pages.

Do NOT expose participant-facing labels such as:
canonical, whitelist, unlock, evidence, mastery gate, randomized item, transfer item, source-check, crosswalk, competency ID, repository code.

These may remain in governance/source metadata but not in normal book prose.

## Competency ladder
Prerequisite/mastery logic remains fully active in curriculum and RIQA OS, but the book expresses it naturally:
**Pelajari → Pahami → Latih → Dalami → Uji → Tuntas → Lanjut.**
Use simple phrases such as “Pastikan bagian sebelumnya sudah dikuasai sebelum melanjutkan.”

## RIQA OS
RIQA OS appears only when useful to learning:
- Dengarkan contoh bacaan
- Rekam latihan
- Kirim tugas
- Lihat hasil
Do not explain application architecture on ordinary learning pages.

## Teacher readiness
Teacher readiness is an outcome of deep learning, not the dominant voice of every page. Sections such as **Bekal Mengajar** may appear briefly after substantial learning when appropriate.

## Tartil example
For ب ت ث, dominant content is: bentuk/bunyi → makhraj → sifat → comparison → examples → common reading errors → why they occur → listening/discrimination → reading practice → analysis → deeper practice → evaluation. Teaching notes are secondary.

## Domain rule
The same principle applies differently by domain:
- Tartil: deepen Qur'anic reading science and practice.
- Bahasa Arab: deepen meaning, use, skills, structure, and Bi'ah according to stage.
- Tahfidz: deepen memorization, retention, murojaah, tasmi', and understanding of the memorization process.
- Akhlak: deepen understanding, internalization, cases, conduct, reflection, and character formation.

## Visual identity
Retain Kitab Kompetensi Pesantren Modern + restrained mushaf-inspired decoration + Uthmani Arabic typography in final production.
