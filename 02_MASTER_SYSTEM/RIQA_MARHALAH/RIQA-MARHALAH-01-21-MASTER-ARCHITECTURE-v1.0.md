## 1. Purpose
Marhalah RIQA 1–21 is the integrated competency ladder for RIQA human-resource development. Each Marhalah represents an integrated competency profile, not a collection of unrelated subjects.

## 2. Four Integrated Domains
Every Marhalah contains:
1. **TARTIL AL-QUR'AN** — reading competency, accuracy, fluency, tajwid, correction, teaching, assessment, and quality supervision according to level.
2. **BAHASA ARAB** — integrated through three connected strands:
   - communicative Arabic / vocabulary / language skills;
   - language theory including nahwu–sharaf, introduced only when developmentally appropriate;
   - Bi'ah 'Arabiyah: functional Arabic use in real RIQA/QURBATA learning environments.
3. **TAHFIDZ AL-QUR'AN** — new memorization, cumulative murojaah, tasmi', quality, mentoring, teaching, and system supervision according to level.
4. **AKHLAK RIQA** — personal adab, learning adab, Qur'anic adab, social ethics, teacher responsibility, and leadership ethics. QURBATA NIDHOM is an integrated source/crosswalk within this domain, not the entirety of Akhlak RIQA.

## 3. Integration Rules
### 3.1 Horizontal integration
Within each Marhalah, Tartil ↔ Bahasa Arab ↔ Tahfidz ↔ Akhlak RIQA should reinforce one another through shared contexts and authentic learning activities whenever naturally relevant.

### 3.2 Vertical integration
Competencies are cumulative. M(n) contains new competency plus maintenance/evidence of prerequisite competencies from prior Marhalah. No silent regression or disconnected jumps.

### 3.3 RIQA ↔ QURBATA integration
Existing QURBATA canonical/frozen competencies are crosswalked into RIQA Marhalah. They are not copied into a second competing corpus. Source IDs and canonical identities must be preserved.

### 3.4 Non-forced integration
Integration must be pedagogically authentic. A nahwu competency, for example, must not be forced onto a Tartil page if there is no natural relationship; it may remain connected through Marhalah prerequisites, activities, or assessment.

## 4. Competency Relationship Contract
Each competency should be traceable as:
**competency → prerequisites → material/source → integrated activity → related competencies → evidence/assessment → mastery → next-Marhalah progression**.

A participant is considered to have mastered a Marhalah only when the required profile across the four domains has been satisfied according to its assessment rules.

## 7. Domain Progression Principle
Higher Marhalah do not stop learning Tartil, Arabic, Tahfidz, or Akhlak. The performance level changes:
- personal mastery → assistance → teaching → training → supervision/quality assurance.
- Bi'ah 'Arabiyah progresses from responding/using Arabic personally, to sustaining the environment, to helping others use it, to training teachers and supervising implementation.
- Tahfidz progresses from memorizing/retaining, to mentoring, teaching, training mentors, and supervising quality.
- Akhlak progresses from personal adab to professional teaching ethics and leadership/quality stewardship.
