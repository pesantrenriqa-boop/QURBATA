# RIQA MARHALAH — DOMAIN-SPECIFIC DEPTH & INTEGRATION STANDARD v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED BASELINE
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Applies to:** M01–M21, Books 1–5
**Purpose:** define different professional-learning engines for each domain while preserving one RIQA/QURBATA SDM pathway.

## 1. Shared Professional Progression
All domains contribute to the same SDM progression:
**Mampu → Paham → Terapkan → Analisis → Ajarkan → Nilai → Bina → Jaga Mutu.**
Depth and authority increase according to Marhalah. No participant may skip prerequisites.

## 2. Tartil — Phonetic, Practical, Diagnostic, Pedagogical
Professional depth may include:
- accurate personal reading;
- makhraj and relevant sifat al-huruf;
- tajwid at the appropriate competency;
- auditory/visual discrimination;
- common error patterns and causes;
- tashih sequence and correction cues;
- talqin/talaqqi technique;
- QURBATA teaching sequence and learner whitelist;
- transfer to unseen allowed items;
- microteaching;
- performance assessment, remediation, assessor calibration and quality supervision at higher Marhalah.
Teacher principle: **SDM must know more than the learner-facing QURBATA page, but must not teach material before the learner's QURBATA ladder allows it.**

## 3. Bahasa Arab — Meaning, Use, Structure, Bi'ah, Pedagogy
Professional depth follows language-learning characteristics:
- meaning and functional use;
- vocabulary in context;
- listening/speaking/reading/writing according to stage;
- communicative classroom Arabic;
- structures and patterns;
- nahwu-sharaf only when prerequisite readiness exists;
- Qur'anic Arabic analysis where relevant;
- error analysis and correction;
- ability to explain language;
- language-teaching pedagogy;
- design and maintenance of Bi'ah 'Arabiyah;
- training teachers and supervising language-environment quality at higher Marhalah.
Do not copy Tartil's makhraj/sifat chapter structure into Arabic lessons.

## 4. Tahfidz — Retention, Quality, Retrieval, Halaqah Management
Professional depth may include:
- correct memorization input/talqin;
- ziyadah according to canonical allocation;
- tikrar and cumulative murojaah;
- retrieval and tasmi';
- rabth/continuity as appropriate;
- error detection and correction;
- diagnosis of weak retention;
- individualized remediation;
- listening to/assessing a learner;
- target planning and halaqah management;
- training musyrif/teachers;
- quality assurance of Tahfidz programs at higher Marhalah.
Canonical Tahfidz corpus remains source-of-truth.

## 5. Akhlak RIQA / NIDHOM — Understanding, Internalization, Conduct, Formation
Professional depth follows character formation:
- fahm of approved source/value;
- concrete meaning;
- personal internalization;
- case/situation analysis;
- ethical judgment;
- habitual conduct;
- reflection and self-evaluation;
- role-modeling;
- pedagogical correction with adab;
- teacher ethics and amanah;
- mentoring others;
- institutional culture and value-quality supervision at higher Marhalah.
Do not reduce Akhlak to memorizing a hadith and answering recall questions. Candidate/unapproved hadith must not be promoted to final learner material.

## 6. Integration Contract
Domains retain their own learning engines but meet in authentic RIQA/QURBATA practice. Example microteaching may simultaneously require:
- correct Tartil;
- appropriate Arabic classroom language/Bi'ah;
- correct Tahfidz handling when relevant;
- ethical/adab-based correction.

Integration is competency-based, not cosmetic. Cross-domain prerequisites must be explicit where necessary.

## 7. Role Progression
- M01–M04 Anggota: personal mastery + foundational professional awareness.
- M05–M08 Praktisi: stable practice + basic diagnosis/application.
- M09–M12 Asisten Guru: assist, observe, bounded correction.
- M13–M16 Guru: explain, teach, assess, remediate, run relevant RIQA/QURBATA learning.
- M17–M19 Trainer: train teachers, standardize pedagogy and assessment.
- M20–M21 Supervisor: audit quality, calibrate trainers/assessors, safeguard standards and program implementation.

## 8. Book Authoring Rule
Do not force one identical chapter template across all domains. Each competency chapter must use the domain engine above while still containing:
**knowledge/depth + practice + analysis + professional application + assessment/evidence + mastery gate** as appropriate.

## 9. Primary Goal
Every page must contribute to forming SDM who can progressively **run, teach, assess, develop within authority, and safeguard the quality of RIQA & QURBATA**, rather than merely finish exercises.
