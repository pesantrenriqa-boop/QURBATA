# RIQA MARHALAH — STRICT SCOPE & VERTICAL DEPTH RULE v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED CORRECTION / PRODUCTION GATE
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Applies immediately to:** Book 1 M01–M04 and all later Marhalah books.

## Core rule
**Tangga menentukan APA yang boleh dipelajari. Versi SDM menentukan SEBERAPA DALAM kompetensi itu dipahami.**

A Marhalah book may deepen the active competency vertically, but MUST NOT expand horizontally into a competency that has not yet arrived in the approved ladder.

## M01 Tartil correction
If M01 target is ب ت ث and accurate basic pronunciation, SDM depth may include:
- accurate recognition;
- correct short sound according to talaqqi;
- simple, directly useful explanation of lip/tongue position needed to pronounce the active letters;
- common errors specifically on those active letters;
- self-correction and practice within the allowed material.

M01 MUST NOT automatically introduce a Tajwid/sifat curriculum such as:
jahr, hams, syiddah, rikhwah, istifal, infitah, qalqalah,
unless the approved competency ladder explicitly places those concepts in M01.

Professional depth is not permission to teach future competencies early.

## Same rule across domains
### Bahasa Arab
Deepen only the active vocabulary/expression/skill. Do not introduce nahwu, i'rab, tashrif, or later language theory before its approved stage.

### Tahfidz
Deepen only the active memorization competency and immediate method needed to master it. Do not import advanced Tahfidz theory merely because the learner is future SDM.

### Akhlak
Deepen the active adab/akhlak target through understanding, reflection, and cases. Do not import later professional/leadership ethics before its stage.

## Authoring test
Before any paragraph is admitted:
1. What active competency does this paragraph deepen?
2. Is the concept already inside the current Marhalah boundary?
3. If removed, can the current competency still be mastered deeply?
4. Is it actually a future competency disguised as “teacher depth”?

If #2 fails or #4 is yes, move it to the later Marhalah.

## Production consequence
The current Book 1 production draft must be curriculum-audited before final layout. Remove premature Tajwid terminology from M01 and any comparable curriculum creep in M02–M04. Do not solve this by reducing depth; replace horizontal expansion with deeper explanation, observation, practice, error analysis, and reflection on the competency actually active.
