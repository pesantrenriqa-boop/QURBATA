# RIQA MARHALAH — BALANCED DOMAIN WEIGHT & TAHFIDZ DEPTH ADDENDUM v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED ADDITIVE STANDARD
**Date:** 8 September 2026

## 1. Balanced Weight
A single early Tartil family such as ب ت ث must not consume a disproportionate share of a Marhalah book. Professional depth remains, but presentation is concise and proportional so that Bahasa Arab, Tahfidz, and Akhlak receive substantial learning space.

Depth is not measured by page count. Combine related Tartil material efficiently:
- bentuk + bunyi;
- makhraj/sifat in concise comparison;
- common errors + self-correction;
- reading practice + understanding;
- short Bekal Mengajar at the end.

## 2. Tahfidz Is a Full Learning Domain
Tahfidz must appear as substantive study, not a placeholder or merely “tasmi' target”. At appropriate Marhalah depth, participants study:
- how memorization is formed;
- correct initial input/talqin;
- repetition/tikrar;
- ziyadah;
- cumulative murojaah;
- retrieval/tasmi';
- retention over time;
- common causes that weaken memorization;
- confusion in similar verses (mutasyabihat) according to stage;
- connecting verse sequences;
- concentration, rhythm/routine, and realistic load;
- diagnosing weak memorization;
- repair/remedial strategies;
- only near the end: implications for teaching/listening to QURBATA/RIQA participants.

Do not invent a new Qur'anic memorization corpus; target verses remain governed by the approved Tahfidz track.

## 3. Tahfidz Learning Voice
The participant is learning Tahfidz science and practice for themselves first. Use simple language:
**Pahami hafalan → bentuk hafalan → ulangi → jaga → panggil kembali tanpa bantuan → temukan titik lemah → perbaiki → uji.**
Teaching guidance is secondary and placed near the end.

## 4. Common Weakening Factors
Book discussion may cover, with stage-appropriate nuance:
- adding new memorization before old memorization is stable;
- repetition that is too little or inconsistent;
- memorizing from an inaccurate reading;
- dependence on looking at text/prompts;
- neglecting cumulative murojaah;
- excessive daily load;
- weak attention to verse order;
- confusion between similar passages;
- only practicing in one fixed sequence/context;
- long gaps without retrieval.

## 5. Similar Verses
Mutasyabihat are treated as a memory challenge and Qur'anic-text awareness skill, not as random trivia. Teach participants to notice distinguishing words/phrases, context and sequence at the level appropriate to their corpus. Exact examples must come from the approved memorization corpus.

## 6. Book Balance
For an integrated M01 prototype, no single domain should visually dominate simply because its professional explanation is easier to expand. Allocate meaningful study pages to all four domains, while allowing natural variation according to competency load.
