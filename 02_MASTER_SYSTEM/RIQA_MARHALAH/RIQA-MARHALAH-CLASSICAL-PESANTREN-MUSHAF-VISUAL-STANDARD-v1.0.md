# RIQA MARHALAH — CLASSICAL PESANTREN & MUSHAF VISUAL STANDARD v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED ADDITIVE STANDARD
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Important:** This standard changes presentation/visual-language only. It does NOT replace or alter the approved M01–M21 architecture, five-book SDM stages, four integrated domains, prerequisite/mastery gates, or RIQA↔QURBATA crosswalk rules.

## Identity
The RIQA Marhalah series should feel like a **Kitab Kompetensi Pesantren Modern**:
**turats/pesantren learning culture + contemporary mushaf aesthetics + modern textbook/LKS + RIQA OS mastery technology.**

Target readers are prospective/developing RIQA SDM: Anggota, Praktisi, Asisten Guru, Guru, Trainer, Supervisor. The tone is mature even when early competencies are elementary.

## Arabic Use
Arabic is substantive, not decorative. Short Arabic headings, maxims, instructions, or paragraphs may be used when they are within the learner's competency/whitelist and pedagogically useful. Arabic density may increase gradually across Marhalah as Arabic competence rises.

Do not insert difficult Arabic into early Marhalah merely to create a classical appearance.

All Arabic typography in final production must use the approved **Uthmani font standard** consistently, including headings, explanatory Arabic paragraphs, instructions, exercises, Tahfidz text, and quotations.

## Mushaf-inspired Layout
Use a contemporary, restrained interpretation of mushaf aesthetics:
- refined page frame/border;
- subtle corner ornaments;
- geometric/floral Islamic separators;
- medallion/cartouche for Marhalah/competency numbers;
- classical chapter-heading cues;
- balanced margins and strong central text area;
- generous writable LKS areas;
- high readability and no decorative overload.

Do not imitate a sacred Qur'an page so literally that ordinary instructional text could be confused with Qur'anic text.

## Classical Learning Language
Where suitable, learning sections may use pesantren-flavored labels alongside clear Indonesian, e.g.:
- مَقْصُودُ التَّعَلُّمِ — tujuan;
- الشَّرْحُ — penjelasan;
- مِثَالٌ — contoh;
- التَّمْرِينُ — latihan;
- التَّطْبِيقُ — penerapan;
- التَّقْوِيمُ — evaluasi;
while keeping comprehension appropriate to the current Marhalah.

## Modern Layer
RIQA OS remains subtle and functional: QR/code, audio, recording, evidence, verification, mastery/unlock. Technology must not overpower the kitab/mushaf identity.

## Progression
As M01→M21 rises, content may gradually become more scholarly/professional: perform → understand → analyze → teach → correct → train → assess → supervise, while all prerequisite/mastery rules remain unchanged.
