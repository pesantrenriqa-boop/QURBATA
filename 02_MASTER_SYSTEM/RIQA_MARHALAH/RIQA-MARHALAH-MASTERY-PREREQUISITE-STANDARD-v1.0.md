# RIQA MARHALAH — MASTERY & PREREQUISITE STANDARD v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED BASELINE
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Applies to:** RIQA Marhalah M01–M21

## Core Decision
Buku Marhalah RIQA adalah **buku pelajaran + LKS/pendalaman + competency mastery book** untuk peserta MARHALAH-SDM RIQA & QURBATA. Buku ini bukan panduan penggunaan dan bukan buku latihan contoh seperti buku Tartil QURBATA.

Peserta menempuh kompetensi dari awal sampai akhir sesuai tangga resmi. **Tidak boleh melanjutkan ke anak tangga berikutnya sebelum prerequisite/tangga sebelumnya tuntas.**

## Distinction from QURBATA
- QURBATA supplies canonical learning corpus, examples, methods, and domain competency sources.
- Marhalah organizes SDM mastery progression, explanation, deep understanding, varied exercises, transfer, evidence, assessment, verification, and progression gates.
- Marhalah must teach the competency, not merely display practice examples.

## Mandatory Competency Learning Cycle
**PAHAMI → CONTOH → LATIH TERBIMBING → LATIH MANDIRI → TRANSFER → UJI KOMPETENSI → EVIDENCE/VERIFIKASI → TUNTAS → BUKA TANGGA BERIKUTNYA**

If not mastered:
**BELUM TUNTAS → diagnosis bagian lemah → remedial/pengulangan → uji ulang.**

## Internal Competency Ladder
Each Marhalah may contain multiple ordered competency nodes per domain, e.g. M01-T01 → M01-T02 → M01-T03. Printed adjacency does not grant permission to skip prerequisites.

Every competency record must define:
- competency ID;
- observable mastery statement;
- prerequisite competency IDs;
- source/crosswalk;
- explanation/concept;
- guided examples;
- guided practice;
- independent practice;
- transfer/new-item task;
- assessment/evidence;
- mastery rule/threshold from approved source;
- remediation route;
- next unlocked competency.

## Four-Domain Network
Tartil, Bahasa Arab, Tahfidz, and Akhlak RIQA are integrated through authentic learning activities and explicit prerequisite relationships where appropriate. They do not have to advance at identical speed.

A competency in one domain may require mastery from another domain when pedagogically necessary. Cross-domain prerequisites must be explicit, not assumed.

## Completion Rule
Finishing pages is not completion. A participant completes a competency/Marhalah only after required mastery evidence is verified.

**MARHALAH RIQA bukan buku yang harus diselesaikan halamannya; MARHALAH RIQA adalah tangga kompetensi yang harus dituntaskan anak tangganya.**

## Print + RIQA OS
The book should subtly show current position and locked/available progression without becoming a user manual. RIQA OS carries digital evidence, recordings, assessment, verification, mastery state, and unlock logic.

Suggested subtle state vocabulary:
TANGGA SAYA · SEDANG DIPELAJARI · SIAP DIUJI · TERVERIFIKASI · BELUM TUNTAS · LANJUT

## Governance
No competency may be marked final/mastered merely because content exists in QURBATA. Approved thresholds and verification rules govern progression. Unknown thresholds must remain TBD until approved.
