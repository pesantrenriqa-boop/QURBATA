# RIQA MARHALAH — TEACHER READINESS & PEDAGOGICAL DEPTH STANDARD v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED ADDITIVE STANDARD
**Date:** 8 September 2026
**Applies to:** M01–M21 and all five RIQA Marhalah books
**Does not replace:** M01–M21 architecture, SDM stages, four-domain integration, mastery/prerequisite rules, QURBATA canonical crosswalk, or RIQA OS evidence model.

## Core Purpose
Buku Marhalah is professional formation material for prospective/developing RIQA & QURBATA SDM. It must prepare participants not only to perform a competency personally, but progressively to **understand it, diagnose errors, correct it, explain it, teach it, assess it, train others in it, and supervise its quality** according to the participant's Marhalah.

Therefore, even an elementary QURBATA corpus item may receive deeper professional treatment in Marhalah.

## Critical Distinction
**QURBATA learner material:** corpus/examples/practice appropriate to the learner's reading stage.
**MARHALAH SDM material:** professional knowledge behind that corpus and how to teach it correctly.

Example: QURBATA page may practice بَ تَ ثَ. Marhalah SDM must additionally study, at the appropriate professional depth:
- identity and visual discrimination of ب ت ث;
- correct articulation/makhraj;
- relevant sifat al-huruf;
- sound quality and common distortions;
- common learner errors;
- likely causes of error;
- correction cues and teacher language;
- demonstration/talqin/talaqqi technique;
- sequencing of practice;
- what material must NOT yet be introduced to a QURBATA learner;
- observation/assessment indicators;
- remedial decisions;
- relationship to later competencies.

## Two Ladders Must Be Distinguished
1. **QURBATA learner ladder:** what the QURBATA student is allowed to learn at that point.
2. **SDM professional ladder:** what the Marhalah participant must understand in order to teach, correct, assess, or supervise that QURBATA point.

Professional explanation may therefore be deeper than the learner-facing QURBATA page, but must never alter the QURBATA learner sequence.

## Standard Professional Competency Chapter
For relevant competencies, include:
1. **Matan/Inti Kompetensi** — concise core statement.
2. **Syarah/Pembahasan** — concept and professional explanation.
3. **Dasar Praktik** — what correct performance looks/sounds like.
4. **Makharij & Sifat** — when phonetic/Qur'anic reading competence requires it.
5. **Kesalahan yang Sering Terjadi** — observable error patterns.
6. **Mengapa Kesalahan Terjadi** — likely technical/pedagogical causes.
7. **Cara Mentashih** — safe, precise correction sequence.
8. **Cara Mengajarkan dalam QURBATA** — teacher moves, talqin/talaqqi, sequencing, learner whitelist.
9. **Batas Materi** — what must not be introduced prematurely to the QURBATA learner.
10. **Latihan Analisis SDM** — diagnose examples/cases, not merely repeat them.
11. **Simulasi Mengajar** — microteaching where stage-appropriate.
12. **Uji Kompetensi SDM** — perform + explain + diagnose/correct as required by Marhalah.
13. **Evidence & Verification**.
14. **Remedial / Unlock next prerequisite**.

## Progression of Professional Responsibility
The same knowledge is revisited at higher levels with greater responsibility:
- M01–M04 Anggota: correct personal performance + foundational awareness of why/correction.
- M05–M08 Praktisi: stable performance + identify common errors + guided correction.
- M09–M12 Asisten Guru: assist teaching, observe, diagnose, give bounded correction.
- M13–M16 Guru: explain, teach independently, sequence learning, assess and remediate.
- M17–M19 Trainer: train teachers, analyze teaching errors, standardize correction.
- M20–M21 Supervisor: audit quality, calibrate assessors/trainers, safeguard standards and system implementation.

## Arabic / Pesantren Layer
Classical Arabic headings or short paragraphs may deepen professional formation where appropriate. All final Arabic typography follows the approved Uthmani standard. Arabic must remain pedagogically controlled and must not be inserted merely as decoration.

## RIQA Programs
Marhalah content should progressively prepare SDM to operate RIQA programs relevant to their role, including use of QURBATA instructional standards, assessment/evidence, RIQA OS workflow, learner support/remediation, and quality assurance. Operational material must appear when it becomes a real competency, not as generic handbook exposition.

## Governance
Do not invent makhraj/sifat rules, correction standards, thresholds, or teacher procedures when canonical RIQA/QURBATA sources exist. Map to approved sources; unresolved items remain working/TBD pending validation.
