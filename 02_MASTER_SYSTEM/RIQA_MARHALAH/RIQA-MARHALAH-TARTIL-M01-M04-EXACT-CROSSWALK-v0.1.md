## Global Book-1 Tartil boundary
Allowed: 29 letter identities; fathah, kasrah, dhammah according to page order; separated 2–3 element sequences; visual/fonetic discrimination; directly relevant SDM pronunciation depth; cumulative review, transfer and evaluation.
Not allowed: connected forms, tanwin, mad, sukun, tasydid, later tajwid rules, or future learner-facing material.

## SDM-depth rule
For each active letter family, MARHALAH may deepen exact articulation/makhraj, directly relevant sifat, common errors, listening discrimination, guided production, independent production and diagnostic observation. This depth does not activate future reading rules.
