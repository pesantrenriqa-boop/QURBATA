# QC-000 — QURBATA Constitution

Version: 0.1.0 (Founding Draft)

## Chapter 1 — Identity

### 1.1 Official Name
**QURBATA**

Working title: Integrated Qur'anic Education and Knowledge Framework.

### 1.2 Nature
QURBATA is an academic framework integrating research, curriculum, assessment, teacher development, documentation, and educational technology into a single evidence-informed ecosystem.

## Chapter 2 — Purpose
1. Develop research-based Qur'anic education standards.
2. Build documented curricula and learning resources.
3. Support continuous improvement through evidence.

## Chapter 3 — Vision
To become an integrated academic framework for Qur'anic education based on research, scholarly standards, and sustainable innovation.

## Chapter 4 — Mission
- Develop standards.
- Conduct research.
- Produce high-quality educational resources.
- Support teacher development.
- Integrate technology.

## Chapter 5 — Core Values
Tawhid, Amanah, Itqan, Academic Integrity, Evidence-Based Development, Continuous Improvement.

## Chapter 6 — Scope
This constitution governs the QURBATA ecosystem, including governance, standards, research, curriculum, books, assessment, technology, and quality.

## Chapter 7 — Definitions
Initial definitions for Constitution, Standard, Framework, Guideline, Specification, and Evidence.
