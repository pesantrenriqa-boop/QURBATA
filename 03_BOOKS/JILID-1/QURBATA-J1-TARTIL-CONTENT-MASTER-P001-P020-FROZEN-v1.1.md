**Scope:** Tartil practice material only. Integration domains remain governed by Integration Register v1.3.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Normal page target: 8 × 3 = 24 reading groups.
- A 3-letter group must be a real meaningful Arabic word with the printed harakat.
- Priority: Quran-confirmed lemma → verified fusha lemma.
- Never introduce a letter, harakat, sukun, shaddah, tanwin, mad, connection feature, or other reading feature before its curriculum page.
- If the legal word inventory is insufficient, repeat valid words deliberately or use legal 2-letter drills. Never invent a pseudo-word.
- Detached ه must render as the required two-hole form; this is a renderer gate, not a Unicode substitution.
- P019/P020 are curated; random/cyclic 3-letter generation is prohibited.
