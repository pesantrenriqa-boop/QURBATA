## Dhammah semantic-bank strategy
Unlike Fathah/Kasrah, many valid Form-I perfect verbs have middle-radical dhammah (فَعُلَ). Quranic Corpus explicitly confirms examples such as **كَبُرَ** (“to be great”) and **حَسُنَ** (“to be good”). These are useful only from the page where the relevant dhammah-bearing letter is legal. Corpus existence never overrides QURBATA page legality.
