## 5. First Integrated Prototype — M01.01
**Working title:** Dengarkan, Ikuti, dan Baca dengan Adab

### Target
Learner recognizes/reads the first QJ1-P001 controlled Tartil family; responds to اِسْتَمِعْ, كَرِّرْ, اِقْرَأْ; begins the canonical M01 Tahfidz task; and demonstrates attentive listening, serious imitation, and calm correction.

### Pemantik
Bagaimana cara belajar Al-Qur'an dengan benar? Use visual choices of appropriate/inappropriate learning conduct.

### Bahasa Arab in action
Teacher says اِسْتَمِعْ → learner listens.
Teacher says كَرِّرْ → learner repeats.
Teacher says اِقْرَأْ → learner reads.
No grammar explanation.

### Tartil core
Use QJ1-P001 controlled forms only: بَ، تَ، ثَ. No connected forms, mad, tanwin, sukun, tasydid, or premature elements. Progress visual distinction → sound → 2-unit sequences → 3-unit sequences.

### Tahfidz connection
Use exact canonical M01 Tahfidz allocation. If participant literacy is not appropriate, use teacher-led oral/tasmi' mode; do not invent new memorization.

### Akhlak mission
Dengarkan dengan baik, tirukan dengan sungguh-sungguh, dan perbaiki kesalahan dengan tenang. This is observable conduct; do not attach unapproved hadith text.

### Pendalaman
Visual discrimination; sound recognition; Arabic instruction-action matching; simple understanding question; respectful-correction scenario.

### Evaluation
Tunjuk cepat; baca acak; dengar & lakukan; canonical Tahfidz/tasmi'; situasi akhlak; reflection; assessor practical checklist. Written score alone cannot pass M01.
