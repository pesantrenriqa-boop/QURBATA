## Competency rise from Jilid 3
Jilid 3 focused on controlling self in immediate social situations. Jilid 4 raises the ladder to **amanah, responsibility, justice, and social courage**: the learner is expected not only to choose a good response but to carry obligations, protect rights, repair consequences, and act correctly under social pressure.

- Use story, case, choice, explanation, role-play, mission, reflection, and cumulative evaluation to prevent monotonous repetition.
