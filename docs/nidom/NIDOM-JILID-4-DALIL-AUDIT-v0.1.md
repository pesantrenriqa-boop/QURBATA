# QURBATA NIDOM — JILID 4 DALIL AUDIT v0.1

Status: PRE-RENDER CONTENT GATE
Scope: J4 P001–P040

## Audit rule
Arabic matn/ayat, translation, source label, and vocabulary must be verified before being admitted into the renderer. Pages designed as story/case/practice/reflection/evaluation are not forced to contain a new hadith.

## A. Amanah / promise anchors
### A1 — Qur'an 4:58
Arabic anchor: `إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُكُمْ أَنْ تُؤَدُّوا الْأَمَانَاتِ إِلَىٰ أَهْلِهَا`
Use: P001/P002 conceptual anchor for returning trusts to those entitled to them.
Source: Qur'an, al-Nisa' 4:58.
Status: VERIFIED.

### A2 — Signs of hypocrisy (trust/promise clauses)
Arabic full anchor: `آيَةُ الْمُنَافِقِ ثَلَاثٌ إِذَا حَدَّثَ كَذَبَ، وَإِذَا وَعَدَ أَخْلَفَ، وَإِذَا اؤْتُمِنَ خَانَ`
Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 33 (also repeated in Bukhari 2749/6095).
Use: P003 may emphasize `وَإِذَا وَعَدَ أَخْلَفَ`; an amanah page may emphasize `وَإِذَا اؤْتُمِنَ خَانَ`.
Label rule: if only a clause is printed, explicitly label it as a **potongan dari hadits tanda-tanda kemunafikan**, not as an independent hadith.
Pedagogy rule: do not label a child “munafik”; evaluate the behavior (breaking promise/betraying trust), not the child's identity.
Status: VERIFIED.

## B. Justice / rights anchors
### B1 — Qur'an 4:58 justice clause
Arabic anchor: `وَإِذَا حَكَمْتُمْ بَيْنَ النَّاسِ أَنْ تَحْكُمُوا بِالْعَدْلِ`
Source: Qur'an, al-Nisa' 4:58.
Use: P011 as primary justice anchor; P012–P019 apply it through cases rather than repeating the verse on every page.
Status: VERIFIED.

## C. Moral courage / truth anchors
### C1 — Truth leads to righteousness
Arabic anchor: `إِنَّ الصِّدْقَ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْبِرِّ وَإِنَّ الْبِرَّ يَهْدِي إِلَى الْجَنَّةِ`
Source: Sahih Muslim 2607a; parallel authenticated narrations in the same chapter.
Use: P021/P022. Later pages vary into peer-pressure case, defending without violence, protective reporting, guarding others' dignity, admission of one's own mistake, and role-play.
Status: VERIFIED.

### C2 — Extended truth/lie contrast
If a longer matn is pedagogically needed, use the verified Muslim 2607c wording beginning `عَلَيْكُمْ بِالصِّدْقِ...` and including the warning against lying. Do not overload beginner-facing pages with the full long matn unless layout and comprehension gates justify it.
Status: VERIFIED, OPTIONAL LONG FORM.

## D. Responsibility / repair
P031–P035 should primarily use cases and concrete repair behavior. Do not invent a hadith merely to fill the page. Reuse an already audited anchor only when it directly supports the task and label it transparently as murojaah dalil.

## E. Evaluation pages
P036–P040: no requirement for new Arabic evidence. They test transfer of amanah, justice, moral courage, and repair into decisions and habits.
