## Competency rise from Jilid 4
Jilid 4 focused on amanah, justice, moral courage, and repairing consequences. Jilid 5 raises the ladder to **adab lisan, empathy, cooperation, and disciplined self-management**. Learners are expected to regulate speech, read social situations, work with others fairly, and maintain good habits without continuous supervision.

## Locked construction rules inherited from J4
1. Official QURBATA logo must be stamped into the final PDF, not only displayed through CSS/HTML.
2. Arabic must use Uthman Taha and pass the Arabic-font gate.
3. Vocabulary meaning cells remain blank for learner completion.
4. Reasoning prompts are adaptive to available page space: up to 5, fewer when hadith/dalil + vocabulary make the page dense.
5. Do not shrink the established large-type hierarchy merely to fit more questions.
6. One theme may repeat for several pages, but the activity model must vary: hadith, story, case, role-play, comparison, mission, reflection, cumulative evaluation.
7. Do not place a new hadith on every page.
8. Every hadith/Qur'an anchor must be audited before content freeze.
9. Every page must pass OVERFLOW=0.
10. Signature area and footer remain unobstructed.
